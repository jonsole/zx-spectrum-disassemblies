6460: Room sizes
Three entries: the half-size in U, the half-size in V (about the room's centre at 128), and the floor's height. Bits 6-7 of a room record's third byte pick one, and the builder (BUILD_ROOM) copies it to ROOM_HALF_U.
ROOM_SIZES 6460 DEFB $40,$40,$40 Size 0: half-sizes 64 in U and 64 in V; floor at 64
6463 DEFB $20,$40,$40 Size 1: half-sizes 32 in U and 64 in V; floor at 64
6466 DEFB $40,$20,$40 Size 2: half-sizes 64 in U and 32 in V; floor at 64
