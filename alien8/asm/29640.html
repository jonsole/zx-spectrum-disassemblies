73C8: Object template table
A word per object template, the group header's bits 3-7 doubled as the index (BUILD_ROOM), in two pages: the builder keeps the page it is reading in TEMPLATES_AT, and template 31 moves it on by 64 bytes, to the second, which has seven. Templates 0 and 31 of each page are not templates, so their words are zero.
A template is a list of five-byte pieces -- graphic, the half-sizes in U and V, the height, flags -- each copied to +0 and +4 to +7 of an object record at the group's position, for as long as the byte after a piece is not zero.
