A971: The robot being oiled, in the ending (graphic 92)
Reached through UPDATES: the scene after the twenty-fourth chamber, set up by GAME_ENDED in pixels (the scene is not projected). The robot waits 64 turns, is lowered three pixels a turn into the can of oil (graphic 85) until it is below 40, stays there while its count runs on to 128, then rises three pixels a turn, now bright white, to 128, with a sound pitched by its height as it moves. Then a tune, and back to the menu (AFTER_GAME).
+11 counts: 0 to 63 waiting; 64 going down and then counting at the bottom; 128 and over going up. +10 is its colour (COLOUR_SCENE_OBJECT, in SCENE_SPARKS).
