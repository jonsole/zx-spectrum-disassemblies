AD13: A thing that drops from the ceiling (graphic 73)
Reached through UPDATES. Knight Lore's spiked ball: deadly both ways, it hangs until a turn when the random number is under 16 (one in sixteen), no other is falling (DROPPING), and the drop latch (DROP_LATCH) is clear -- which in an even-numbered room it is not until something has been picked up. Then it falls, with a sound pitched by its height, lands with a thud (THUD_ON_LANDING), and lets the next one go.
Bit 2 of +D: falling. Once on the floor it can be let go again, but it has nowhere to fall and makes no second thud. Rooms $0C, $1C, $58 and $84 have them (object template 20).
CEILING_DROP AD13 CALL MAKE_DEADLY deadly both ways
AD16 CALL DRAW_AT_L12_D8 draw 12 left and 8 down
AD19 LD A,(DROP_LATCH) latched: nothing drops
AD1C AND A
AD1D RET NZ
AD1E BIT 2,(IX+$0D) falling
AD22 JR NZ,CEILING_DROP_0
AD24 LD HL,DROPPING another is falling
AD27 LD A,(HL)
AD28 AND A
AD29 RET NZ
AD2A LD A,(RANDOM) one turn in sixteen
AD2D CP $10
AD2F RET NC
AD30 SET 2,(IX+$0D) let go
AD34 LD (HL),$01
AD36 RET
CEILING_DROP_0 AD37 CALL DEC_DZ_AND_UPDATE_UVZ fall
AD3A CALL THUD_ON_LANDING a thud if it has just landed
AD3D BIT 2,(IX+$0C) landed
AD41 JR NZ,CEILING_DROP_2
AD43 CALL BEEP_BY_Z a sound pitched by its height
CEILING_DROP_1 AD46 JP SET_WIPE_AND_DRAW_FLAGS redraw
CEILING_DROP_2 AD49 RES 2,(IX+$0D) landed: another may fall
AD4D LD HL,DROPPING
AD50 LD (HL),$00
AD52 JR CEILING_DROP_1
