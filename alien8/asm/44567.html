AE17: The picture of the valve a socket wants (graphics 104 to 107)
Reached through UPDATES. A chamber's socket (SOCKET) is built with a record after it for its sparkle (SOCKET_SPARKLE), which from time to time becomes, for two turns, a picture of the valve the socket takes (graphics 104-107 share the valves' sprites, 96-99), hovering 13 above the socket. Whenever anything lies in the room from the places -- a valve, even a seated one, or an extra life -- it goes, rubbed out and emptied, so a chamber once activated has none.
+10 counts the two turns; then it is the sparkle again (graphic 4 more).
WANTED_VALVE AE17 CALL DRAW_AT_L12_D5 draw 12 left and 5 down
AE1A CALL ANY_SPECIAL_HERE anything from the places in the room: go
AE1D JP NZ,VANISH
AE20 LD A,(IX-$1D) 13 above the socket, the record before
AE23 ADD A,$0D
AE25 LD (IX+$03),A
AE28 DEC (IX+$10) two turns
AE2B RET NZ
AE2C SET 2,(IX+$00) then the sparkle, redrawn
AE30 JP SET_WIPE_AND_DRAW_FLAGS
