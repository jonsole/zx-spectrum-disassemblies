AE5D: A valve seated in its socket (graphics 100 to 103)
Reached through UPDATES. What a valve becomes when it activates its chamber (LOOSE_VALVE): it stays where it is, never falling, and cannot be picked up (TAKE_OR_LEAVE takes only graphics 96-99). The summary after a game counts a chamber activated by finding one of these in its room (SUMMARISE_CHAMBERS).
