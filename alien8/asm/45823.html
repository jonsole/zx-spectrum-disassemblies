B2FF: The update routine for a block that can be pushed (graphic 28)
Object template 2. Its flags have bit 2 set, and the collision code gives a thing with that bit the step of whatever pushes into it (ADJ_DU_FOR_OBJ_INTERSECT, ADJ_DV_FOR_OBJ_INTERSECT). Each turn it falls and moves by its steps; if it moved, its steps in U and V are cleared, so that a push moves it once and not on and on (the end of MOVE_PLAYER does the clearing), and it sounds a note pitched by its position and is redrawn. As Knight Lore's chest (upd_85) and table.
The position is kept before the move and compared after it, for the case where it has no step left but has moved. By the reading that cannot happen -- with every step zero the move adds nothing -- and the sessions never ran it.
Input
IX the block
PUSHABLE B2FF CALL DRAW_AT_L16_D9 the drawing nudge
This entry point is used by the routine at PUSHABLE_INVERTED.
PUSHABLE_MOVE B302 CALL SAVE_UVZ keep its place
B305 CALL DEC_DZ_AND_UPDATE_UVZ fall, and move
B308 CALL IS_OBJ_MOVING any step left in U, V or Z?
B30B JR NZ,PUSHABLE_0
B30D CALL SAME_UVZ none: redraw it only if it has moved (never, see above)
B310 RET Z
B311 JP SET_WIPE_AND_DRAW_FLAGS
PUSHABLE_0 B314 CALL CLEAR_DUV moving: one step per push; a note pitched by U, V and Z, and redraw it
B317 JP SOUND_AND_DRAW
