B3C5: The tune as a game starts
Played to its end by AFTER_GAME between the menu and the first room. Seventeen notes, 18 units of about 0.155 seconds; the notes are 37 to 45 of NOTES.
A tune is a string of note bytes ended by $FF: the low six bits of a note index the notes (NOTES, 0 would be a rest, and no tune has one), and the top two bits are its length less one, so a note lasts one to four units. The other tunes are in the same format. The winning tune (TUNE_WON) begins with these seventeen notes.
TUNE_START B3C5 DEFB $25,$28,$2C,$25,$2A,$2D,$2C,$2A Note bytes
B3CD DEFB $28,$2C,$2A,$28,$27,$2A,$28,$27
B3D5 DEFB $65
