B65F: Unused: Knight Lore's werewolf sound
Code nothing reaches: no instruction or table holds its address, and no session ran it. sna2ctl left it as data; it decodes cleanly to its RET. It is Knight Lore's sound_transform byte for byte (found by searching Knight Lore's snapshot for these bytes): a warble of 16 to 40 single waves, the number set by the low two bits of the graphic -- the four frames of Sabreman changing into the werewolf and back -- each at a half-wave count of the step XOR $55, plus the step. Alien 8 kept the routine and dropped its only caller.
