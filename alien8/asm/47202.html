B862: The end of the won game's scene
The zero that ends the list at SCENE_WON (SCENE_LOST_END). The stage-1 annotations had this byte as unused; it is read by SET_UP_SCENE at the end of every won game.
SCENE_WON_END B862 DEFB $00 End of the list
