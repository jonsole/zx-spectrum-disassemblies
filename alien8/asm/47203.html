B863: Mark the robot's room as seen
Used by the routine at ENTER_ROOM.
ROOMS_SEEN is a 256-bit map, a bit per room number: the byte is the room number divided by 8, the bit its low three bits. SET takes its bit number in the opcode, so the second byte of the SET instruction at the end is rewritten before it runs. Called as a room is entered (ENTER_ROOM); COUNT_ROOMS_SEEN counts the bits for the rating. Knight Lore's flag_room_visited, byte for byte in its logic.
