B8A9: The game is won: the arrival screen
Used by the routine at GAME_ENDED.
Jumped to from GAME_ENDED when WON is set. Clears the buffer, blanks the screen with bright red attributes, prints the seven lines of the arrival text -- the station arrived and slowed, the activation circuits held, all crew systems go -- clearing TEXT_SHOWN first so that the list also draws the border and shows the screen, then plays the arrival tune to its end, waits about fourteen seconds or for a key, and goes on to the summary as for a lost game. As Knight Lore's game_complete_msg.
ARRIVAL_SCREEN B8A9 CALL CLEAR_SCRN_BUFFER clear the buffer; the border black, the attributes bright red, the screen blank
B8AC CALL CLEAR_SCRN
B8AF LD DE,ARRIVAL_COLOURS colours from ARRIVAL_COLOURS, positions from ARRIVAL_XY, strings from ARRIVAL_TEXT: seven lines
B8B2 EXX
B8B3 LD HL,ARRIVAL_XY
B8B6 LD DE,ARRIVAL_TEXT
B8B9 LD B,$07
B8BB XOR A the first list since the flag was cleared: border and screen too
B8BC LD (TEXT_SHOWN),A
B8BF CALL DISPLAY_TEXT_LIST
B8C2 LD DE,TUNE_ARRIVAL the arrival tune, in full
B8C5 CALL PLAY_TUNE
B8C8 LD B,$08 about 14 seconds, or until a key
B8CA CALL WAIT_KEY_OR_TIME
B8CD JP SUMMARY_SCREEN then the summary
