BA36: Print the summary's three counts
Used by the routine at GAME_ENDED.
Prints the counts SUMMARISE_CHAMBERS has worked out over the spaces the summary's text list (SUMMARY_COLOURS) leaves for them, into the buffer, in the digits' font: the activated chambers (SUMMARY_ACTIVE) at pixel x 184, y 127; the unactivated ones (SUMMARY_IDLE) at x 184, y 95; the crew lost (SUMMARY_LOST, four BCD digits of which the last three are printed) at x 176, y 79. Called by the summary (GAME_ENDED), before it draws the border and shows the buffer.
