BA57: Print the number of lives
Used by the routines at COLOUR_PANEL and EXTRA_LIFE.
LIVES as two BCD digits into the buffer at pixel x 128, y 7, on the panel. Called when the panel is drawn (COLOUR_PANEL) and when an extra life is taken (EXTRA_LIFE), which then copies the two characters to the screen (BLIT_2X8).
LIVES counts in binary (START sets 5, NEW_LIFE takes one, EXTRA_LIFE adds one), so the display is right only while he has fewer than ten -- as in Knight Lore.
