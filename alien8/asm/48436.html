BD34: The colours of the carried things
Four attributes, indexed by the low four bits of a carried thing's graphic (SHOW_CARRIED): bright red, magenta, cyan and white ink on black, for the valves of graphics 96 to 99. Only valves can be carried (CAN_PICK_UP). sna2ctl took them for text, since they are the codes of four capital letters.
