BEA7: Can this thing be picked up?
Used by the routine at TAKE_OR_LEAVE.
Only a valve (graphics 96 to 99), and only when he is on or beside it: IS_ON_OR_NEAR_OBJ, their boxes overlapping in U and V, and in Z with him lowered by 4, so that a thing he stands on counts as touching. The caller (TAKE_OR_LEAVE) has made his box 4 bigger each way. Knight Lore's can_pickup_spec_obj and is_on_or_near_obj together; EXTRA_LIFE uses the second alone.
Input
IX the robot's legs
IY the thing
Output
F carry set if it can
CAN_PICK_UP BEA7 LD A,(IY+$00) a valve, graphic 96 to 99; otherwise no carry
BEAA SUB $60
BEAC CP $04
BEAE RET NC
