C357: Shorten dZ at the floor
Used by the routine at ADJ_FOR_OUT_OF_BOUNDS.
The floor is the only limit on Z: there is no ceiling. FLOOR is 64 in all three room sizes (ROOM_SIZES). A move that would take the object's base below it is shortened a unit at a time until it would not, and bit 2 of +$0C records that the move was stopped. Called by ADJ_FOR_OUT_OF_BOUNDS before the other objects are tried.
Input
IX the object
H dZ
Output
H dZ, shortened
