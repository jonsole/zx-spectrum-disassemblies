C40B: Out through the wall at high V?
Out if the near edge in V after the move is at or past the wall at 128 plus the room's V half-size. V becomes the marker $FF, to arrive at the wall at low V, and the room is the one 16 on: the next row, wrapping from the last row to the first.
Input
IX the legs' record
HL the room's half-sizes (pushed by HANDLE_EXIT_SCREEN)
EXIT_HIGH_V C40B POP HL H = the wall
C40C LD A,H
C40D ADD A,$80
C40F LD H,A
C410 LD A,(IX+$02) V + dV - half-size short of it: stay
C413 ADD A,(IX+$0A)
C416 SUB (IX+$05)
C419 CP H
C41A RET C
C41B LD (IX+$02),$FF Arrive at the wall at low V
C41F LD A,(IX+$08) Room + 16
C422 ADD A,$10
C424 JR EXIT_SCREEN
