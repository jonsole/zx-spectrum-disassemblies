C5E9: Shorten dU at the walls
Used by the routine at ADJ_FOR_OUT_OF_BOUNDS.
Keeps the object's whole footprint within the room: its U after the move no further from 128 than the room's U half-size (ROOM_HALF_U, 64 or 32) less its own half-size. dU is shortened a unit at a time, and bit 0 of +$0C set if it had to be.
Two things switch the walls off, as in Knight Lore: the count in bits 4-7 of +$0C, the walk into a room, which the legs' update runs down a turn at a time (PLAYER_LEGS); and bit 0 of the flags, which a doorway sets on the robot while he is in it (CHK_PLYR_NEAR_ARCH). That is how he walks through one at all.
Input
IX the object
C dU
Output
C dU, shortened
