CB8A: Draw the border round the screen
Used by the routines at GAME_ENDED and DISPLAY_MENU.
From BORDER_DATA, into the buffer through the spare record at PANEL_RECORD: the corner sprite (graphic 4) drawn four times, turned each way; the top and bottom edges as 24 pieces of graphic 6, 8 pixels apart; and the sides as 128 of graphic 5 -- a sprite one pixel high -- a pixel apart. It frames the menu (DISPLAY_MENU, the first text list printed) and the screens after a game (GAME_ENDED); a room has none. Knight Lore's print_border, with the same counts and steps.
