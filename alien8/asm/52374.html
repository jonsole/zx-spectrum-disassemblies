CC96: The address of the object record numbered A
Used by the routines at SORT_AND_DRAW and RENDER_DYNAMIC_OBJECTS.
Bit 7 of A is ignored: the draw list (DRAW_LIST) sets it on entries already drawn. BC is kept. As Knight Lore's get_ptr_object, from OBJECTS here.
Input
A the record's number, 0-55
Output
HL the record
