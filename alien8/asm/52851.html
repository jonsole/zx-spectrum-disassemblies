CE73: Clear the screen and make the border black
Used by the routines at AFTER_GAME, GAME_ENDED and ARRIVAL_SCREEN.
A black border, the attributes bright red on black (CLR_ATTRIBUTE_MEMORY), and the bitmap cleared (CLR_BITMAP_MEMORY). Used at the start of every game (AFTER_GAME) and by the screens after a game (GAME_ENDED, ARRIVAL_SCREEN). As Knight Lore's clear_scrn.
CLEAR_SCRN CE73 XOR A
CE74 OUT ($FE),A
CE76 CALL CLR_ATTRIBUTE_MEMORY
CE79 JR CLR_BITMAP_MEMORY
