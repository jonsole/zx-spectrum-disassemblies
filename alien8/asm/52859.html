CE7B: Clear the screen buffer
Used by the routines at GAME_ENDED, ARRIVAL_SCREEN, MENU and ENTER_ROOM.
6144 bytes of BUFFER, through CLR_BYTE (CLEAR_OBJECTS): on entering a room (ENTER_ROOM), on the menu (MENU) and on the screens after a game. As Knight Lore's clear_scrn_buffer.
