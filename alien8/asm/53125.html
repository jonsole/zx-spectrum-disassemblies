CF85: Copy a rectangle of the buffer to the screen
Used by the routines at RUN_CLOCK, SHOW_CARRIED, BLIT_2X8 and RENDER_DYNAMIC_OBJECTS.
Works up the screen a row at a time, as SHOW_BUFFER does: for the areas RENDER_DYNAMIC_OBJECTS wiped, the carried things on the panel (SHOW_CARRIED), and pieces of the panel redrawn in place (RUN_CLOCK, BLIT_2X8). As Knight Lore's blit_to_screen.
Input
HL the buffer address of its bottom-left byte
DE the display address of the same byte
B the height in rows
C the width in bytes
