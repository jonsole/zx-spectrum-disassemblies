EA00: The stack
From here up to MIRROR_TABLE, where ENTRY puts the stack pointer; the main loop puts it back there before every object's update routine (AFTER_GAME), so nothing is left on the stack from one object to the next. The deepest use is RENDER_DYNAMIC_OBJECTS, which keeps three words for every area it wipes until they are copied to the screen. Zeros on the tape.
