8060: Read the movement keys into the player's record
Used by the routine at MOVE_PLAYER.
V (move) and C (jump) become bits 2 and 1 of the flags at +7, in one go. SYMBOL SHIFT and M turn the player, but only when not stunned.
Input
IX The player's record
READ_CONTROLS 8060 LD BC,$FEFE CAPS SHIFT to V, made active high: C is bit 3, V bit 4.
8063 IN A,(C)
8065 CPL
8066 AND $18 Down to bits 1 and 2: jump and move.
8068 RRCA
8069 RRCA
806A LD E,A Replace the player's jump and move bits with them.
806B LD A,(IX+$07)
806E AND $F9
8070 OR E
8071 LD (IX+$07),A
8074 LD A,(IX+$06) Stunned: no turning.
8077 AND A
8078 RET NZ
8079 LD B,$7F SPACE to B: SYMBOL SHIFT is bit 1, M bit 2.
807B IN A,(C)
807D CPL
807E AND $06
8080 CP $02 SYMBOL SHIFT alone: turn one way...
8082 JR NZ,READ_CONTROLS_0
8084 DEC (IX+$04)
8087 RET
READ_CONTROLS_0 8088 CP $04 ...M alone: the other.
808A JR NZ,READ_CONTROLS_1
808C INC (IX+$04)
READ_CONTROLS_1 808F RET
