8097: Wait for a key (USR 32919)
Loops on the ROM's KEY-SCAN until it reports a key. BASIC calls it for every "press any key" pause.
WAIT_KEY 8097 CALL $028E KEY-SCAN.
809A LD A,$FF E is $FF while no key is down.
809C CP E
809D JR Z,WAIT_KEY
809F RET
