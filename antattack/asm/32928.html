80A0: Draw one sprite into the render buffer
Used by the routine at DRAW_HERE.
A sprite frame is 64 bytes: 16 rows of mask, graphic, mask, graphic. Frame f lives 64f bytes above PLAY, so the frame number is the address, less PLAY, divided by 64 -- the sprites are at SPRITES (frames $68-$7F) and MORE_SPRITES (frames $DC-$FF).
Input
IX The frame byte of the sprite's entry in SPRITE_LIST; moved on one
HL The sprite's place in PLANES
DE The render buffer's row length less one ($1F)
