8203: Draw a block (views 0 and 2)
One block's picture, drawn straight into the render buffer as immediate data, 16 pixels wide. Each of the eight steps paints one row of the block's outline -- the two bytes' edge pixels merged with what is behind them -- and the row eight below it outright, in the dithered shades of the faces. There is one of these for each pair of views, since turning the view round a quarter mirrors the shading: DRAW_BLOCK_TURNED is the other. SELECT_BLOCK_DRAWER points IY at the right one and DRAW_SCENE jumps to it.
Ends by jumping back into DRAW_SCENE rather than returning.
Input
HL The block's place in PLANES
DE The row length less one ($1F)
