8360: Step the random number generator
Used by the routines at MOVE_ANT and NOISE.
A 32-bit shift register at RANDOM_BITS, big-endian, fed back from two bits of its first byte. The carry out is the random bit: MOVE_ANT uses it to pick which way an ant turns and NOISE to make a hiss.
