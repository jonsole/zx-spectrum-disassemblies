8400: Turn the view with 0, P, ENTER or SPACE
Used by the routine at GAME_FRAME.
Each key picks one of the four views and re-centres VIEW_ORIGIN on the player for it, the view origin being the player's position less an offset that depends on the view. No key: nothing changes.
