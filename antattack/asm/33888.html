8460: Scroll the view when the player nears its edge
Used by the routine at DRAW_VIEW.
Reads where the player was placed this frame (the last entry of SPRITE_LIST) and, if that is outside a central window of the view, steps VIEW_ORIGIN a cell in the direction the player is facing -- unless the player is jumping or falling. It takes effect next frame, since the view has already been gathered.
