84A0: Draw the view
Used by the routine at GAME_FRAME.
A painter's algorithm in eleven steps: gather the map, sort it into PLANES by height, clear the render buffer, work out where each object falls in PLANES, scroll if the player is near the edge, sort the objects by that, turn their places into distances, pick the block picture for this view, paint the lot back to front, and copy it to the screen.
DRAW_VIEW 84A0 CALL GATHER_VIEW The map, into VIEW_CELLS.
84A3 CALL BUILD_PLANES Into PLANES, by height.
84A6 CALL CLEAR_BUFFER_ROWS Clear the visible rows of the render buffer, a quarter at a time.
84A9 CALL CLEAR_BUFFER_ROWS
84AC CALL CLEAR_BUFFER_ROWS
84AF CALL CLEAR_BUFFER_ROWS
84B2 CALL PROJECT_SPRITES Where each object falls.
84B5 CALL SCROLL_VIEW Scroll next frame, if need be.
84B8 CALL SORT_SPRITES Sort the objects...
84BB CALL SPRITE_DISTANCES ...and space them out.
84BE CALL SELECT_BLOCK_DRAWER The block picture for this view.
84C1 CALL DRAW_SCENE Paint.
84C4 CALL COPY_TO_SCREEN Show.
84C7 RET
