8600: Work out where each object falls in PLANES
For each object, last to first, its position relative to VIEW_ORIGIN is turned for the view and projected: the half-row is v - u - 2h and the column (u + v) / 2, where u and v are the turned coordinates and h the height. The place is height x 512 + half-row x 16 + column -- the height counting a whole pass of DRAW_SCENE -- and it goes into SPRITE_LIST with the sprite frame: the object's first frame, plus four times its animation frame, plus its facing relative to the view.
