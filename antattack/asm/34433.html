8681: Gather the cells in view, view 0
Each row is 16 cells along a diagonal from the start, then 16 more from the cell beside it; the next row starts a cell further back. The other three views' routines are the same with the signs turned.
GATHER_VIEW0 8681 EX AF,AF' First half-row...
8682 PUSH DE
8683 LD B,$10
GATHER_VIEW0_0 8685 CALL READ_MAP_CELL
8688 INC E
8689 INC D
868A DJNZ GATHER_VIEW0_0
868C POP DE ...then the second, from the next cell over.
868D INC D
868E PUSH DE
868F LD B,$10
GATHER_VIEW0_1 8691 CALL READ_MAP_CELL
8694 INC E
8695 INC D
8696 DJNZ GATHER_VIEW0_1
8698 POP DE The next row starts a cell back.
8699 DEC E
869A EX AF,AF'
869B DEC A
869C JR NZ,GATHER_VIEW0
869E RET
