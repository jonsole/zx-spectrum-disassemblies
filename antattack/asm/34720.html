87A0: See whether an exploding grenade got an ant
Only ants at the grenade's height and not already exploding. Within four cells (counted along the grid, not diagonally) the ant is blown up -- "GOOD SHOT!" -- and within seven it is stunned for 24 frames. Both seen in play, in staged scenes.
Input
HL The grenade's x and y
C Its height
IY The ant
BLAST_ANT 87A0 LD A,(IY+$02) Another height, or already exploding: no.
87A3 CP C
87A4 RET NZ
87A5 LD A,(IY+$09)
87A8 AND A
87A9 RET NZ
87AA LD E,(IY+$00)
87AD LD D,(IY+$01)
87B0 CALL DISTANCE
87B3 CP $08 Eight or more away: missed.
87B5 RET NC
87B6 CP $05 Five to seven: stunned.
87B8 JR C,BLAST_ANT_0
87BA LD (IY+$06),$18
87BE RET
BLAST_ANT_0 87BF LD (IY+$09),$05 Four or less: blown up...
87C3 LD (IY+$06),$00
87C7 LD A,(GRENADE_EVENTS) ...and "GOOD SHOT!".
87CA OR $04
87CC LD (GRENADE_EVENTS),A
87CF RET
