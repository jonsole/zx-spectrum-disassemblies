88C0: The rescued person shares the player's cell
Only FOLLOW_PLAYER comes here, when the distance between the two is 0: one is standing on the other. If the rescued person is the higher of the two they take the player's facing and walk on; if lower, they stop. Both seen in play, in staged scenes.
Input
IX The rescued person
IY The player
Output
C The player's facing
ON_TOP 88C0 LD C,(IY+$04)
88C3 LD A,(IX+$02)
88C6 NOP A NOP, left where something was taken out.
88C7 CP (IY+$02)
88CA RET NC
88CB RES 2,(IX+$07)
88CF RET
