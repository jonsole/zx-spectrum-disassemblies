8900: Move a position one cell in a direction
Used by the routines at SCROLL_VIEW, MOVE_OBJECT and FOLLOW_PLAYER.
Input

HL The position: L is x, H is y

8906 JR Z,STEP_1

890A JR Z,STEP_0

STEP_0 890E DEC H 2: y down.
890F RET
