8A5D: An ant's turn
Used by the routine at MOVE_ANTS.
A paralysed ant does nothing. Otherwise an ant moves every frame except one in every +D: 2 is half speed, 20 very nearly full. The first ant is the fast one (line 140's DATA); BASIC sets the others' speed from sp, which goes up as the levels do.
