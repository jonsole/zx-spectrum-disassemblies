8BD1: Paralyse the ant the player has landed on
Used by the routine at LANDED.
An ant that is not exploding and not already paralysed is paralysed for good, with "PARALYSED AN ANT !". Seen in play, by dropping the player on a stunned ant; and landing on a plain one-block wall finds no ant here and returns.
Input
HL The cell
PARALYSE_ANT 8BD1 LD DE,$0010 Five ants.
8BD4 LD IY,ANT1
8BD8 LD B,$05
PARALYSE_ANT_0 8BDA LD A,(IY+$00) Is this one in the cell?
8BDD CP L
8BDE JR NZ,PARALYSE_ANT_1
8BE0 LD A,(IY+$01)
8BE3 CP H
8BE4 JR Z,PARALYSE_ANT_2
PARALYSE_ANT_1 8BE6 ADD IY,DE
8BE8 DJNZ PARALYSE_ANT_0
8BEA RET
PARALYSE_ANT_2 8BEB LD A,(IY+$09) Not while exploding, and not twice.
8BEE AND A
8BEF RET NZ
8BF0 LD A,$FF
8BF2 CP (IY+$06)
8BF5 RET Z
8BF6 LD (IY+$06),A Paralysed, for good.
8BF9 LD A,$10
8BFB CALL PLAY_SCRIPT
8BFE RET
