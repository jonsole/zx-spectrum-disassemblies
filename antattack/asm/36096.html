8D00: Throw a grenade, or keep one flying
Used by the routine at MOVE_GRENADE.
S, D, F and G throw, further along the keyboard throwing further: each key's bit is its flight time (2, 4, 8 or 16 frames). The grenade starts at the player and moves like anything else, a cell a frame; when the time runs out it explodes where it is.
Every frame of the flight, not only the last, the grenade is checked against the player's cell and the rescued person's: if it is in either, they are blown up.

IY The player
