8F00: React to what happened to the player and the rescued person
Used by the routine at GAME_FRAME.
Reads and clears the two event bytes (+F). A step is a click; a bad fall, "NASTY FALL !" or "HELP! I FELL!"; blown up, energy to 0 with "SILLY! YOU BLEW YOURSELF UP !" or "HOW COULD YOU ?"; bitten, an energy point off with "BITTEN!" or "THEY GOT ME", or when it reaches 0, "EATEN ALIVE" or "IVE BEEN EATEN ALIVE". All seen in play.
HANDLE_EVENTS 8F00 XOR A E = the player's event, D = the other's, both cleared.
