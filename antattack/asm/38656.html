9700: Where the load finishes
The loader in the BASIC line leaves LD-BYTES's return address pointing here. A failed load (carry clear) or a short one (DE not zero) resets the machine.
LOADED 9700 JP NC,$0000 Carry clear: a tape error.
9703 LD A,D Bytes left over: a short load.
9704 OR E
9705 JP NZ,$0000
9708 LD A,$98 The interrupt vectors at INTERRUPT_VECTORS: every one points to INTERRUPT.
970A LD I,A
970C IM 2
970E DI Run the BASIC.
970F JP RESTART_BASIC
