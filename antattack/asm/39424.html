9A00: Sprites: the grenade and the girl
This entry is frame $68: grenade's own frames.
Frames $68-$6B are the grenade's by its record. In flight it is drawn as $F4 onwards, and its home, x=0, y=$40, is open ground far outside the walls -- but it is drawn there, as these frames, whenever the player goes near it (watched in the emulator). $6C-$7F are the girl. 64 bytes a frame, mask and graphic interleaved (see DRAW_SPRITE).
