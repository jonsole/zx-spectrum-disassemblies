757D: What is in each room
One pointer per room, 150 of them, each to a $0000-terminated list of the records that belong to that room. Read out of the game, room $00's list names $EEE0, $EEF0 and $EF00 -- which are exactly the door records found in the running game when the player starts there.
The lists themselves follow immediately, from ROOM_LIST_00 on. Their entries are addresses into the initial-state template at INITIAL_STATE rather than into the runtime tables, and subtracting ROOM_CONTENTS is exactly the relocation from one to the other. See POPULATE_ROOM.
