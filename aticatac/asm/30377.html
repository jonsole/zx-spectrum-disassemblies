76A9: What is in room $00

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
12 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: A.C.G. key, piece 1, A.C.G. key, piece 2, A.C.G. key, piece 3 and sprite $31.
