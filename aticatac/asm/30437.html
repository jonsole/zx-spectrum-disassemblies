76E5: What is in room $04

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
5 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_04 76E5 DEFW DOOR_R04_R03 A door to room $03
76E7 DEFW DOOR_R19_R04 A door to room $19
76E9 DEFW DOOR_R05_R04 A door to room $05
76EB DEFW GHOST_PICTURE_R04_R15 A ghost picture
76ED DEFW WALL_TROPHY_R04_R14 A wall trophy
76EF DEFW $0000 End of the list
