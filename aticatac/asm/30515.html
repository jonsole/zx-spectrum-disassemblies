7733: What is in room $0A

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
ROOM_LIST_0A 7733 DEFW DOOR_R0A_R09 A door to room $09
7735 DEFW DOOR_R0B_R0A A door to room $0B
7737 DEFW BOOKCASE_R0A_R16 A bookcase (the wizard only) to room $16
7739 DEFW WALL_ANTLERS_R00_R0B A wall trophy
773B DEFW $0000 End of the list
