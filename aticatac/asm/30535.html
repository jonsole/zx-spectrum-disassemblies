7747: What is in room $0C

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food and food.
ROOM_LIST_0C 7747 DEFW DOOR_R0C_R0B A door to room $0B
7749 DEFW DOOR_R0D_R0C A door to room $0D
774B DEFW GHOST_PICTURE_R0B_R0C A ghost picture
774D DEFW WALL_SHIELD_R70_R0C A wall trophy
774F DEFW $0000 End of the list
