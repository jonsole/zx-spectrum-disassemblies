77FF: What is in room $1E

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_1E 77FF DEFW DOOR_R1E_R1F A door to room $1F
7801 DEFW CYAN_DOOR_R25_R1E A cyan door to room $25
7803 DEFW $0000 End of the list
