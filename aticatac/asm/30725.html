7805: What is in room $1F

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
3 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: collectable $88.
ROOM_LIST_1F 7805 DEFW DOOR_R1E_R1F A door to room $1E
7807 DEFW DOOR_R1F_R20 A door to room $20
7809 DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R1F_R21 A suit of armour
780B DEFW $0000 End of the list
