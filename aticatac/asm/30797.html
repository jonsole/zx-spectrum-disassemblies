784D: What is in room $27

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
7 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_27 784D DEFW DOOR_R27_R28 A door to room $28
784F DEFW DOOR_R2E_R27 A door to room $2E
7851 DEFW DOOR_R27_R2F A door to room $2F
7853 DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R27_R2B A suit of armour
7855 DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R27_R2B_B A suit of armour
7857 DEFW WALL_ANTLERS_R5A_R27 A pumpkin picture
7859 DEFW WALL_TROPHY_R5A_R27 A wall shield
785B DEFW $0000 End of the list
