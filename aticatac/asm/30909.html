78BD: What is in room $33

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_33 78BD DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R32_R33 A cave door to room $32
78BF DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R33_R34 A cave door to room $34
78C1 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R33_R36 A cave door to room $36
78C3 DEFW SKELETON_R33_R55 A skeleton
78C5 DEFW $0000 End of the list
