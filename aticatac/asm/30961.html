78F1: What is in room $3A

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
3 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food and food.
ROOM_LIST_3A 78F1 DEFW GREEN_CAVE_DOOR_R38_R3A A green cave door to room $38
78F3 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R3A_R3B A cave door to room $3B
78F5 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R3A_R94 A cave door to room $94
78F7 DEFW $0000 End of the list
