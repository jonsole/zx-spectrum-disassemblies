7923: What is in room $40

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: mushroom, which drains the player's life force.
ROOM_LIST_40 7923 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R3F_R40 A cave door to room $3F
7925 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R40_R41 A cave door to room $41
7927 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R40_R42 A cave door to room $42
7929 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R40_R8F A cave door to room $8F
792B DEFW $0000 End of the list
