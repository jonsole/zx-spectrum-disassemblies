792D: What is in room $41

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food and food.
ROOM_LIST_41 792D DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R40_R41 A cave door to room $40
792F DEFW BOOKCASE_R3E_R41 A bookcase (the wizard only) to room $3E
7931 DEFW $0000 End of the list
