7939: What is in room $43

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: mushroom, which drains the player's life force and devil.
ROOM_LIST_43 7939 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R42_R43 A cave door to room $42
793B DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R43_R44 A cave door to room $44
793D DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R43_R46 A cave door to room $46
793F DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R1D_R43 A cave door to room $1D
7941 DEFW $0000 End of the list
