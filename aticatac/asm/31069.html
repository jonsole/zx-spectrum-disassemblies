795D: What is in room $48

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: collectable $83.
ROOM_LIST_48 795D DEFW RED_CAVE_DOOR_R47_R48 A red cave door to room $47
795F DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R48_R49 A cave door to room $49
7961 DEFW CYAN_CAVE_DOOR_R48_R4A A cyan cave door to room $4A
7963 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R48_R4D A cave door to room $4D
7965 DEFW $0000 End of the list
