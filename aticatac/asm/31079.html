7967: What is in room $49

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: collectable $89 and food.
ROOM_LIST_49 7967 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R48_R49 A cave door to room $48
7969 DEFW BOOKCASE_R3D_R49 A bookcase (the wizard only) to room $3D
796B DEFW $0000 End of the list
