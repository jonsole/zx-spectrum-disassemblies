7973: What is in room $4B

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_4B 7973 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R4A_R4B A cave door to room $4A
7975 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R4B_R4C A cave door to room $4C
7977 DEFW TRAPDOOR_R61_R4B Where the trapdoor in room $61 lands: a rug
7979 DEFW BARREL_R38_R4B A barrel (the serf only) to room $38
797B DEFW $0000 End of the list
