7999: What is in room $50

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
3 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: mushroom, which drains the player's life force.
ROOM_LIST_50 7999 DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R4F_R50 A cave door to room $4F
799B DEFW CAVE_DOOR_R50_R51 A cave door to room $51
799D DEFW GREEN_CAVE_DOOR_R50_R52 A green cave door to room $52
799F DEFW $0000 End of the list
