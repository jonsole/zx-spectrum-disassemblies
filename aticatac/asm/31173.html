79C5: What is in room $56

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
6 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: humpback.
ROOM_LIST_56 79C5 DEFW DOOR_R56_R57 A door to room $57
79C7 DEFW DOOR_R56_R5A A door to room $5A
79C9 DEFW CYAN_DOOR_R67_R56 A cyan door to room $67
79CB DEFW YELLOW_DOOR_R69_R56 A yellow door to room $69
79CD DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R56_R24 A suit of armour
79CF DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R56_R24_B A suit of armour
79D1 DEFW $0000 End of the list
