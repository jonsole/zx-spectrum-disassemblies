7A61: What is in room $64

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: collectable $84.
ROOM_LIST_64 7A61 DEFW DOOR_R63_R64 A door to room $63
7A63 DEFW YELLOW_DOOR_R64_R65 A yellow door to room $65
7A65 DEFW CYAN_DOOR_R60_R64 A cyan door to room $60
7A67 DEFW A_C_G_SHIELD_R66_R64 A wall trophy
7A69 DEFW $0000 End of the list
