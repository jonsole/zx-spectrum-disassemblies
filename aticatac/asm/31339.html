7A6B: What is in room $65

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
5 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_65 7A6B DEFW YELLOW_DOOR_R64_R65 A yellow door to room $64
7A6D DEFW DOOR_R61_R65 A door to room $61
7A6F DEFW DOOR_R65_R1B A door to room $1B
7A71 DEFW TRAPDOOR_R03_R65 Where the trapdoor in room $03 lands: a rug
7A73 DEFW WALL_TROPHY_R66_R65 A wall shield
7A75 DEFW $0000 End of the list
