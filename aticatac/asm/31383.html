7A97: What is in room $69

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food, food and food.
ROOM_LIST_69 7A97 DEFW YELLOW_DOOR_R69_R56 A yellow door to room $56
7A99 DEFW BOOKCASE_R69_R6A A bookcase (the wizard only) to room $6A
7A9B DEFW $0000 End of the list
