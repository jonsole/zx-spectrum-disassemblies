7AAD: What is in room $6C

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_6C 7AAD DEFW DOOR_R6B_R6C A door to room $6B
7AAF DEFW CYAN_DOOR_R6C_R03 A cyan door to room $03
7AB1 DEFW TRAPDOOR_R8B_R6C Where the trapdoor in room $8B lands: a rug
7AB3 DEFW BOOKCASE_R6C_R6E A bookcase (the wizard only) to room $6E
7AB5 DEFW $0000 End of the list
