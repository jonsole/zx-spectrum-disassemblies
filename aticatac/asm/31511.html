7B17: What is in room $78

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
3 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food.
ROOM_LIST_78 7B17 DEFW DOOR_R77_R78 A door to room $77
7B19 DEFW DOOR_R78_R79 A door to room $79
7B1B DEFW TRAPDOOR_R78_R8A A trapdoor, falling to room $8A
7B1D DEFW $0000 End of the list
