7B61: What is in room $80

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food, food and food.
ROOM_LIST_80 7B61 DEFW GREEN_DOOR_R7F_R80 A green door to room $7F
7B63 DEFW DOOR_R80_R82 A door to room $82
7B65 DEFW $0000 End of the list
