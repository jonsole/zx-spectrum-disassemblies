7B81: What is in room $84

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
4 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: collectable $87.
ROOM_LIST_84 7B81 DEFW RED_DOOR_R83_R84 A red door to room $83
7B83 DEFW DOOR_R84_R86 A door to room $86
7B85 DEFW YELLOW_DOOR_R84_R89 A yellow door to room $89
7B87 DEFW TRAPDOOR_R76_R84 Where the trapdoor in room $76 lands: a rug
7B89 DEFW $0000 End of the list
