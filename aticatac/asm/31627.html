7B8B: What is in room $85

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
2 records, then a zero to end the list. Each entry is its record in INITIAL_STATE, which POPULATE_ROOM relocates to where the running game keeps it.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food and food.
ROOM_LIST_85 7B8B DEFW DOOR_R86_R85 A door to room $86
7B8D DEFW DOOR_R85_R83 A door to room $83
7B8F DEFW $0000 End of the list
