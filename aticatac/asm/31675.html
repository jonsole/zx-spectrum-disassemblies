7BBB: What is in room $8A

As a new game sets it out, drawn by the game itself.
6 records, then a zero to end the list. Add $8A83 to each to get where it ends up at run time.
Also here when a game starts, found by their own room byte rather than named in the list: food and food.
ROOM_LIST_8A 7BBB DEFW DOOR_R89_R8A A door to room $89
7BBD DEFW GREEN_DOOR_R8A_R23 A green door to room $23
7BBF DEFW TRAPDOOR_R78_R8A Where the trapdoor in room $78 lands: a rug
7BC1 DEFW BARREL_R8A_R08 A barrel (the serf only) to room $08
7BC3 DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R88_R8A A suit of armour
7BC5 DEFW SUIT_OF_ARMOUR_R88_R8A_B A suit of armour
7BC7 DEFW $0000 End of the list
