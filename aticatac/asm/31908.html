7CA4: Take the highlight off a menu cell
Used by the routine at HIGHLIGHT_LINE.
Clears bit 7 of an attribute -- the FLASH bit -- and steps on. The title screen shows the current choice by flashing it, so selecting is a matter of turning this bit off one line and on another.
UNHIGHLIGHT 7CA4 RES 7,(HL)
7CA6 INC HL
7CA7 RET
