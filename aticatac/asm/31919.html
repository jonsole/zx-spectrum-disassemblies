7CAF: Draw the title screen's list of options
Points the tile source at the text font and then walks seven entries, each with its own string and position, writing the current one into $5E22 as it goes. Those seven are the six choices -- three control methods and three characters -- and the line that starts the game.
DRAW_MENU 7CAF LD HL,$BE4C
7CB2 LD ($5E01),HL
7CB5 LD DE,$7CEA
7CB8 EXX
7CB9 LD HL,$7CF1
7CBC LD DE,$7CF8
7CBF LD B,$07
DRAW_MENU_0 7CC1 EXX
7CC2 LD A,(DE)

7CC6 INC DE
7CC7 EXX

7CC9 LD A,(HL)
7CCA INC HL
7CCB PUSH HL
