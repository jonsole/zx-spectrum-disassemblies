80AA: Blank everything and black the border
Used by the routines at TITLE_SCREEN and START_GAME.
Bitmap, attributes, and then an OUT to port $FE with zero, which sets the border black. The whole screen gone in three calls.
CLEAR_SCREEN 80AA CALL CLEAR_ATTRIBUTES
80AD CALL CLEAR_DISPLAY
80B0 XOR A
80B1 OUT ($FE),A
80B3 RET
