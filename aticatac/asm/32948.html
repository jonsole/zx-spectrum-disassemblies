80B4: Blank the display file
Writes $00 over $4000 for $58 pages -- the whole bitmap, all three thirds. The two routines below it share the same inner loop with a different address and length, which is why they are three entry points rather than three routines.
CLEAR_DISPLAY 80B4 LD HL,$4000
80B7 LD B,$58
This entry point is used by the routine at CLEAR_VARIABLES.
CLEAR_DISPLAY_0 80B9 LD C,$00
This entry point is used by the routine at CLEAR_ATTRIBUTES.
CLEAR_DISPLAY_1 80BB LD (HL),C
80BC INC HL
80BD LD A,H
80BE CP B
80BF JR NZ,CLEAR_DISPLAY_1
80C1 RET
