80CB: Blank the game's variables
Used by the routine at START_GAME.
$5E10 for $60 bytes -- the block holding the score, the clock, the inventory and everything else the game keeps about a session. Called when a new game starts.
CLEAR_VARIABLES 80CB LD HL,$5E10
80CE LD B,$60
80D0 JR CLEAR_FROM_HL
