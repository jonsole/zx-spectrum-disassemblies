8283: Fire, if there is nothing already in the air
Refuses outright if the weapon slot at $EA98 is occupied, so only one shot exists at a time -- that single test is the whole of the game's rate of fire. With the way clear it makes the sweep noise and calls FIRE_WEAPON to launch one.
