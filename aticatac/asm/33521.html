82F1: Drive the spinning sword
Sprites $38 to $3F are one sword drawn at eight angles, a compass point apart, and cycling through them is what makes it appear to spin.
This is the serf's weapon. Each character throws its own: firing as each in turn and reading the type out of the player record's upper half gives $3E for the serf, $41 to $47 for the knight's axe and $36 for the wizard's spell.
SPIN_SWORD 82F1 CALL ACTOR_TO_WORKSPACE
82F4 LD (IX+$05),$46
82F8 LD HL,$EA9F
82FB CALL SIGN_TO_DIRECTION
82FE JP SPIN_SPELL_0
