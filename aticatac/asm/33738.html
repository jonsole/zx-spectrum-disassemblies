83CA: How far to move, for each of sixteen headings
Sixteen pairs, x then y, indexed by an actor's +$09 doubled. They run (3,0), (3,0), (3,1), (3,1), (3,1), then six of (2,2), then (1,3) three times and (0,3) twice -- a quarter turn walked round in sixteen steps, with the total distance kept roughly the same so a diagonal is not faster than a straight line.
