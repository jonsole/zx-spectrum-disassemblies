83EA: Put a new monster into the room
Used by the routine at UPDATE_KNIGHT.
Monsters are not placed once and left. When the player is in the room named by $5E26, a countdown at $5E27 runs down and a new creature is dropped into the room when it expires -- which is why standing still in one place does not make you safe.
It looks for a free slot among only the first three of the eight monster records, and gives up if all three are taken. Those three are also the ones INERT_SPRITE bothers to burn time for, so the spawning slots and the timed slots are the same three.
A new monster is a straight sixteen-byte copy of a template at MONSTER_TEMPLATE -- one LDIR, and the record is complete.
