845F: Move one actor, bounce it off the walls, animate it
The per-actor update. Actors drift in a direction until they hit the edge of the room, then reverse; the direction is re-rolled at intervals from the refresh register, which is the game's random number source.
Velocity lives in +$08 (x) and +$09 (y) and is nudged one step per call toward +2 or -2 rather than being set outright, so things accelerate and turn smoothly instead of snapping.

Nothing in the disassembly jumps here, which is not because the routine is dead: it is entry $5C/$5D of the table at ACTOR_HANDLERS, and DISPATCH_ACTOR reaches it through a JP (HL). Breaking here does catch it, 16 times in 20, always with IX = $EE80 -- the monster whose sprite byte is currently $5C. Two earlier rounds of sampling reported zero hits and concluded it was unused; both were taken before the player had finished spawning, when no monster was in the room yet.
STEP_ACTOR is also entered directly by eight other routines, which is why the position update is written as a separate stretch: they supply their own velocity in +$08/+$09 and reuse the bounce logic. Verified against the running game -- ($5E1D) reads 56 by 56, and sampled actor positions stay inside $58 +/- 56 by $68 +/- 56.
