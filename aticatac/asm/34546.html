86F2: Give a creature a random up or down
Reads the refresh register, takes one bit to decide whether to act and two more to make plus or minus two, and drops the result into the vertical velocity. The same trick MOVE_ACTOR uses for direction, applied to one axis.
RANDOM_VERTICAL 86F2 LD A,R
86F4 BIT 0,A
86F6 JR Z,RANDOM_VERTICAL_2
86F8 AND $04
86FA SUB $02
RANDOM_VERTICAL_0 86FC LD (IX+$09),A
86FF LD A,R
8701 RRA
8702 BIT 0,A
8704 JR Z,RANDOM_VERTICAL_3
8706 AND $04
8708 SUB $02
RANDOM_VERTICAL_1 870A LD (IX+$08),A
870D RET
RANDOM_VERTICAL_2 870E AND $02
8710 SUB $01
8712 JR RANDOM_VERTICAL_0
RANDOM_VERTICAL_3 8714 AND $02
8716 SUB $01
8718 JR RANDOM_VERTICAL_1
