8C35: Clear the castle away and show how it went
Used by the routine at LOSE_LIFE.
Reached from UPDATE_KNIGHT when the player's last life goes. It blanks the play area, prints GAME OVER across it, and hands over to DRAW_SUMMARY for the three figures underneath, then sits in a counting loop long enough to read them.
Note the second and third instructions: the tile source has to be pointed back at the text font first. During play it holds FONT_DIGITS, the copy biased so that digits index themselves, and anything printed through PLOT_TILE while it is still there comes out as the wrong glyphs entirely.
GAME_OVER 8C35 CALL CLEAR_PLAY_AREA The castle goes; the status panel down the side stays.
