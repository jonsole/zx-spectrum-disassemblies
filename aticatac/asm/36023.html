8CB7: Raise the player back out of the floor
The handler for sprite $66, and the mirror of DYING: +$06 counts up instead of down and the sprite rises. It is what runs at the start of a game as well as after a death, which is why the player's sprite byte reads $66 for several seconds before a new game is really under way -- and why CHECK_HIT, which only counts sprites below $31 as a player, cannot register a hit during it.
MATERIALISING 8CB7 LD A,($5E3C) A bonus flashing on the scroll takes priority over rising.

8CBB JR NZ,FLASH_SCORE
8CBD LD A,($5C78) One step in four frames.

8CCF CP (IX+$06) At the top, so become a player again.
