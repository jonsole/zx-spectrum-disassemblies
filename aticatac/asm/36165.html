8D45: Sink the player into the floor
The handler for sprite $67. Every fourth frame it counts +$06 down and shifts the sprite further down the screen by that much, so the character appears to sink. When the count passes zero it drops an object where the body was and hands over to $9443.
DYING 8D45 LD A,($5C78) One step in four frames.
8D48 AND $03
8D4A JR Z,MATERIALISING_1
8D4C DEC (IX+$06) Down a little further, until it goes negative.
8D4F JP M,DYING_0
8D52 LD A,(IX+$07)
