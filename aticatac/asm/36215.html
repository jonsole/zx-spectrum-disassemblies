8D77: Turn the controls into movement
The other end of READ_CONTROLS. It takes the byte that routine returns and turns it into a signed step on each axis: bit 0 right, bit 1 left, bit 2 down, bit 3 up, each tested for being clear because a zero bit is a pressed one. B holds the distance, so left is B negated and right is B as it stands.
The result goes through STEER rather than straight into the position, so the player accelerates into a direction and coasts out of it instead of starting and stopping dead.
