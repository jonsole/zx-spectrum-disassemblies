8EEF: Move a heading towards a target, within limits
Adds E and D to the pair at +$06 and +$07 and clamps the result to L and H, handling the negative side by negating, comparing and negating back. The two fields are a signed heading, so this is how a creature turns towards something gradually rather than snapping round to face it.
