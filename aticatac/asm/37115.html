90FB: Is the player within 12 pixels of this thing?
Used by the routines at EAT_FOOD, PICK_UP and MUSHROOM.
The symmetric version of the test PLAYER_AT_DOOR makes -- absolute difference on both axes against the same 12-pixel box CHECK_HIT uses, with no room check and no one-sidedness.
