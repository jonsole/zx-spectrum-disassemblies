91BC: Draw something twice over and make a noise
Draws the thing with the low bits of its drawing mode forced on, then again with the bottom bit of its sprite flipped -- the other animation frame -- then puts the mode back and draws it properly, and finishes by jumping into SOUND_NOISE_BURST. Three overlapping draws and a rasp, which is what a thing being destroyed looks and sounds like.
FLASH_AND_RASP 91BC LD A,($5E12) One gate before any of it.
91BF AND A
91C0 JP NZ,DRAW_DOOR
91C3 JR FLASH_AND_RASP_0
TRAPDOOR 91C5 LD A,($5E05)
91C8 AND A
91C9 JP NZ,TRAPDOOR_FALL
FLASH_AND_RASP_0 91CC LD A,(IX+$05) Force the low bits of the drawing mode on, and draw.
91CF PUSH AF
91D0 OR $03
91D2 LD (IX+$05),A
91D5 CALL DRAW_RECORD
91D8 LD A,(IX+$00) The other frame, over the top.
91DB XOR $01
91DD LD (IX+$00),A
91E0 CALL DRAW_RECORD
91E3 POP AF Put the mode back and draw it properly.
91E4 LD (IX+$05),A
91E7 CALL DRAW_DOOR
91EA JP SOUND_NOISE_BURST And the rasp.
BIG_DOOR 91ED LD BC,$2020
91F0 JR DOORWAY
