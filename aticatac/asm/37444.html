9244: A locked door of the first sort
Asks DOOR_NEEDS_KEY whether the player has the matching key. With one it sets the sprite on both halves to $02 through SET_BOTH_HALVES and goes through to ENTER_ROOM; without one it just draws itself shut.
DOOR_LOCKED_A 9244 CALL DOOR_NEEDS_KEY
9247 JP NC,DOOR_1
924A LD A,$02
This entry point is used by the routine at DOOR_LOCKED_B.
DOOR_LOCKED_A_0 924C CALL SET_BOTH_HALVES
924F JP ENTER_ROOM
