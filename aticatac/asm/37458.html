9252: A locked door of the second sort
The same as DOOR_LOCKED_A but setting $01 rather than $02, so the two look different once open. They share everything from the third instruction on.
DOOR_LOCKED_B 9252 CALL DOOR_NEEDS_KEY
9255 JP NC,DOOR_1
9258 LD A,$01
925A JR DOOR_LOCKED_A_0
