9260: Give both halves of a door the same sprite
Used by the routines at WAIT_THEN_ACT and DOOR_LOCKED_A.
Writes the sprite into the record IX points at, then flips bit 3 of the address -- the DOOR_OTHER_SIDE trick -- and writes it into the far half too. An open door has to look open from both rooms.
SET_BOTH_HALVES 9260 PUSH IX

9264 EX AF,AF'
9265 LD A,L
9266 XOR $08
