9358: Put the oldest carried thing back in the room
Used by the routines at PICK_UP and PUT_DOWN.
Reads the third slot at $5E38 and returns at once if it is empty, so nothing happens until the player is already carrying three. Otherwise it builds a record where the player is standing -- the sprite it had, the player's room from $EA91, then $80, then the player's x and y from $EA93 and $EA94 -- so a dropped object lands at your feet and can be picked straight back up.
