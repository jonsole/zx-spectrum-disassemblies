9398: Read the cursor keys
The branch READ_CONTROLS takes when the cursor option was chosen. The cursor keys are not adjacent in the matrix the way Q, W, E, R and T are, so this reads a different half-row and assembles the same five bits by hand rather than swapping two of them.
READ_CURSOR_KEYS 9398 LD A,$EF
939A OUT ($FD),A
939C IN A,($FE)
939E LD C,A
939F AND $08

93A2 LD A,C

93A4 RRCA

93A7 OR E
