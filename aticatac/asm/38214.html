9546: Open or shut a door, according to its sprite
Bit 0 of the sprite says which, so a door's own number carries whether it is currently open, and the two routines below do the work on both halves.
DOOR_OPEN_OR_SHUT 9546 LD A,(IX+$00)
9549 AND $01
954B JR Z,$9565
