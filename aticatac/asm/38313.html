95A9: Leave an object where the player is standing
Four slots of eight bytes at $EAE8. The first with a zero sprite is taken, given sprite $8F, and the seven bytes after it are copied straight out of the player's own record -- room, flag, position and all -- so the thing appears exactly where the player is. With all four in use the routine simply returns and nothing is dropped.
The copy is what makes it cheap: because an object and an actor share the same eight-byte header, placing one is one LDIR rather than half a dozen assignments.
