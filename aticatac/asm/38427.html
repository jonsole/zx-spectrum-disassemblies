961B: The A.C.G. door: it opens only for the whole of the A.C.G. key
The handler for the A.C.G. door. It walks the three inventory slots four bytes at a time and wants $8C, $8D and $8E in them, in that order -- the three pieces of the A.C.G. key, ACG_KEY_PARTS -- and only then opens the door (OPEN_DOOR) and behaves as one, with a wider doorway than most. Anything less and it is drawn shut (SHUT_DOOR). Through it is room $8E, and reaching that ends the game.
ACG_DOOR 961B LD HL,$5E32
961E LD DE,$0004
9621 LD A,(HL)
9622 CP $8C
9624 JR NZ,ACG_DOOR_0
9626 ADD HL,DE
9627 LD A,(HL)
9628 CP $8D
962A JR NZ,ACG_DOOR_0
962C ADD HL,DE
962D LD A,(HL)
962E CP $8E
9630 JR NZ,ACG_DOOR_0
9632 CALL OPEN_DOOR
9635 LD BC,$3020
9638 JP DOORWAY
ACG_DOOR_0 963B CALL SHUT_DOOR
963E JP DRAW_DOOR
