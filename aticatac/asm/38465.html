9641: Print the three end-of-game figures
Three labels and three numbers, stacked at the left of the cleared play area. The labels carry their own colour in their first byte and their own punctuation: the one for the clock ends with the font's colon glyph, so TIME's digits print either side of a colon that was drawn with the word.
It assumes the text font is already selected, which is why GAME_OVER repoints $5E01 before calling. Halfway through it switches to the digit-biased copy for the numbers.
