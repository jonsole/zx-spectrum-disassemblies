98D2: Put three of the keys, and the mummy, in rooms chosen for this game
Used by the routine at START_GAME.
Writes into INITIAL_STATE before START_GAME copies it: the rooms of GREEN_KEY, RED_KEY and CYAN_KEY, each one of eight from RANDOM_ROOMS_ONE, RANDOM_ROOMS_TWO and RANDOM_ROOMS_THREE, chosen by the frame counter mixed with the running values at $5E12 and $5E13. The mummy goes into the same room as the red key. The yellow key's room is not touched, and neither is anything else here -- so these are the things that are somewhere different in every game.
PLACE_KEYS 98D2 LD A,($5C78)
98D5 LD HL,RANDOM_ROOMS_ONE
98D8 CALL TABLE_LOOKUP_3BIT
98DB LD ($603E),A GREEN_KEY+1: the green key's room
98DE LD A,($5C78)
98E1 LD C,A
98E2 LD A,($5E12)
98E5 ADD A,C
98E6 LD HL,RANDOM_ROOMS_TWO
98E9 CALL TABLE_LOOKUP_3BIT
98EC LD ($6046),A RED_KEY+1: the red key's room...
98EF LD ($640E),A ...and MUMMY+1, the mummy's
98F2 LD A,($5C79)
98F5 LD C,A
98F6 LD A,($5E13)
98F9 ADD A,C
98FA LD HL,RANDOM_ROOMS_THREE
98FD CALL TABLE_LOOKUP_3BIT
9900 LD ($604E),A CYAN_KEY+1: the cyan key's room
9903 RET
