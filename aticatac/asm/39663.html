9AEF: Drawing mode 5: furniture turned a half turn (mirrored and upside down)
Mirrored like BLIT_SPRITE_MIRRORED, and turned over as well: the call to START_AT_LAST_ROW moves the data pointer to the last row before anything is drawn, so the rows come out in the opposite order.
This is why there are eight drawing routines in each family rather than one. They are the eight symmetries of a rectangle -- the four ways to flip it (as stored, mirrored, upside down, both, which is a half turn) and the same four turned through a right angle -- and the top three bits of a door's or piece of furniture's +$05 pick between them.
BLIT_SPRITE_FLIPPED 9AEF POP BC The combining opcode.

9AF3 LD (BLIT_FLIPPED_OP),A
9AF6 CALL FETCH_SPRITE
9AF9 CALL START_AT_LAST_ROW Move to the last row: this one draws bottom to top.
