9BEA: Draw the room the player is in
Looks the room up twice. Its own entry in ROOM_TABLE gives a colour and a shape number; the shape number then selects an entry in ROOM_SHAPES, which carries how far the player may walk and where the outline's geometry lives. Rooms therefore share outlines freely -- only the colour and the shape number are per-room.
Verified against a running game: in room $00 the table gives colour $42 and shape $00, shape $00 gives 56 by 56 and the two pointers $A9DF and $A9EF, and the machine's own $5E1A, $5E1D and $5E1E read back $42, 56 and 56 with the attribute file filled with $42.
