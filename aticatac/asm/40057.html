9C79: Draw a line between two points
Takes the difference along each axis, remembers which way each one runs in a pair of bits, and picks whichever axis is longer to step along -- the ordinary way of drawing a line one pixel at a time on a machine with no multiply. $5E23 and $5E24 hold the working values.

DE The other
