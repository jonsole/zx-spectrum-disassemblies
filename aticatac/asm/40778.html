9F4A: Draw a record's sprite
Used by the routines at TRY_FIRE_ALT, SPAWN_MONSTER_INTO_ROOM, MOVE_871A, MOVE_MUMMY, MOVE_HUMPBACK, MATERIALISING, DRAW_ROOM_CONTENTS, DROP_CARRIED, PLACE_PLAYER, DROP_OBJECT, DRAW_LIST, DRAW_LIVES and DRAW_TITLE_ICONS.
Sets the drawing up through SETUP_SPRITE_DRAW, clears the working count at $5E18, and draws. The entry most handlers use when they simply want something on the screen.
