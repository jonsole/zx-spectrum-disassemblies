A00E: Note where a thing is, then draw it
Copies the position out of the record into $5E16 and $5E17 before falling into the drawing proper. Those two are what the erase pass reads to find where something was last frame, so recording them here is what lets the next pass rub it out cleanly.
Input
IX The thing to draw
WORKSPACE_AND_DRAW A00E LD A,(IX+$03) Where it is now...
A011 LD ($5E16),A
A014 LD A,(IX+$04) ...kept for the pass that will erase it.
A017 LD ($5E17),A
