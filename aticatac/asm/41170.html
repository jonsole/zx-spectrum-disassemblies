A0D2: A colour writer using the second colour
Writes from E rather than D, so a sprite drawn through this mode comes out in its alternate colour.
FILL_ATTRS_ALT A0D2 POP HL
FILL_ATTRS_ALT_0 A0D3 PUSH BC
A0D4 PUSH HL
A0D5 DEC L
A0D6 LD (HL),E
A0D7 INC L
FILL_ATTRS_ALT_1 A0D8 LD (HL),D
A0D9 INC L
A0DA DJNZ FILL_ATTRS_ALT_1
A0DC POP HL
A0DD LD BC,$0020
A0E0 AND A
A0E1 SBC HL,BC
A0E3 POP BC
A0E4 DEC C
A0E5 JR NZ,FILL_ATTRS_ALT_0
A0E7 DEC L
A0E8 LD (HL),E
A0E9 INC L
A0EA JR FILL_ATTRS_3_2
