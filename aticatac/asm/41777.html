A331: The nine pictures on the title screen
Nine records of eight bytes, in the ordinary short form -- sprite, room, flag, x, y, drawing mode. Read out of the game: sprites $48 and $49 make the keyboard at the top, $4A and $4B the joystick, $32 and $33 the cursor keys, and then $01, $11 and $21 draw the knight, the wizard and the serf down the left, at the very sprite numbers DRAW_LIVES computes from the menu selection.
