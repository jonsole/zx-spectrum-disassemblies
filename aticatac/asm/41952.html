A3E0: The note that goes with the flashing score
Used by the routine at FLASH_SCORE.
One call from FLASH_SCORE, once every sixteen steps of its countdown. BC is $8060 -- half-period $80, and $60 is 96 cycles of it -- which measures as 96 milliseconds at about 1030 Hz: a clear steady pip rather than a sweep, and long enough to be heard over everything else.
SOUND_BONUS A3E0 LD BC,$8060
A3E3 JR BEEP_BC
