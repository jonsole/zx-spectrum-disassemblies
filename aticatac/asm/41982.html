A3FE: Free the slot when a sound finishes
Zeroing the sprite is all it takes: the dispatcher skips a record with sprite $00, so the sound simply stops being found. Shared by all three sound handlers.
END_SOUND A3FE LD (IX+$00),$00
A402 RET
