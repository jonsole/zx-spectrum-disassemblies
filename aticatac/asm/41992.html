A408: Sound $65, one frame of it
The same shape as SOUND_64 with the pitch derived differently -- three rotations, complemented, folded about $40 -- which is what makes it a different effect rather than the same one at another speed.
SOUND_65 A408 DEC (IX+$01) Counting down.
A40B JR Z,END_SOUND
A40D LD A,(IX+$01) Cycles from the count.
A410 LD C,A
A411 RLCA Pitch from the count as well, but along a different curve.
A412 RLCA
A413 RLCA
A414 CPL
A415 XOR $40
A417 LD B,A
A418 JP BEEP_0
