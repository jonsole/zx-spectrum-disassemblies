A45F: A sound whose pitch comes from a creature's heading
Takes +$06, complements and masks it, and uses the result as the half-period -- so the noise a thing makes depends on which way it is going.
SOUND_FROM_HEADING A45F LD A,(IX+$06)
A462 CPL
A463 RLCA
A464 AND $3F
A466 OR $40
A468 LD B,A
A469 LD C,$10
A46B JP BEEP_0
