A854: Colour and shape of each room
Two bytes per room, indexed by room number: the first is the attribute the whole play area is filled with, the second is an index into ROOM_SHAPES. With 152 rooms sharing a much smaller set of outlines, this is most of what makes the castle fit in memory.
