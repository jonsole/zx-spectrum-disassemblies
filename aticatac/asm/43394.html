A982: Geometry of each room shape
Six bytes per shape: how far the player may walk from the centre horizontally and vertically, then a pointer to the shape's vertex table, then a pointer to its edge list. Shape $00 reads 56, 56, $A9DF, $A9EF.
A vertex table is two bytes per point, x then y; an edge list is the $FF-separated groups DRAW_OUTLINE walks.
