B32A: The status panel, as tile numbers
24 rows of 8, each byte an index into PANEL_TILES. The loop at $A228 walks it a row at a time, calling PLOT_TILE for each cell and colouring it as it goes. The eight columns start at x=192, which is the first character column past the play area -- the same place PAINT_PANEL colours.
