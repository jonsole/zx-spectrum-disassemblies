B7AF: The wizard, drawn for sprite $1E, $20
20 rows of 16 pixels. The first byte is the row count; the rest are the rows, two bytes each, top down.
Shared by 2 codes, which is how the game gets a mirrored or repeated frame without a second copy of the picture.
