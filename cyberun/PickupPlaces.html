Where the pick-ups are dealt

The add-ons and the rocket's parts are not in the tables of things lying on the planet. At a new game new_game has deal_places deal each player 19 places from a pool of 46, following the dealing list dealing_list, into the player's record (player_places for the player in play). Each place is four bytes: map column, map row, x and y in the page. As the view moves, load_map_window puts any of the current player's places in the window that are not yet taken (bit 7 of the first byte) into the two pick-up slots: places 0-10 as graphic $0C, an add-on lying on the planet, with the place's number as its frame (addon_graphics), and places 11-18 as graphic $1E, rocket parts 0-7 (rocket_part_frames). The pictures are drawn by the game that way: one place given to the player, in an emptied scratch copy of the map, brought in by load_map_window and drawn by update_ground_objects.

The dealing list

An entry of 1 to 5 deals a random place not yet dealt from that group of the pool: a random byte (random_a) ANDed with the group's size less one, tried again while the place is taken, so the groups' sizes are powers of two. An entry of 0 deals nothing and leaves the place $FF, no place -- unless the next entry is 3, when the place before it is copied 16 to the right and 35 up. The pool is all free again at each call, so the two players' places are dealt independently and may coincide. At the end of a planet the last entry alone ($61BB) is dealt again by planet_secured: the nose cone moves.
