5B7A: The top of the stack
The landscape renderer (after_step_drawn) does not save and restore SP around its POP blitter: it loads SP with this address after each piece, which must be where the stack stands inside draw_landscape when that is called from the play loop -- three calls deep, so that these six bytes are three return addresses and the renderer's own RET still finds its caller's (inferred from the reset; the chain of calls was not traced here).
