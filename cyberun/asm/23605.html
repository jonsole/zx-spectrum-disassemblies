5C35: The piece is a ceiling
$80 when the piece's direction is 7 or more, the ceiling shapes (set_piece_shape); cleared for each piece by draw_landscape; read by after_step_drawn and fill_behind_moving_piece. The collision routines (next_box, hit_block, hit_slope, place_collision_box) also keep in it what has happened this pass, bit 7 a block hit and bit 4 a slope.
