5DB6: The number of objects in each map column
One byte for each of the 96 map columns: how many of the objects at map_object_kinds and map_object_places lie in it. They add up to 290. spawn_column_objects adds up the counts of the columns before the screen's column to find its first object, and takes the counts of that column and the next.
A 97th byte follows, a copy of column 0's count: when the screen is at column 95, the next column is column 0 again, and spawn_column_objects reads its count from here before it starts again at the beginning of the tables.
