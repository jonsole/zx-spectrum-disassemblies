605B: The places the pick-ups can be dealt to
At every new game deal_places deals each player nineteen places for the pick-ups, chosen at random from this pool and copied into the player's record (from +$1D of score or other_player_record); load_map_window later puts the ones in the screen's cells into object slots. Each place is four bytes: the map column, the map row, the x within the cell (to the slot's +2) and the y within the cell (to the slot's +5). While dealing, bit 7 of the first byte marks a place already given out.
Five groups, one for each value in the dealing list (dealing_list). No table says where they are: deal_places picks a group by its own code, loading its first place and a mask one less than its size. Within each group the places are in column order. Laid out below a line per place, decoded from the game's bytes when the disassembly is built.
