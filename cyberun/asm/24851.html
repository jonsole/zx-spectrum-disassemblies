6113: Deal a player the places of the pick-ups
Used by the routine at new_game.
Called twice by new_game at a new game, once for each player's record (score, other_player_record). Goes through the dealing list at dealing_list and, for each entry, writes a four-byte place (see place_pool_kind3) to the record and moves on four bytes. An entry of 1 to 5 deals a place at random from that group of the pool, one not dealt already; an entry of 0 deals nothing, leaving the four bytes as they were ($FF, no place) -- unless the next entry is 3, when it copies the place before it, 16 to the right and 35 up, so the two pick-ups lie side by side. The list ends with its entry of 4, which is dealt too.
Every place in the pool is free again at the start, so the two players are dealt independently and may be given the same places.
Input
DE The player record's first place (+$1D)
deal_places 6113 LD IY,dealing_list The whole list, nineteen entries
This entry point is used by the routine at planet_secured.
With IY at the list's last entry alone (planet_secured, at the end of a stage), this deals the record's last place again.
