61A9: The dealing list
One byte for each of the nineteen places deal_places deals a player, in the order they go into the record: the kind of the pool group (place_pool_kind3) each is dealt from, or 0 for one dealt from nowhere. load_map_window gives the first eleven places object kind $0C and the last eight kind $1E, and puts the place's number, counted again from 0 at the twelfth, in the slot's +$13.
