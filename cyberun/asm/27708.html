6C3C: The landscape tiles
A word for each of the 171 tiles, $00-$AA: the address of its record in tile_records. Tile $AA is never in the map: load_map_window draws it instead of tile $3E while map_rebuild has bit 7 set.
Many tiles are other tiles turned upside down or mirrored left-right, and their records share bytes: the address of such a tile is one, two or three bytes before the plain tile's, where the markers that say so lie (see tile_records).
