724C: Sound a falling zap
Used by the routines at planet_secured, ship_contact, contact_damage, contact_drain_fuel, contact_extra_life, test_player_shots, update_tough_attacker and draw_rocket.
The commonest effect: called by planet_secured (the bonus count), ship_contact (the shield starting), contact_damage (damage), contact_drain_fuel (fuel), contact_extra_life (an extra life), test_player_shots (a shot hits), update_tough_attacker and draw_rocket. 16 cycles whose half period is (NOT (4 x C)) AND $7F as C counts down from 16: $3F, $43, ... $7B, so the note falls.
The entry at zap_sweep takes the number of cycles in C (sound_death uses 7).
sound_zap 724C LD C,$10 Sixteen cycles
