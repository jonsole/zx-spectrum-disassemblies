725C: Sound a short whine when the ship changes colour
Used by the routine at colour_ship.
Called by colour_ship whenever the ship's colour changes: it works out $47 (white), $46 (yellow, when the ore tank is full: the ore count in ore is 18) or $45 (cyan, with the shield on, flashing in its last $20 passes), and sounds this when that differs from last time (the attribute in the patch at ship_colour_patch, its third byte). Ten cycles of half period $8A down to $81 (rising_sweep). It never ran in the build's sessions, none of which had the shield or a full tank; a recording of a staged shield running out, made for the Sounds page, bears the reading out.
sound_ship_colour 725C LD C,$0A C=10, D=$80
725E LD D,$80
7260 JP rising_sweep
