7275: Sound the tough attacker's hum
Used by the routine at update_tough_attacker.
Called by update_tough_attacker, the update routine of type $19, each pass. Silent on even passes; otherwise four cycles of half period $13 down to $10 (rising_sweep). With bit 6 of the record's +$16 set it would play three cycles of half period $1E to $1C instead, but only on a pass whose count AND 3 is 0 -- and every even pass has returned already, so that branch can never sound. Bit 6 was never set in the build's sessions; eight calls with it set, recorded for the Sounds page, were all silent.
