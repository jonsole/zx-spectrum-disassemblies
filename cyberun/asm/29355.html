72AB: Sound the ship's death
Used by the routine at update_objects.
Called by update_objects each pass once the death byte (death_count) has reached 10, until it reaches $2D: from nine passes after the ship is destroyed. Docking with the finished rocket does not play it: draw_rocket sets the death byte to 10 only for the two calls that put the ship away, and back to 0 in the same call, before update_objects, its only caller, runs in the pass. It plays the warble (warble) with the death byte - 10, then, on every eighth count, a seven-cycle zap (zap_sweep); on the other odd counts, eleven clicks with the death byte halved as the scramble (crackle); on even counts nothing more.
sound_death 72AB LD A,(death_count) Nothing from $2D on
72AE CP $2D
72B0 RET NC
72B1 SUB $0A The warble, note = death byte - 10
72B3 CALL warble
72B6 LD A,(death_count) A multiple of 8: the zap below
72B9 LD B,A
72BA AND $07
72BC JR Z,sound_death_0
72BE BIT 0,B Even: nothing more
72C0 RET Z
72C1 LD A,B Odd: eleven clicks, scramble = death byte / 2
72C2 SRL A
72C4 LD E,A
72C5 LD C,$0B
72C7 JP crackle
sound_death_0 72CA LD C,$07 Seven cycles of the zap
72CC JP zap_sweep
