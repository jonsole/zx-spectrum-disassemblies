72F2: Sound a crackle, chosen by the record
Used by the routine at fire.
Jumped to by fire. 15 clicks with scramble $17 (crackle); or, when bit 7 of the record's +$16 is set, 23 clicks with scramble $20 -- a missile's, which the build's every-add-on session fired.
Input
IX The record
sound_crackle_b 72F2 LD C,$0F 15 clicks, scramble $17...
72F4 LD E,$17
72F6 BIT 7,(IX+$16)
72FA JP Z,crackle
72FD LD C,E ...or 23, scramble $20
72FE LD E,$20
7300 JP crackle
