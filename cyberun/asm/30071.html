7577: Add-on 8: a small bar
Lying on the planet, as the compositor draw_sprite_pair draws a type $0C object; and fitted to the ship: build_ship_sprite builds the ship's picture with it (its mask, if it has one, ANDed on first by addon_mask_and_draw) and the direct drawer draw_direct_pair draws the ship.
The add-ons (addon_graphics, drawn into the ship's sprite by fit_addon) have a byte before them, the height the ship's sprite must have to show the add-on; then width, height and the picture bottom line first; then $FF, or a mask of the same size that addon_mask_and_draw ANDs on before the picture is ORed. 2 bytes by 4 lines, masked.
