75B1: Add-on 9: a small figure
Lying on the planet, as the compositor draw_sprite_pair draws a type $0C object; and fitted to the ship: build_ship_sprite builds the ship's picture with it (its mask, if it has one, ANDed on first by addon_mask_and_draw) and the direct drawer draw_direct_pair draws the ship.
2 bytes by 15 lines (see addon_bar_graphic for the form). There is no $FF after the picture, so fit_addon takes the next 30 bytes as its mask: the six below, then all of add-on 7 (addon_post_graphic: its height byte, size, picture and mask) and add-on 4's height byte. Whether that was meant is not known.
