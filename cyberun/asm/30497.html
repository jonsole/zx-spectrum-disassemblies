7721: The ore in the tank
The ship with the ore tank (add-on 3) fitted and 18 units of ore aboard: build_ship_sprite builds the ship's picture and draws the ore into it (load_ore_3), and the direct drawer draw_direct_pair draws the ship.
Up to 18 rows of 2 bytes, bottom first: load_ore copies one row for each unit of ore collected (the ore count in bare_ship) into bytes 2 and 3 of the rows of the ship's sprite, so the tank fills as ore is picked up. The same bytes begin the mask fit_addon uses for add-on 3 (rocket_tank_graphic).
