8347: Adjust a landscape run's start and width for its end pieces
Used by the routine at fill_behind_moving_piece.
Called by fill_behind_moving_piece (when the byte at piece_rise is 0) just before it draws a run of the landscape. piece_extra_a and piece_extra_b hold the codes of the run's first and last pieces (piece_ends sets them from the map); pieces $90-$93 are ends that need the run moved or widened, depending on the scroll step across (the second byte of scroll_down) and on E, which is 0 or $AA (fill_behind_moving_piece sets DE to 0 or $55AA, the dither's phase). B is added to the run's x and C to its width.
The width's base is the operand of the LD A,n near the end of this routine, which fill_behind_moving_piece writes as its own width less one. The result goes to piece_step_y and HL is left at the run's screen position from saved_speed with x moved.
How the pieces look and why each case needs its adjustment is not yet worked out; a first piece of $93 never came up in the build's sessions.
Input
IX The landscape strip's record (nothing is done when +1 is 0)
E 0 or $AA
Output
HL The run's screen position, x moved by B
A Its width, also in piece_step_y
