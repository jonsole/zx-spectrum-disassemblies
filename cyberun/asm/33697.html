83A1: Move a landscape run's start for its first piece
Used by the routine at landscape_end_pieces.
Part of landscape_end_pieces, for the first piece (piece_extra_a). $90: B=7 when E=0; otherwise B is minus the scroll step across (the second byte of scroll_down) when that is positive, with the run one wider, and 0 when it is not. $91: B=7 unless E=0, when it is minus the scroll step. Other pieces leave B at 0.
L holds piece_extra_a itself here, so the AND 7 near the end always gives 0 for piece $90 and the run is always one wider on that path.

E 0 or $AA

B The shift in x
