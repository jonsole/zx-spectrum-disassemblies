83D6: Show the planet-secured screen, award the bonus, and move on to the next planet
Used by the routine at level_done.
Called by level_done once the rocket has launched. Prints the four lines at planet_secured_lines and the name of the planet just finished (one of five, planet_names; +$12 of the current player's record, score, is its number) into planet_secured_name_text, then the four lines from next_planet_lines with the next planet's name copied into next_planet_name_text -- after the fifth planet the first comes round again.
It then resets the rocket's base position (rocket_x), sets the player's +$1A and +$1B to 6, and counts a bonus of ten times 5000 (BCD, the score adder in test_player_shots) onto the score with a zap (sound_zap) and a pause after each. Last it deals the player's last pick-up place again (the second entry point of deal_places, with IY at the dealing list's last entry and DE at the current player's last place, +$65 of score) and clears the player's own gone bits in the 290 world objects' kinds (mask_world_objects) with a mask worked out from the player in play (player_in_play): $7F for player 1 (bit 7), $BF for player 2 (bit 6).
