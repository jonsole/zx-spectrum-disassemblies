86B9: Read the menu's keys
Used by the routine at menu_loop.
A (row $FD, bit 0) chooses one player, B (row $7F, bit 4) two (player_count = 0 or 1). Keys 1 and 2 (row $F7, bits 0 and 1) step player 1's and player 2's control method on through 0-3 (Keyboard, Kempston, Cursor, Interface II), but only once that player's pointer has reached the line of the method it has, so a held key steps once per arrival rather than once a pass.
The entry at cycle_method steps one player's method: IX the pointer record, B the key's bit.
menu_keys 86B9 LD A,$FD A: one player
86BB OUT ($FD),A
86BD IN A,($FE)
86BF AND $01
86C1 JR NZ,menu_keys_0
86C3 LD (player_count),A
menu_keys_0 86C6 LD A,$7F B: two players
86C8 OUT ($FD),A
86CA IN A,($FE)
86CC BIT 4,A
86CE JR NZ,menu_keys_1
86D0 LD A,$01
86D2 LD (player_count),A
menu_keys_1 86D5 LD IX,player1_pointer Player 1 and key 1
86D9 LD B,$01
86DB CALL cycle_method
86DE LD IX,player2_pointer Player 2 and key 2
86E2 LD B,$02
