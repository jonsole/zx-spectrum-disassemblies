87AF: Count towards the next attacker, and run the meteor-shower timer
Used by the routine at play.
Called by the play loop (play) once a pass. Nothing while the load (object_load) is 8 or more.
The meteor-shower timer (meteor_shower): when it is 0, a shower starts on the passes when the random byte random_a is $6A and the low two bits of its second byte are 0 -- one pass in 1024 -- and lasts 128 passes, during which start_attacker puts rocks and comets near the ship instead of attackers.
Every eighth pass (spawn_countdown at 7) a cloud may start (spawn_cloud); at 0 the count starts again from 8 and the entry at spawn_attacker looks for a free record among the attackers' four (attacker_records) and fills it (start_attacker). update_objects also jumps to spawn_attacker when the load is light.
