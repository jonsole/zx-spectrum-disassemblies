8970: Fire a spray of shots downwards from high up
Used by the routine at fire_from.
Part of fire_from, for frame 7 (kind 6), jumped to from its test of the frame. sna2ctl took these bytes for data, since no session ran them: they call fire_from's random spray, then, if the shooter is above line $67 on the screen, its Y velocity turned round so the spray goes down. Then back to the sound near the end of fire_from.
