89A7: Flags for the eight kinds of shot
One byte for each of kinds 1-8 (fire_from reads the byte before this table plus the kind): bit 7, only from a shooter above line $58 (and, for the frames with no velocity of their own, fire downwards); bit 6, only from line $50 down; low nibble the colour, in both halves of +$0D. Kinds 1 and 2 bright yellow, from low down; 3 bright cyan, from high up; 4 and 5 bright white, from low down; 6 bright cyan; 7 and 8 bright white.
