89AF: Templates for the eight kinds of shot
Four bytes for each of kinds 1-8 (fire_from reads four bytes on per kind from the fifth byte of shot_kind_flags): the gun's mouth as an X and Y offset (put in +7 and +9 for one move by move_object, then replaced), the frame byte (+$13: low nibble the frame of type $1C, high nibble its timing) and the frame range (+$14).
Kind 1 frames 0-2 (the bullet), 2 frame 3 (the refuel pick-up), 3 frame 4, 4 frame 5, 5 frame 6 (the fuel thief), 6 frame 7, 7 and 8 frame 10 (the dash at dash_graphic), kind 7 from 32 pixels to the right of the attacker.
