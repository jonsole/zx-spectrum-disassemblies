8C05: Add an object's acceleration to its velocity, within its top speed
Used by the routines at move_missile, update_cloud, update_small_object, update_tough_attacker, update_attacker and update_rocket_part.
Called by the update routines move_missile, update_cloud, update_small_object, update_tough_attacker and update_attacker. Velocity (+7 +8 in X, +9 +A in Y) and acceleration (+B +C, +E +F) are signed 8.8 numbers, high byte first. The top speed is (+$10 AND 7) + 1 whole pixels a pass; a velocity that goes past it is set to exactly that (limit_speed).
