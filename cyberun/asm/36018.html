8CB2: Break the ship up
Used by the routine at update_objects.
Called by update_objects when the death byte (death_count) reaches 10. Fills all nine object records (object_records): the first four as debris (type $15, made by the second entry point of check_shot) 28 pixels to the right of the ship and above it by half the ship sprite's height (ship_height), +$15 0, $40, $80 and $C0 so that each piece takes a different part of the debris path; the other five as type $1C bullets (frame 0) at the same place (+$14 = 2, frames 0-2, which also marks them as pieces of the wreck), thrown out with the velocities at wreck_velocities and pulled down by gravity (Y acceleration $0020).
