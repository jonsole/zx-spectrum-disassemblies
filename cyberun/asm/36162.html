8D42: Update the nine object records, once a pass
Used by the routine at play.
Called from the play loop (play) once a pass, and the most expensive thing in it: for each of the nine records at object_records that is not free it rubs out last pass's picture, runs the type's update routine through run_sky_object_handler (which moves, animates and redraws it), tests it against the ship (ship_contact) and against the player's shots (test_player_shots). It also keeps the death byte (death_count) counting, and at the end starts a new cloud or attacker when the load (object_load) is light.
The load is the throttle on spawning: one for every record in use, one more for each that was on the screen last pass, one more for a big cloud, and half the number of rocket parts delivered (rocket_parts_load), since draw_rocket draws those in the same pass. Below 4 this routine spawns; spawn_tick stops at 8 and find_free_record at 7.
