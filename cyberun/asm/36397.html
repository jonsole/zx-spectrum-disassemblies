8E2D: Test the current object against the ship and apply its contact effect
Used by the routine at update_objects.
Called by update_objects after each object's update. Nothing happens while the rocket is launching (launch_state), while the ship is dying (death_count), when the object is harmless (contact effect 0, object_load), when it was not drawn this pass, or when it is exploding. Its box -- the screen x and bottom line it was drawn at (+$11, +$12), the width (sprite_width_drawn) and height (in old_sprite_x) it was drawn with -- goes to collision_box, and an object whose x is outside $28-$8D is too far from the ship to touch it. The ship's box is fixed in x: 40 pixels wide from x=100, 16 lines high, its bottom 8 lines below the ship's line (ship_line). boxes_overlap tests the two.
On a touch the effect bits are acted on from bit 7 down (see object_load), IX the object and IY the ship's record (in bare_ship). $FF is different: it starts the shield (shield_timer = $FF, counted down every other pass by ship_pass, so about 25 seconds), and frees the object with a sound.
Without a touch, a cloud may still touch what the ship is carrying (carried_part keeps a flag in the player record, score, while it carries something): the carried thing's box (from carried_part_facing) is tested, and on a touch the ship's X velocity becomes minus half the cloud's, and fuel_saver is set. That branch never ran in the build's sessions; it rests on reading alone.
