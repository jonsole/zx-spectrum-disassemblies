8F1E: Contact effect bit 7: halve the ship's velocity
Used by the routine at ship_contact.
A cloud's effect ($80). Halves each component of the ship's velocity (the words at +$19 and +$1B of its record) that has the same sign as the object's velocity in that axis, so flying through a cloud is slow. Not done in a pass after the carried thing was pushed by a cloud (last_pass_contact).
