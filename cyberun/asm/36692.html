8F54: Contact effect bit 6: damage the ship
Used by the routine at ship_contact.
The attackers' effect. An object damages the ship at most once every 16 passes: it sets the cool-down in the high nibble of its +$17 to 15 (update_objects counts it down) and does nothing while it is not zero. With the shield up (shield_timer) an ordinary attacker (effect $50 or $51) is destroyed instead, with a sound.
damage_ship (the entry point at the middle of this routine), reached also from a beam (beam_hits_ship) and from ship_hits_ground_objects, does nothing with the shield up or while the ship is already dying. Otherwise it counts the hit (the last byte of ship_icon_positions) and recolours the ship icon on the panel: yellow ($46) after the first hit, magenta ($43) after the second, and black after the third, which sets the death byte (death_count) to 1. It is also the repair: a refuel pad (ship_hits_ground_objects) sets the count to $FF first, so it wraps to 0 and the icon goes back to white ($47). The icon is a 4 by 2 character patch (ship_icon_positions) at x=$30 for player 1 and x=$B0 for player 2 (the player number is in player_in_play), painted by fill_attributes.
