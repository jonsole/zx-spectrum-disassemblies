8FC4: Contact effect bit 5: refuel
Used by the routine at ship_contact.
The refuel pick-up's effect ($28 with bit 3: refuel and collect). Adds -$1C00 to the fuel word through add_fuel, which clamps it at full: the gauge's high byte runs from $42 (full) to $BE (empty), so a pick-up is worth 28 of its 124 steps.
contact_refuel 8FC4 LD DE,$E400
8FC7 JP add_fuel
