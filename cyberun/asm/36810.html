8FCA: Contact effect bit 4: bounce the object off the ship
Used by the routine at ship_contact.
Part of the default effect. For each axis the object's velocity is halved and negated, so it bounces away at half speed, and the same amount is added to the ship's velocity word in that axis (+$19, +$1B of its record).
