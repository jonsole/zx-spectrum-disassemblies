9035: Score a shot object and turn it into debris
Used by the routines at update_rammer and update_attacker.
Used by the attackers' update routines (update_attacker, update_rammer). If the object has been shot (bit 5 of +$10) it is scored (score_object, in test_player_shots) and becomes debris: type $15 (update_debris), frame count $50, frames 0-5, and the path step at the start of a random quarter of the path's mirror images (bits 6-7 of +$15). The zero flag tells the caller which.
make_debris is also how the ship's wreck starts (wreck_ship), which sets the four quarters itself.
Input
IX The object
Output
F Z if it had not been shot
check_shot 9035 BIT 5,(IX+$10) Not shot
9039 RET Z
903A CALL score_object Score it
This entry point is used by the routine at wreck_ship.
