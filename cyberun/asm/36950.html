9056: Test an object against the landscape, a little down and to the left
Used by the routines at move_bouncing, move_or_explode and probe_shot.
Moves the object 4 pixels left and 4 down (by setting its velocity to that, moving it with move_object and putting the velocity back), tests it against the landscape with collide_with_landscape, and moves it back. The 4-pixel offset lines the object's box up with its sprite. The mode collide_with_landscape tests in (piece_step_x) is the caller's: see move_bouncing and move_or_explode; the result is in scratch_count.
nudge_object moves the object by A pixels down and -A across, keeping its velocity, with the velocity saved in the four bytes at current_piece while it does.
