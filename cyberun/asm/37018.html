909A: Move an object that bounces off the landscape
Used by the routines at move_missile and update_attacker.
Used by the attackers, the debris and the homing missiles. Tests the landscape in mode 0 (piece_step_x) and then moves the object by its velocity (move_object). In mode 0 place_collision_box negates the object's velocity before the test, and this routine negates it again after; how a touch turns into a bounce is collide_with_landscape's business and not yet worked out here.
Input
BC The box's height and width in pixels
move_bouncing 909A XOR A Test the landscape in mode 0
909B LD (piece_step_x),A
909E CALL test_landscape_at
90A1 CALL negate_velocity Negate the velocity again, and move
90A4 JP move_object
