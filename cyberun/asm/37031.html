90A7: Move an object that explodes on the landscape
Used by the routines at update_small_object and update_rammer.
Used by the small objects (update_small_object) and type $1D (update_rammer). Tests the landscape in mode $80 (piece_step_x); on a touch (scratch_count) the object starts exploding (bit 3 of +$17), stops, and makes a sound (sound_tick). Then it is moved by its velocity (move_object).
