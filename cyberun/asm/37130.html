910A: Step an object's animation
Used by the routines at update_small_object, update_rammer, update_attacker and update_ground_objects.
The frame byte +$13: bits 0-3 the frame, bits 4-5 a count, bits 6-7 the count's reload. Each call counts bits 4-5 down; when they read 1 the frame steps on, from the last frame (low nibble of +$14) back to the first (high nibble of +$14), and the count is reloaded from bits 6-7. So a type's animation runs at one frame every one to three passes over any run of its frame list. (A count of 0 sets bits 4-7 all, and the animation goes on at the slowest rate.)
