9143: Make the current object's box for the shot tests
Used by the routines at update_objects and beam_hits_ground_object.
The box at collision_box from where the object was drawn this pass (current_object): x, bottom line, width and height. A sprite straddling the left edge of the view (a flag in old_sprite_width) has its x moved in and its width cut, and one running off the right is cut at the right edge ($EE). row_count gets its top line. Nothing is done when it was not drawn (height 0 in old_sprite_x); the height left in D tells test_player_shots the same.
