917C: Test the current object against the player's four shots
Used by the routine at update_objects.
The object's box is at collision_box (object_box). The shots are four records of 24 bytes at shot_records (updated by update_shots). Two kinds: a homing missile (bit 7 of its +$16; update_missile) is a box 8 wide and 5 high at its drawn position (+$11, +$12), tested only while drawn. The other kind, the laser, is a line: x in its +$0B (0 when there is none), line in +$0C, length in characters in +$0E; when bit 7 of its +$15 is set it is 8 lines high and the line test is skipped. The test is boxes_overlap.
A hit marks the shot (bit 7 of +$10; a missile is also freed) and the object shot (bit 5 of +$10), which its update routine turns into a score and debris (check_shot). A missile hitting type $19 counts a hit on it directly (+$16) and scores it. A shot with bit 7 of +$15 set destroys any object outright instead (bit 3 of +$17), scoring it double; type $19 gives its points that way only when it is already worn down (+$D = $FF), and then also credits ore to the part being carried (the second entry point of load_ore). Only the first shot that hits counts.
score_object adds an object's points to the score: 250 for types $0D and $1B, otherwise from type_points, doubled for one destroyed outright. add_score adds BC (BCD, B the high pair) to the current player's score (the first three bytes of the player record, score, BCD, low first), and prints it on the panel with print_string: the digits are font codes $1A-$23 (font), written into the panel's text (p1_score_digits, the record in front).
Input
