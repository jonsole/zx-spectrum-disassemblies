92B4: Pick a target for a homing missile
Used by the routine at update_missile.
Called by update_missile when the missile has no target. The type is first tested for a free record by INC A, so the SUB $14 after it leaves the type less $13, the same index as type_points's. Goes through the first seven records at object_records and picks, of those drawn this pass, the one whose type is worth most in missile_target_values, putting its record number in the high nibble of the missile's +$17 (the low nibble is cleared with it). None found: +$17 stays $F0. The best value so far is kept in scratch_count.
