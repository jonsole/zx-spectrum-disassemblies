9307: Update a homing missile
Used by the routine at update_shots.
Called by update_shots for each of the player's shots that is a homing missile (bit 7 of +$16) and is flying (first byte 0; $FF when spent). With no target (the high nibble of +$17 is $F) it looks for one (the code at offset 24 of type_points). With one it steers at it (steer_towards), and gives it up when the target's record is freed. Then it moves (move_missile).
