9341: Accelerate an object towards another
Used by the routines at update_missile, home_on_ship and turn_at_ship.
Sets the acceleration (+$0B, +$0E) to $C0 (three quarters of a pixel a pass, each pass) towards the target in each axis. In X: the same page column decides by the pixel; otherwise the target is taken to be to the left when the object is in the column after the view's (kept in start_filled), and to the right otherwise. In Y, by the whole position.

IX The object
IY The target
