938D: Move, draw and time out a homing missile
Used by the routine at update_missile.
Rubs out its old picture, accelerates it (accelerate), faces it (set_facing), counts its life down in +$15 (freed at 0, or when marked by a hit), moves it bouncing off the landscape in a 16 by 11 box (move_bouncing), and draws it.
One odd case: when the landscape piece under the missile is $3E (looked up in the four in map_window_pointer by which quarter of the view it is in) and its line is between $18 and $D7, its line is set to $6A. Not yet worked out.
