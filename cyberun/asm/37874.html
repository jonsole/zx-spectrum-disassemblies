93F2: Update routine for type $13, the clouds
A cloud slows the ship (contact effect $80, contact_slow_ship), cannot be shot (its +$10 has bit 5 set from the start, spawn_tick), and explodes when it drifts below the bottom of page row 10 or into row 3. Clouds made high up (top speed 4) do not drift in Y. White clouds (+$D = $77) drop things on the ship (attacker_fires, kind 3). The big one (frame 1, one in eight) counts twice in the load. It moves bouncing off the landscape in a 48 by 32 box. Identified as clouds by their place in the sky, their colours and their harmlessness, not by name.
