9457: Update routine for type $1C, the bullets, pick-ups and wreck pieces
The small things: what the attackers drop (attacker_fires), the pick-ups, and the five pieces of the ship's wreck (wreck_ship). The frame picks the contact effect (small_object_effects). A wreck piece (+$14 = 2) accelerates (accelerate); the others fly straight. The ship icon and the ball (frames 8 and 9) keep their frame; the rest animate. Each lives as many passes as +$15 says, then explodes, and explodes at once on touching the landscape (move_or_explode).
