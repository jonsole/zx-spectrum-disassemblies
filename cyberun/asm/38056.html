From ten hits it flashes white (+$D = $FF instead of $55), and at fourteen it explodes, scored. It has an engine sound (sound_hum), drops things on the ship (attacker_fires, kind 7 or 8 by its facing), changes course now and then (change_course, steer_tough_attacker), faces the way it moves, and moves bouncing in a 48 by 24 box.
