9507: Update routine for types $14, $16, $17, $18 and $1A, the attackers
A shot attacker is scored and turned to debris (check_shot). Otherwise it changes course by its type (change_course) and accelerates (accelerate). Then, as for most types: animate (animate_object), move bouncing off the landscape in a 34 by 35 box (move_bouncing), place it (place_object), and draw it (draw_object_images) with its colour (colour_sprite). The other update routines join this tail at its entry points.
