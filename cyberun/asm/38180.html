9524: Update routine for type $1E, a rocket part falling into place
Reached through the type table at object_handlers (its last word). A part the ship has let go of (drop_part) falls under its own acceleration, harmless to touch apart from a push (effect $10), and does not test the landscape. It stops when it reaches its place -- page row +$15 and the line in +$16, set by drop_part -- and is freed: as soon as it is in that row with its line at or below the slot's, so a part let go no higher than its slot is freed on its first pass without falling (run in SkoolKit's simulator for the Animations page); the parts delivered (offset $1B of the player record, score) go up by one and the player scores 1500. Then it is drawn (draw_object, the tail of update_attacker).
