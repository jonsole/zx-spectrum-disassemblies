96B0: The rocket parts' table
Eight entries of five bytes, one a part, in the order they are delivered: the flags and drawn x and line draw_rocket keeps between passes (copied to and from +$10 to +$12 of rocket_record), then the part's offset from the base (rocket_x, rocket_y), X and Y, as signed pixels. The parts stack up: offsets in Y 0, -9, -18, -34, -30 (part 4, 14 left), -30 (part 5, 22 right), -47, -63. drop_part reads the Y offset to say where a falling part stops. Bit 7 of part 0's flags (drawn) tells draw_rocket when the launched rocket has left the screen.
