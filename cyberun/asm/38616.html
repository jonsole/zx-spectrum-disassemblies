96D8: Update routine for type $15, debris
Debris -- what is left of a shot attacker (check_shot) and four pieces of the ship's wreck (wreck_ship) -- is harmless, makes the debris sound (sound_debris), and flies a fixed path: the velocity is taken each pass from debris_path, the step in +$15 (bits 0-5) going on by two. Bit 7 of +$15 mirrors the path in X and bit 6 in Y, so four pieces of the wreck flying the four mirror images make a burst. At the path's end ($80) it is freed. It animates, and bounces off the landscape (the tail of update_attacker).
