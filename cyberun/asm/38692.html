9724: Let a carried rocket part go over the rocket's place
Used by the routine at draw_ship.
Called by draw_ship. The part the ship carries is kept at offset $1A of the player record (score; $FF none). When the ship is over the rocket's place (page column $52 pixel $5C) in page row 10 or above, the part is let go: a record is found for it (find_record_for_part) and filled as type $1E (update_rocket_part) at column $52 pixel $68 and the ship's height, falling with a Y velocity made from the ship's (its Y velocity word in bare_ship, negated) and a slight downward acceleration ($1B), to stop at row 10 at line $73 plus the part's Y offset (rocket_part_table). Part 6 is let go only when full -- when the ore loaded into it (a count in bare_ship, filled by load_ore) is 18 -- and falls with frame 8; its load and a byte of the player record are cleared. Then the ship carries nothing.
It all ran in the build's sessions (the deliveries of part 0 and of the ore tank).
