97DF: Change an attacker's course now and then
Used by the routines at update_tough_attacker and update_attacker.
Counts +$15 down and, at 0, sets it again to 1-32 passes and jumps to the type's steering routine through the table steering_routines. Once bit 7 of +$16 is set (home_on_ship), there is no wait: the steering runs every pass. The routines: $14 and $16 home in on the ship (home_on_ship), $17 turns at it every 10-25 passes (turn_at_ship), $18 and $1A drift diagonally at random (drift_at_random), and $19 turns at it or stops (steer_tough_attacker).
