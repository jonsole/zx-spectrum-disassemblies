9894: The frame lists of types $15 to $1A
The frame lists, one a type, which the table at offset 24 of object_handlers points into: a word for each frame, the address of its graphic (a byte of width, a byte of height in lines, then the bitmap, bottom row first; no mask). The lists overlap: type $15 starts at the first word (frames 0-5), $16 at the seventh, $17 at the eleventh, $18 at the thirteenth, $19 at the seventeenth, $1A at the nineteenth; the list of type $1A runs on into the clouds' (cloud_frames).
