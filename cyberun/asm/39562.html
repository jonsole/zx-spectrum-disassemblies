9A8A: AND every byte of the objects-in-the-world table with E
Used by the routines at planet_secured and new_game.
map_object_kinds holds one byte for each object lying in the world, in map-column order: bits 0-5 its kind, bit 7 set once it is gone for player 1 and bit 6 for player 2 (ship_hits_ground_objects, beam_hits_ground_object). A new game passes $3F (new_game), clearing bits 6 and 7 of each, so everything is back. planet_secured passes $7F for player 1 or $BF for player 2 at the end of a planet, bringing back only that player's objects.
