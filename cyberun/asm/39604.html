9AB4: Play one note on the beeper
Used by the routines at play_tune_until_key and play_tune.
A note is a byte: bits 0-5 the note (0 a rest), bits 6-7 its length less one, in units. A note's entry in note_table gives the half-period as a delay count and the number of cycles in one unit; the beeper is toggled between $00 and $10 (black border throughout) for length times that many cycles. A rest waits about 0.13 seconds a unit.
Input
A The note
DE The tune, at the note
Output
