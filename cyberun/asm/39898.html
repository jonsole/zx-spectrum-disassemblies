9BDA: Draw the flickering beams, and test them against the ship
Used by the routine at scroll_landscape.
Called by scroll_landscape, not while map_rebuild is set. A beam crosses a screen whose landscape piece is $A6, at a fixed height in it, unless the thing that makes it has been shot: a bit in the player's record, found by map_square_bit and tested by a BIT instruction this routine writes into itself (the third after the CALL to map_square_bit). Each pass the old beams are rubbed out (erase_flicker_beam) and drawn again in the four screens around the view (the four pieces in map_window_pointer, in the order 0, 2, 1, 3; bit 0 the page row below, bit 1 the column to the right). A beam is a one-line row of bytes ORed onto the screen in the current pattern, rotated to follow the fine scroll, its characters coloured (fill_attributes) and its extent kept in flicker_beam_records for rubbing out (draw_flicker_beam draws it, place_flicker_beam places it).
The pattern changes when its timer runs out: half the time one of the eight at flicker_beam_patterns in a random bright colour, for 1-32 passes; otherwise none, for 1-16. A beam that touches the ship's box (40 by 16) damages it (beam_hits_ship), then not again for 16 passes.
All of it, the pattern changes included, ran in the build's sessions.
