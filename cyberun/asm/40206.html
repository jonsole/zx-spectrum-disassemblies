9D0E: Test a beam against the ship
Used by the routine at draw_beams.
Not during the launch. The beam, D pixels wide and one line high at HL, is tested against the ship's box at collision_box (test_collision_box); a touch starts 16 passes of grace (beam_grace) and damages the ship (contact_damage).
