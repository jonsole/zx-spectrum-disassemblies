9D43: Where the beams were drawn
Four records of four bytes, one for each page around the view, in draw_beams's order (the view's page, the one to the right, below, below right): the screen address (low byte first), the length in bytes and the pattern of the beam drawn there (draw_flicker_beam), for rubbing it out next pass (erase_flicker_beam). A zero first byte means none.
