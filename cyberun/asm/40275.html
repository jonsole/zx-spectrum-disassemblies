9D53: Work out where a beam lies on the screen
Used by the routine at draw_beams.
Called by draw_beams. From the view's pixel position in its page (HL, from view_x), the origins (DE, from origin_x) and the page being drawn (draw_beams's quarter, in row_count: bit 0 the page below, bit 1 the page to the right), it works out the beam's x and line, kept in moved_x, and its length in bytes in B (0: not in view). The beam's pattern (from beam_state) is rotated right by the fine scroll (origin_x AND 7) into C, and HL becomes the beam's screen address (pixel_to_screen). A beam lies at page y $21, 9 lines below the top of its tile-$A6 cell.
