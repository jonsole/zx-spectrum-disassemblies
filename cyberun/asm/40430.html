9DEE: Test an object against a sloping landscape edge
Used by the routines at test_landscape_piece, test_slope_swapped and test_slope_upside_down.
Used by the landscape tests (test_landscape_piece and those after it). IX is a landscape piece's collision record: +0 and +2 its x range, +1 and +3 its bottom and top lines, +4 flags (bits 0-2 the steepness, bit 7 which way up). The object's box: x in L, bottom line in H, width D, height E (test_landscape_piece). When the line ranges meet, works out how far the object reaches into the slope at that line: B is the depth of that line under the slope's high edge, scaled by the steepness (2 as it is, 3 halved, others doubled). scratch_count is 1 if the object is in the piece's x range.
Input
IX The piece's collision record
Output
B The slope's offset at the object's line
