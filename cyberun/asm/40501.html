9E35: Test an object against one landscape piece
Used by the routine at hit_slope.
Called by hit_slope for each of a landscape piece's collision records. The object's box is in box_x-box_width, its x, bottom line, height and width (made by place_collision_box). Bits 6-7 of the record's flags pick one of four slope shapes -- the same slope mirrored left to right and upside down (test_slope_reversed, test_slope_upside_down, test_slope_both_ways); which way each faces is not yet worked out. A touch leaves scratch_count 1 and the depth into the slope in touch_depth.
All four shapes ran in the build's sessions; the RET after the four tests cannot be reached.
