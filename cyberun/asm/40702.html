9EFE: Dissolve the play area to black
Used by the routine at dissolve_and_clear.
The play area is 28 bytes by 160 lines from x=8 at its bottom line $B7, walked from the bottom up (line_up_hl). Sixteen times over, every byte is ANDed with the next of the nine masks at dissolve_masks, each clearing one bit (one clears none), the masks running on from byte to byte and line to line, so the picture loses its pixels in a scattered pattern rather than line by line.
