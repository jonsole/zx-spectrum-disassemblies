9F4E: Pause while SPACE or CAPS SHIFT is pressed
Used by the routine at play.
Called once a pass from the play loop (play). Pressing SPACE or CAPS SHIFT pauses the game: it waits for the key to be let go, then for a second press, then for that to be let go too, and play goes on.
