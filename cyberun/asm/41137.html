A0B1: Print one character
Used by the routine at print_string.
For print_string, which prints strings. The character's seven bytes are at font plus seven times its code. They are copied up the screen from the bottom row (print_char_rows), or, for a double-size string, drawn twice as wide and tall by print_char_double: the JR here is the switch, its offset written by print_string ($00 or $10). In the snapshot the offset is $FE, a jump to itself, but print_string always writes it first.
