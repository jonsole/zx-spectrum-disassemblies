A149: Turn pixel coordinates into a screen address
Used by the routines at place_flicker_beam, dissolve_play_area, clear_play_area, print_string, set_direct_new, record_screen_address, draw_sprite_pair, copy_box_in, load_ore, toggle_carried_part, draw_carried_piece, draw_ship_image, draw_trail, side_jet, lift_jet, draw_bitmap, update_fuel_gauge, step_screen_address, fill_behind_moving_piece and fly_in_step.
The standard Spectrum screen-address calculation from a pixel position: L is x (0-255, its low three bits dropped) and H is y (0-191 from the top). Everything in the game that draws on the screen uses this.
Input
L x in pixels
H y in pixels
Output
HL The screen byte containing the pixel
