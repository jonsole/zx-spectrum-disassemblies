A18D: Run a sky object's update routine
Used by the routine at update_objects.
Jumps to the update routine for the object in record IX, chosen by its graphic number (+0), which for the sky objects runs from $13 to $1E: the first twelve words of run_sky_object_handler's table object_handlers are their handlers in that order. Called once for each live record of the nine sky-object records at object_records every pass (update_objects), between the setting up of its erase (prepare_erase) and the drawing (prepare_draw and draw_object_images, which the handlers end with).

IX The object's record
