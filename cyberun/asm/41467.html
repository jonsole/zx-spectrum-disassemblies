A1FB: Clip a sprite's rows to the play area
Used by the routines at new_sprite_header and old_sprite_header.
Sprites are drawn upwards from their bottom row, inside the lines $18-$B7 (24 to 183): below the panel at the top and above the bottom row of the screen. A sprite reaching above line $18 has its height cut so that its top row is $18; one reaching below line $B7 has its bottom rows skipped (the graphic is stored bottom row first, so DE moves on by width times the rows cut) and its y put at $B7. A sprite wholly outside is not caught here; place_on_screen decides what is visible.
Input
A y of the bottom row
B Height in rows
H Width in bytes
DE The graphic's rows
Output
L The y to draw from
B The rows to draw
clip_sprite_rows A1FB LD L,A Does the top row reach above line $18?
A1FC SUB $17
A1FE SUB B
A1FF JR NC,clip_sprite_rows_0
A201 LD A,L Yes: draw only the rows from y up to line $18
A202 SUB $17
A204 LD B,A
A205 RET
clip_sprite_rows_0 A206 LD A,L Does the bottom row lie below line $B7? Return if not
A207 SUB $B7
A209 RET Z
A20A RET C

A20C LD A,B
