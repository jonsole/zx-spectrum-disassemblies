A25D: The shift tables' page for the old image
Used by the routine at set_old_sprite.
B becomes $F8 plus x AND 6: $FA, $FC or $FE for an image at 2, 4 or 6 pixels past a byte, the pages of the shift tables shift_tables that build_shift_tables builds (on the first page of each pair a byte shifted right that many bits, on the page after it the bits that fall into the next byte). $F8 for a byte-aligned image is never used as a table. The second instruction is also an entry, used by set_new_sprite: the same for the x in A.
