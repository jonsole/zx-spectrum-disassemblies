A266: Set up the new image for the direct drawer
Used by the routine at draw_trail.
For draw_direct_pair, the drawer that works straight on the screen: the new image's bottom line (direct_new_line, from the sprite position word in moved_x), its height (ground_cell) and width (sprite_width_drawn), and the screen address of its bottom-left byte.
Input
B Height
H Width
Output
HL Screen address
