A2FA: The compositor's left-clip flag
Not zero while the pair is drawn straddling the left edge of the play area: set by place_on_screen for the new image and by prepare_erase (from +$10 bit 6) for the old, cleared by clear_sprite_pair. draw_sprite_pair then works with both x positions $60 less, and copies only the part of the box right of the edge. object_box and colour_sprite read it too.
sprite_left_clip A2FA DEFB $00
