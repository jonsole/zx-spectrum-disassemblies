A3F6: Copy the box from the screen into the buffer
Used by the routine at draw_sprite_pair.
The rows are copied bottom-up into the buffer at page $F8. The loop is three pieces: PUSH HL keeps the row's screen address; a DJNZ whose displacement is patched to land in the LDI chain of copy_in_row as many LDIs from its end as the box is bytes wide (7 less the width, times two, plus three to clear the JP); and the chain's end, which pops the address, moves it up a line and jumps back to the PUSH. When B runs out the DJNZ falls through to the JP instead, with one address left on the stack for erase_old_draw_new to drop. C is $FF so that the LDIs' count of BC never reaches B.
