A43E: Rub out the old image in the buffer, then draw the new one
Used by the routines at draw_sprite_pair and copy_box_in.
The middle of draw_sprite_pair. The register sets are swapped so that the main set holds the old image's graphic and shift page (saved by the EXX after prepare_erase) while the new image's wait in the other set. Each row routine finishes by adding the patched step to L (the box's width less the bytes the routine covered), counting the rows in A', and either jumping back through IY (or IX) for the next row or on to the next stage.
