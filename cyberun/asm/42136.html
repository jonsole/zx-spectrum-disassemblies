A498: Break up a dissolving object's image
Used by the routines at draw_aligned_row and draw_row_shifted.
An object that has been destroyed dissolves over seven passes (prepare_draw): its new image, just drawn into the buffer, is ANDed with that step's mask from dissolve_mask_bits, rotated a bit to the right at every byte so the gaps do not line up in columns. The masks keep seven bits, then six, down to one, so the object crumbles away.
