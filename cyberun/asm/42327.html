A557: Copy a row of the box from the buffer to the screen
Reached from copy_box_out by the DJNZ there, whose displacement copy_box_in patches so that it lands on the LDI that copies just the box's width: one LDI per byte, up to seven. Then the row's screen address comes back off the stack, moves up a pixel line (crossing a character row or a third of the screen as needed), and the loop goes round again at copy_box_out's DJNZ.
