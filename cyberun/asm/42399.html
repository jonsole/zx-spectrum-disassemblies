A59F: Rub out the old image and draw the new one, straight on the screen
Used by the routines at draw_carried_piece, draw_ship_image, draw_trail, side_jet and lift_jet.
The other sprite drawer, for the player's ship (draw_ship), the carried rocket part (draw_carried_piece) and the lift jet (lift_jet): no buffer, but the rows of the old image's erase and the new image's draw interleaved -- one row rubbed out, one drawn, both moving up the screen -- so no part of the ship is missing for long. The images are byte-aligned (no shift tables); a ship facing left is drawn right to left through the bit-reversing table on page $F9 (direct_erase_row_mirrored, direct_draw_row_mirrored). Erase is AND NOT the graphic, draw is OR, as in draw_sprite_pair.
The old image is in the alternate register set (HL' its screen address, DE' its graphic, IY its row routine, its rows in scratch_count), the new in the main set (HL, DE, IX, its rows in ground_cell). When their bottom lines (direct_old_line, direct_new_line) differ, the rows below the higher one's bottom are done first on their own, so that the two then move up in step.
Input
HL The new image's screen address
DE The new image's graphic
IX The new image's draw-row routine
HL' The old image's screen address
DE' The old image's graphic
