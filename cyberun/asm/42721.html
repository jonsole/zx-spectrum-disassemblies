A6E1: Rub a shifted row out of the buffer
One row of a shifted old image: each graphic byte is looked up in the shift tables (B their page, C the byte), the part that stays in this byte at B and the part that falls into the next at B+1, each complemented and ANDed in. So a row of n graphic bytes covers n+1 bytes. About 79 T-states a graphic byte.
