A802: Wait
Used by the routines at planet_secured, menu_loop, pause_delay, game, game_over, level_done and swap_players.
A delay loop: D times 255 turns of DEC E / JR NZ, about 4,100 T-states for each unit of D (D=0 counts 256).
Input
D How long
delay A802 LD E,$FF
delay_0 A804 DEC E
A805 JR NZ,delay_0
A807 DEC D
A808 JR NZ,delay
A80A RET
