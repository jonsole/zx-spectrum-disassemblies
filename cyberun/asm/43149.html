A88D: The second pick-up record
As pickup_record. Its 26 bytes run two past this block: its +$18 and +$19 are the first two bytes of the routine load_ore. Those two fields are read only for records without bit 7 of +3, so for a pick-up they are never looked at. collect_pickup empties it (bit 4 of +$10) when it takes the first piece of a part let go.
