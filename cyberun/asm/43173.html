A8A5: Load ore into the carried ore tank
Used by the routine at draw_shot.
Only while the rocket part the ship carries (+$1A of the player's record score) is part 6, the ore tank: adds one or two units (one for an ore kind 8 or 9, two for kind $0A, from bit 1 of A) to the ore aboard (the byte after the ship record in bare_ship), at most 18 -- a full tank, which drop_part waits for before letting the part go; clears the new rows on the screen, two bytes at x=$70 above the old level from the ship's bottom line; and redraws the level in the ship's picture (the entry at the eighth instruction from the end of the clearing): the ore's rows, bottom up, from the 2-byte-wide graphic after rocket_tank_graphic into bytes 2 and 3 of each row of ship_picture.
Called with the special beam's record by draw_shot, which carries the ore kind in +$13 (beam_hits_ground_object), and with A by test_player_shots at the second instruction. Only the redraw ran in the build's sessions (from build_ship_sprite and start_landscape).
A bug, read only: the clearing loop moves DE up a line (line_up_de) but writes through HL, so it clears the same two bytes every time.

IX A record whose +$13 not zero lets it run
