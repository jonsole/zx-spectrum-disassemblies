A8F4: Test the ship against the ground objects
Used by the routine at ship_pass.
Once a pass (ship_pass). The ship's box is 40 pixels wide from x=$64 and as high as its picture, at its y, both less this pass's scroll steps (scroll_down: across, up and down). Each of the first four ground-object records that is on the screen, between line $28 and the bottom and between x=$30 and $A0, and not of kind $0B, is boxed by its last drawn position and its graphic's size, and tested (boxes_overlap). At the first one that touches:
A refuel pad -- graphic $1B in frame 4 or 5, the fuel station's two frames (map kind $0E, object_part_pairs) -- fills the fuel (fuel), is used up (freed next pass and marked gone in map_object_kinds), and repairs the ship: the hit count (hit_count) is set to $FF before calling the damage handler in contact_damage, whose count then wraps to 0 and colours the ship white again. This branch never ran in the build's sessions.
Anything else is solid: the collision flag trail_collision is set (so draw_ship redraws the ship's trail), and the ship bounces -- across (bounce_across) if the object lies beyond either side of the ship, up or down (bounce_up_down) if the ship's top is level with or below the object's bottom, and again up or down if, while the vertical scroll step is not positive, the ship's bottom is within nine lines below the object's top.
