AA37: Hang a rocket part under the ship, or take it off
Used by the routines at draw_ship_image and start_landscape.
The ship's picture is built in RAM at ship_picture, six bytes a row, bottom row first, and a rocket part the ship carries hangs in its bottom rows. This XORs the part's graphic (from type $1E's frame list rocket_part_frames, by the part number in bits 0-3 of the ship record's +$1F) into the picture, shifted four pixels right through the shift tables' fourth and fifth pages (shift_tables) and one byte in -- so the same call puts it in and takes it out -- and clears the same place on the screen (four bytes from a byte in, the part's height up from the ship's bottom line), presumably because the direct drawer rubs the ship out with its picture as it now is, which would leave the old part's pixels behind. Called from draw_ship when bit 7 of +$1F asks for it.
Then (also the entry at the 24th instruction, used by build_ship_sprite when it rebuilds the picture) the ship's height and the picture's start are set for whether a part hangs there (bit 6 of +$1F): five rows more and the picture from ship_picture, or five fewer and from 30 bytes into it; the ship's line for shots (ship_line) moves by three, and the ship record's screen y and position are moved to match (move_object, ship_speed_from_scroll).
