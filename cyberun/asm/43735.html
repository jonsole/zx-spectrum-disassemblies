AAD7: Pick up a pick-up the ship touches
Used by the routine at update_ground_objects.
For each pick-up record (bit 7 of +3) every pass (update_ground_objects). A pick-up is taken when it was on the screen last pass, between line $28 and the bottom and between x=$30 and $90, and its box touches a narrow box under the middle of the ship: 16 pixels from x=$70, the ship's height less ten. Then a sound (sound_pick_up), 1000 added to the score (the score adder near the end of test_player_shots), the record is emptied (bit 4 of +$10, so its image is rubbed out this pass), and:
A rocket part (graphic $1E) is taken only when add-on 1 is fitted (+7 of the player's record score) and it is the part due next (its kind against +$1B, the parts delivered); its colours become $FA. It is hung under the ship: the ship record's +$1F gets the part number with bits 6 and 7 set (draw_ship then calls toggle_carried_part), +$1A of the player's record the part carried, and the part's 4-byte entry from +$49 of the player's record is marked taken.
Any other pick-up is an add-on: +$1F gets its kind plus one, which draw_ship fits (build_ship_sprite), and its entry from +$1D of the player's record is marked. Kinds 9 and $0A are the slab and the spire (add-ons 10 and 11), the load carried_part puts down: kind $0A, the spire, is never taken; kind 9, the slab, only with add-on 9 fitted (+$0F), and taking it clears +$1F and carried_part_facing, sets +$10 of the player's record (player_count_nine) to 9 (the whole load is carried again) and empties second_pickup_record, which holds the spire. Much of this never ran in the build's sessions: the rocket parts' tests, their hanging and the kind-9 code.
