ABA8: Test the special beam against a ground object
Used by the routine at update_ground_objects.
For each ground object that is not a pick-up, is on the screen, is not already dissolving and is not of kind $0B, while the special beam is out (special_beam_on): its box is set from where it was drawn (object_box), the beam's record found (find_special_beam, which leaves IY at it) and tested against the box (the entry near the start of test_player_shots, through test_shot_box). Kind $0B (a ringed planet, by its picture) is the one kind the beam cannot destroy. If that started the object dissolving (bit 3 of +$17), it is marked gone in map_object_kinds. Then, for graphic $0D only: an ore deposit (kind 8 to $0A) gives its kind to the beam's +$13, which brings the ore back to the tank (load_ore); kinds 6 and 7 set the bit for their map square in the player's record (map_square_bit).
