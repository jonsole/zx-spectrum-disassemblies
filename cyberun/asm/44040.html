AC08: Find a map square's bit in the player's record
Used by the routines at draw_beams and beam_hits_ground_object.
The player's record (score) keeps 24 bits at +3 to +5 (cleared at the end of each level, level_done), one for each of twelve page rows and two halves of the map across (columns below $30 and from $30), marking the squares whose beam generator has been destroyed. This returns the byte and the bit, the bit as eight times its number, ready to add into a BIT, SET or RES opcode. beam_hits_ground_object sets them and draw_beams tests them.
Input
D The page row (1-12)
E The page column
Output
HL The byte
B Eight times the bit number
