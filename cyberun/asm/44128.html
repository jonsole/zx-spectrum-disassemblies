AC60: Update and draw the ground objects
Used by the routine at scroll_landscape.
Once a pass, from the landscape's drawing (scroll_landscape): before or after the landscape is drawn depending on which way the screen is moving up or down. For each of the six records at ground_objects in use:
A ground object (not a pick-up) whose +$18 bits 1-3 are set may fire (ground_object_fires). The pair of images is started (clear_sprite_pair) and, if it was drawn last pass, its erase set up (prepare_erase). animate_object animates it if bit 7 of +$15 is set; a pick-up is tested against the ship (collect_pickup). An object to be removed is freed and only rubbed out; any other is placed for this pass (prepare_draw). The compositor rubs out and draws (draw_object_images); a ground object's colour patch is trimmed (trim_colour_patch); the colours are put on (colour_sprite); and a ground object is tested against the special beam (beam_hits_ground_object).
The ground objects do not move by themselves: their positions in the world are fixed, and they move on the screen as the view scrolls (place_on_screen).
