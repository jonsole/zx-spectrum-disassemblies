AD48: Place an object for this pass and set up its new image
Used by the routines at place_object, move_missile and update_ground_objects.
Works out where the object is on the screen (place_on_screen); if it is visible, sets up its new image for the compositor: its graphic clipped (new_sprite_header), its row routine (set_new_sprite), and its colour for colour_sprite (the third byte of colour_patch), one of the two in +$0D on alternate passes (bit 0 of the pass counter in pass_counter): on odd passes ink bits 0-2 with BRIGHT from bit 3, on even passes ink bits 4-6 with BRIGHT from bit 7, always on black paper, so an object can flicker between two colours.
A dissolving object (bit 3 of +$17) counts its step in bits 0-2 of +$17 and passes it to dissolve_sprite in dissolve_step; at the eighth step it is freed instead.
