AE98: Colour the cells under a sprite just drawn
Used by the routines at move_missile, update_attacker, draw_rocket and update_ground_objects.
The last step of drawing an object's sprite (move_missile, update_attacker, draw_rocket, update_ground_objects call it after draw_object_images has drawn it): works out the rectangle of character cells the sprite covers in the play area, and fills it with the attribute in colour_patch's third byte, set by prepare_draw from the record's two colours. The rectangle's width goes into colour_patch, its height into the next byte, and fill_attributes paints it, bottom row first.
The sprite's position is the third and fourth bytes of old_sprite_x (its x in pixels, its bottom line), its width in pixels is sprite_width_drawn (the width in bytes, multiplied by 8 in draw_sprite_pair) and its height in lines in the last byte of old_sprite_x, as set_new_sprite left them after clipping. The play area runs across from x 8 to x $E7 and down from line $18 to line $B7; the cells are clipped to it. A cell holding only the sprite's top line (the top line being the last of its cell) is not counted: (bottom OR 7 less (top - 7)) / 8 rounds that one away.
Two more entries: draw_shot comes in at the instruction that takes the width from B, with L, H, D and B already set (the special beam), and colour_carried_parts at the height's working, with the width already in colour_patch.
