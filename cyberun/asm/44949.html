AF95: Graphics of the ship's add-ons
Eleven words, the add-ons 1 to 11 in order. fit_addon draws add-ons 1 to 9 into the ship's picture from here; type $0C objects, the add-ons lying on the planet waiting to be collected, use the same list as their frames (object_graphic_lists), their +13 being the add-on's number less one. carried_part draws 10 and 11 apart from the ship.
Each graphic is preceded by a byte: the height the ship's picture has to be to show it, 40 less the buffer row it starts in (see build_ship_sprite). Then its width in bytes, its height in lines, the picture (width times height bytes, bottom row first), and either $FF (no mask) or a mask of the same size, ANDed onto the ship's picture before the graphic is ORed on.
