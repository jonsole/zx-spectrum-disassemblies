AFE9: Build the ship's sprite from its add-ons
Used by the routines at draw_ship_image and start_landscape.
The ship's sprite is put together in a buffer of 50 rows of 6 bytes (300 bytes) at ship_picture, drawn from the bottom row up: the bare ship (84 bytes from bare_ship, 14 rows) goes in rows 26-39, the top of the picture without add-on 9, and every add-on fitted -- the nine bytes from addon_slots, add-on n's slot holding n or 0 -- is masked and drawn in at its own place (fit_addon), each hanging below the ship. The sprite's height in rows (ship_height) is the most any add-on needs, at least 14, and drawing starts that many rows below row 40 (ship_graphic). Add-on 9 sits on top of the ship, rows 36-50, and adds 11 rows to the height after the start is worked out, so the picture then ends at row 50 -- one row past the buffer, in spare_after_ship. Called from draw_ship when an add-on is fitted, and from start_landscape.
The ship's top stays where it was on the screen and its bottom line (ship_screen_y) moves down by the rows added, and the ship's world position (its record at ship_record) moves with it, so the ship does not jump; ship_line, the line its shots come out of, is 8 below its top.
The add-ons' effects set here: 4 doubles the top speed across (top_speed 4 instead of 2); 5 doubles the top climb (top_climb); 9 adds 11 rows to the height, into which the tail of load_ore draws the ore collected (ore rows); a rocket part being carried (rocket_part_carried not $FF) is hung under the ship again by toggle_carried_part's second entry. Add-on 2 always comes with add-on 1: its slot is set to twice add-on 1's. All of it ran in the build's every-add-on session; with every add-on fitted the picture is 51 rows, rows 0-50, its top row in spare_after_ship (simulated for the Graphics page).
