B09C: Draw an add-on into the ship's sprite
Used by the routine at build_ship_sprite.
Draws add-on n, from its graphic in addon_graphics at its place in the buffer from addon_places, and raises the sprite's height (ship_height) to the byte before the graphic if that is more. If the graphic has a mask -- anything but $FF after the picture -- it is ANDed onto the buffer first (addon_mask_and_draw); then the picture is ORed on. Rows in the buffer are 6 bytes apart.
The mask test has a catch: a mask whose first byte is $FF is taken as no mask at all.
