B115: Read the controls, and burn fuel for steering and thrust
Used by the routine at move_ship.
Reads the current player's control method (read_control_method) into controls_now, after three overrides: no thrust at the ceiling -- the view on y page 5 (page 0 with add-on 5) and view_y, the view's line within its page, below $60; only fire and the special key while the special beam is on (special_beam_on); and once the ship has docked with the finished rocket (launch_state set) nothing is read at all and move_ship flies the ship itself.
Then the fuel: 8 a pass while steering or thrusting, 16 while doing both at once, halved with add-on 6 and halved again if fuel_saver is set (by ship_contact when something knocks the ship sideways; cleared every pass in update_objects). It is added to the fuel word through add_fuel, which counts up towards empty.
