B234: Steer the ship: its speed across and up, and this pass's scroll
Used by the routine at ship_pass.
Called first thing in a pass (ship_pass). The ship never moves on the screen across, and up and down only with its height: the landscape moves under it. This works out how far, in pixels, up and across (scroll_down and the byte after it), for the landscape (draw_landscape) and, negated, for the ship's position in the world (ship_speed_from_scroll).
Across, accelerate_across does the work: this gives it the push in DE, in 256ths of a pixel a pass a pass: $40 left or -$40 right, plus a drag of 12 against the way the ship was going (the step across last pass) -- but only when pushing against it; pushing the way the ship goes ignores the drag. With nothing pushed the drag alone slows the ship. Nothing is pushed while fuel_saver is set (knocked sideways). steer_push says which way it was pushed ($80 left, 3 right, 0 not), for accelerate_across and the ship's drawing.
Up: gravity takes $40 a pass from the climb speed (the ship's record +$1B +$1C), or, while thrusting, thrust adds $50 instead -- one or the other is loaded, never both. The speed is clamped (clamp_climb): no faster than half the top climb (top_climb, 2 or 4) up, and -3 down. It is added to a fraction kept in climb_fraction, and the change in the whole part, doubled, is the step: the screen moves 2 lines a unit. A step one unit too big upwards, or down by 6, is cut to the top climb, or 4. At a step of 4 either way, every other fine-scroll position (origin_y bit 1) moves only 2.
While the special beam is on (special_beam_on) the ship hangs still. Once it has docked with the finished rocket (launch_state set) it thrusts by itself at the fast climb until the view reaches y page 0, then stops.
The second entry, at the adding of the speed to the fraction, works the step out from a speed in HL; bounce_up_down and hit_block (a bounce) use it.
