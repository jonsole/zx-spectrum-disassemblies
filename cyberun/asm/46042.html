B3DA: The pattern of a laser going right
The laser is one line high; this and laser_pattern_left are its pattern, 8 bytes each. A beam of n cells takes n bytes from offset +F (beam_pattern), so as the beam grows and +F falls the head stays solid and the tail ragged. Going right: broken at the left, the tail, solid at the right, the head. update_shot draws with it.
laser_pattern_right B3DA DEFB $4D,$9E,$7C,$7F,$3F,$E7,$FF,$FF
