B414: Update the special beam
Used by the routine at update_shot.
The special beam (record +15 bit 7) is fired by the special key with add-on 7 while the ship is still (special_beam_key), and the ship hangs still while it lasts. It is a band 5 to 8 lines high (beam_frames's frames, cycling 0, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1 a pass) that grows a cell every other pass up to 8 cells, its probe 8 pixels further each time (+12), stops growing when the probe meets the landscape (probe_special_beam), and after 22 passes ($16 in +11), or when the key is let go (end_special_beam), shrinks a cell a pass until gone. Going left, its left end moves out as it grows. A sound every pass (sound_flutter).
Drawing as for a laser (draw_shot), but the pattern for rubbing out is last pass's frame expanded into the sprite buffer at the end of build_shifted_pieces (old_beam_frame), and the new one into sprite_buffer_two (new_beam_frame); the beam is coloured bright magenta. When it has gone the ship is free again (special_beam_on).
It ran in the build's every-add-on session, but only going right and never with the probe in the landscape: the left end's moves going left and the probe's return to the ship are read only. Fired with the ship on the ground, the probe is in the landscape at once: the cell the beam has just grown is taken off again, its length is back to 0, and draw_shot frees the record that same pass, so the beam shows only in the air (run in SkoolKit's simulator for the Animations page).
