B483: Update a laser shot
Used by the routine at update_shots.
Called for each laser (and special beam) in flight (update_shots). A laser is a line one pixel high, drawn on the screen and coloured a cell high, which grows out of the ship's nose: its head 16 pixels a pass and its tail 8, its length (+E, in cells) one more each pass. A probe moves through the world 16 pixels a pass ahead of the ship (probe_laser, the record's +7 being the ship's speed plus 16); when it meets the landscape, or the head reaches the play area's edge (x $B8 for the left end of a beam going right), the head stops (+10 bit 7). The pattern's offset (+F) then counts down to 0, after which the tail catches up (bit 6) a cell a pass until the beam has gone. While stopped the beam is moved by the scroll, so it stays on what it hit. The attackers are checked against it in test_player_shots.
On its first pass (+E 0) the line is set: the ship's shot line (ship_line) less this pass's climb, plus a random -1, 0 or +1, and a random colour (random_beam_colour), and the record's world position is moved by that. Going left is update_left_shot; both end in draw_shot, which draws the beam and frees the record when it has gone.
piece_step_x is set to $80 for collide_with_landscape: a shot stops at the first box of landscape it touches.
