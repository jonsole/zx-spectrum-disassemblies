B5AF: Draw a beam, colour it, and free the record when it has gone
Used by the routine at update_left_shot.
The end of every beam's update: update_left_shot comes in at the top with the left-going pattern, update_shot at its second instruction with the right-going one, update_special_beam at its third with its own in DE. The beam follows the vertical scroll (scroll_down) on the screen; last pass's beam is rubbed out (erase_laser) and this pass's drawn (draw_beam), and the record moved by its speed (move_object).
A laser is coloured with its own attribute, a strip of its length one cell high (shot_colour_patch, painted by fill_attributes); the special beam bright magenta over its width and height (colour_sprite's second entry). A beam of no length is finished: its record is freed (+B 0) and, for the special beam, the ship let go (special_beam_on) -- the jump to load_ore then returns at once, a shot's +13 being 0.
