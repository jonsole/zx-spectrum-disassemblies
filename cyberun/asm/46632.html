B628: Update a laser shot going left
Used by the routine at update_shot.
update_shot for a beam going left, mirrored: the left end moves 16 pixels a pass as the beam grows a cell, the probe 16 pixels a pass the other way (+7 the ship's speed less 16), the pattern taken from the left-going one at offset +F counting up. The head stops at the play area's left edge (x 8) or when the probe meets the landscape; the tail catches up when offset and length together reach 8.
