B6F2: Test a shot's probe against the landscape
Used by the routine at probe_laser.
A box C wide and B high at the record's world position, tested with test_landscape_at (which moves it 4 pixels left and 4 down for the test, and back). A laser that hits makes a sound (sound_tick).
Input
B the height in lines
IX the shot's record
Output
F NZ if it hit
probe_shot B6F2 LD C,$18 24 wide: test it
B6F4 CALL test_landscape_at
B6F7 LD A,(scratch_count) not hit: Z
B6FA AND A
B6FB RET Z
B6FC BIT 7,(IX+$15) a laser: a tick; NZ
B700 CALL Z,sound_tick
B703 OR $01
B705 RET
