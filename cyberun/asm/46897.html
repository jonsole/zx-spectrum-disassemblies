B731: Fire, if the fire key is pressed
Used by the routine at draw_trail.
Called every pass from draw_ship. First the special beam's key (special_beam_key); then, if the fire key is held and the gun has cooled (6 passes, gun_cooling), a shot in the first free record of four. Its record is cleared (free_object), takes the ship's facing and world position, 8 pixels ahead of it (24 facing right), at the ship's shot line (place_shot), and is moved there. Then one of three kinds:
A laser (update_shot takes it on), with a zap (sound_crackle_b).
A missile, every other shot with add-on 8 (shot_alternator bit 0): type 0, the small 8 by 3 sprite of missile_frames, steady bright cyan, flying 8 pixels a pass from 32 ahead of the ship (8 facing left) and 3 lines higher, updated as an ordinary object by update_missile (+16 $80).
The special beam (shot_alternator bit 2, from special_beam_key): +15 $80, bright magenta, at x $90 going right or $60 going left, stepping 8 a pass; it holds the ship still (special_beam_on).
Both ran in the build's every-add-on session, though a missile was never fired facing left.
