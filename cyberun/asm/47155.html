B833: Launch or end the special beam
Used by the routine at fire.
The special key (the number row, or a letter with a joystick: read_control_method) launches the special beam if add-on 7 is fitted and the ship is still: its speeds last pass (ship_across_speed across and ship_down_speed up and down, in the ship's record) both within dead_band's dead band. Letting the key go while the beam is out starts it shrinking (end_special_beam).
It ran in the build's every-add-on session.
