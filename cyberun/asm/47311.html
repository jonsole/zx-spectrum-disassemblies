B8CF: The special beam's frames
Six words, the frames in the order they are shown: 0, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1, so the band swells and shrinks. Each frame is a byte of its height (5 to 8 lines), then two bytes a line, a 16-pixel pattern. beam_frame picks one; expand_beam_frame expands it.
