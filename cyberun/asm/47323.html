B8DB: Expand a special beam frame into a pattern buffer
Used by the routines at old_beam_frame and new_beam_frame.
Reached by JP from old_beam_frame and new_beam_frame with DE the frame's graphic and HL the buffer (the one at the end of build_shifted_pieces for last pass's frame, sprite_buffer_two for this pass's). Each line's two bytes are repeated four times into an 8-byte row, for the frame's height, so draw_beam and erase_laser can take up to 8 cells of it. Then DE is the buffer, one byte on if the beam goes left and its length is odd, which keeps the pattern in step with the screen.
Input
A the frame's height
DE the frame's graphic
HL the buffer
IX the shot's record
Output
DE the buffer, adjusted
