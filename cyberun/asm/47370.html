B90A: Put a shot at the ship
Used by the routine at fire.
The shot's world position is the ship's (view_page_column and view_page_row, its record's +1 to +5) and its first move, in +9, is down to the ship's shot line (ship_line) from the ship's bottom line (ship_screen_y). Its speed across is 8 pixels (facing left) or 24 (facing right), for fire to move it ahead of the ship's nose.
