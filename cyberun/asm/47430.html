B946: Find the list of landscape boxes for a page
Used by the routine at place_collision_box.
The landscape's collision boxes are kept page by page: the world is a ring of $60 pages across, each 224 pixels wide (x 8 to $E7), and a stack of pages down, each 160 lines high (y $18 to $B7). The screen shows parts of four at once, and draw_landscape builds a list of boxes for each of them in the four work areas: loader_entry for the view's page (view_column across, view_row down), ground_cell_bottom_left for the one below it, ground_cell_top_right for the one to its right, ground_cell_bottom_right for the one below and right. This finds the list for page D across, B down; a page off the screen gets the empty list at empty_box_list.
Each list is a count, then five bytes a box: +0 its left x, +1 its bottom line, +2 its width, +3 its height (or, for a slope, its other edge's line), +4 its kind (see collide_with_landscape).
Input
B the page down
D the page across
Output
IX the list
F Z if found
