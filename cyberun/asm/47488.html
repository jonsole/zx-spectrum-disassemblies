B980: Move a record across the world by its speed
Used by the routines at leave_slope, place_collision_box and move_object.
Adds the speed (+7 +8) to the position (+2 +3), and steps the page (+1) when the position leaves 8 to $E7: up a page past $E7, wrapping from $5F to 0, down a page below 8, wrapping from 0 to $5F. Both a run past the end and an 8-bit overflow count, so speeds up to 32 pixels a pass are safe. The record is not changed: move_object stores the result, and place_collision_box uses it as the place to test.

IX the record
