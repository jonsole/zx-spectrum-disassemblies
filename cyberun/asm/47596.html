B9EC: Set the ship's speed in the world from this pass's scroll
Used by the routines at toggle_carried_part, build_ship_sprite and ship_pass.
The ship's record, at ship_record, holds its position in the world (+1 to +6) like any object's; it moves exactly as the landscape scrolls, the other way: across and up by minus the steps in scroll_down, whole pixels.

IX the ship's record
