BA0B: Zero a speed too small to matter
Used by the routines at reverse_speed, special_beam_key, hit_block and leave_slope.
A speed (8.8) from -0.65 to 0.39 (-$A8 to $63 in 256ths) becomes 0. The landscape bounces (hit_block, leave_slope) use it so that a bounce dies away; special_beam_key to tell whether the ship is still.
Input
HL the speed
Output
HL the speed, or 0
