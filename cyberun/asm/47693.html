BA4D: The next box, the next part, or done
Used by the routines at hit_block and hit_slope.
The loop's tail: after a bounce (bit 6 of piece_ceiling) the test ends; otherwise the next box, then the next part (next_part_boxes), until ground_cell, the parts left, is 0. When the ship has gone through all of them without stopping, ship_on_slope is cleared. The last part is where a test stops early: a shot stops at its first hit; the ship and the objects stop at a bounce, a slope or a block -- except an object once the ship has docked with the rocket or is dying (death_count 10 or more), which goes on to the other boxes.
