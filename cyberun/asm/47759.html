BA8F: Test a part against a solid block, and bounce
Used by the routine at next_part_boxes.
The block is tested only if the record is moving into it: a floor (kind bit 2 set) when it moves down or not at all (last_speed_up, the climb speed it had last pass negated, so positive while it falls), a ceiling (bit 2 clear: bit 3 is set) when it moves up or not at all, a wall by its speed across (last_speed_across; bit 4 of the kind says which way). Then the boxes are tested for overlap (boxes_overlap). A shot that hits stops there.
Floors and ceilings: the climb speed is reversed and halved (negate_hl, halve_hl), clamped (clamp_object_speed) and dropped if small (dead_band). For the ship the new speed sets this pass's step at once (move_ship's second entry) with a bump (sound_crackle). An object hitting it has its screen fields cleared and, on a floor (bit 3 clear), its bottom line put 20 above the box's; one that has stopped under a ceiling is given a speed of 2 downwards (+9 = $FE while the collision code has its speeds turned round), so that it drops away. A ship hitting a ceiling (bit 3) without add-on 9 sets trail_hold to 4 (counted down in draw_trail).
Walls: the speed across reversed; for the ship halved, clamped and dropped if small, then this pass's step across worked out again (accelerate_across's second entry) with a sound (sound_buzz), and trail_collision set.
Also, before any of this, for the ship: a floor (bit 2) whose bottom line is 0 to 40 below the ship's and which spans part of x+4 to x+40 sets lift_jet_skip, which stops lift_jet drawing the lift jet below the ship that add-on 5 brings -- unless add-on 2 is fitted.
Every branch ran in the build's sessions, the bit-3 boxes, the walls and the hop included.
