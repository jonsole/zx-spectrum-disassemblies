BC13: Test a part against a slope, and bounce off it
Used by the routine at next_part_boxes.
One slope a pass (piece_ceiling bit 4). test_landscape_piece tests the part against the slope's shape and leaves in touch_depth how far into it the part is. A shot that hits stops; the ship sets trail_collision to $FE. Then the speed is reflected, by the slope's kind and facing: a 45-degree slope swaps the speeds across and up (bounce_45_negate, bounce_45_swap_only), any other mixes them with factors of 3/8 and 3/4 (bounce_shallow_negate, bounce_shallow).
