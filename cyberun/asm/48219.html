BC5B: Push an object out of a slope, and keep it sliding
Used by the routines at bounce_45_negate and bounce_shallow_negate.
Where every slope bounce ends. An object (not the ship) is moved across out of the slope by the depth test_landscape_piece found, through move_across with that as its speed for a moment; a push of 16 or more sets its +10 bit 3. Its screen fields are cleared. If its new speeds are both too small to matter, it is set sliding a pixel a pass downhill (downhill). Then ship_on_slope_response for the ship.
