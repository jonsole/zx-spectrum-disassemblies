BD04: Bounce off a 45-degree slope, swapping and negating
Used by the routine at hit_slope.
The speeds across and up are swapped, each clamped (clamp_object_speed): a reflection off a line at 45 degrees. From here both are negated on the way; from bounce_45_swap_only they are not. Which is done is decided by two JRs in this routine whose displacements are patched: 0 runs the CALL to negate_high_byte after each, 3 jumps over it.
Input
IX the box
bounce_45_negate BD04 XOR A the negations on
