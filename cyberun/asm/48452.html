BD44: Bounce off a slope that is not 45 degrees
Used by the routine at hit_slope.
The new speeds are a mix of the old: across = 3/8 across + 3/8 up and down, up and down = -3/4 up and down + 3/4 across, the terms from the other speed negated or not (slope_negate) as two JRs in this routine decide: their displacements are patched to 0 to run the CALL after each (from here) or to 3 to jump over it (from bounce_shallow). For kind 3 slopes the speeds are swapped before and after (swap_speeds), which mirrors the mix about the diagonal: the same rule serves the steep slopes and the shallow. Each result is clamped (clamp_object_speed). The factors shrink the speed, so a bounce loses energy.
