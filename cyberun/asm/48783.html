BE8F: Work out where a record's box is, and its part in the first page
Used by the routine at collide_with_landscape.
The box is the record's position after this pass's move (move_across, move_down; the record is not changed), C wide and B high from its bottom line up. The move is kept in moved_x and move_across_kept (across), and moved_y and tile_mirrored_left_right (down).
Then, from the loading of moved_y (where next_part comes in for the other parts): the page's list of boxes (find_page_boxes), and the part of the box inside the page -- its x (box_x), bottom line (box_bottom), height (box_height) and width (box_width) -- with what sticks out past the right (stick_out_right) and above the top (stick_out_up). On the first part, the number of parts to test, 1, 2 or 4, goes into ground_cell.
For the ship, its record's speeds are put back to last pass's for the bounce rules (ship_across_speed, ship_down_speed); for an object, they are negated (negate_velocity) -- the objects' speeds are kept the other way round.
Input
B the box's height
C the box's width
IX the record
Output
IX the page's list of boxes
