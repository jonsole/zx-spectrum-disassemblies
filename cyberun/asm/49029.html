BF85: Draw the ship's sprite mirrored when it faces left
Used by the routine at draw_ship_image.
The ship's sprite is drawn the right way round facing right. Facing left (ship_facing bit 7) the drawing starts at its right-hand byte -- x moved by the width less one, in bytes (sprite_width), times 8 -- and runs right to left through a blitter that turns each byte round with the table at bit_reverse_table (B = $F9): direct_erase_row_mirrored for a width of 6 bytes, or one of its later entries for 5, 4, or 3 or fewer. draw_carried_piece comes in at the second instruction with the facing of the thing it draws in A.
Input
IY the blitter for drawing the right way round
Output
B $F9, the reversal table's page
HL the screen position
IY the blitter
