BFB8: Choose the mirrored drawer for a sprite when the ship faces left
Used by the routines at draw_carried_piece and draw_ship_image.
The drawing half of mirror_ship_sprite: when the ship faces left (bit 7 of its facing byte, ship_facing) the image is drawn mirrored. The mirrored drawers (direct_draw_row_mirrored on, beside the plain ones that draw_direct_pair jumps into) work from right to left and look each byte up in the bit-reversal table that build_bit_reverse builds at the start (page $F9), through BC with B the table's page. So this moves L to the last byte of the row and picks the mirrored drawer for the width. Facing right, it returns with IX as the caller left it.
Input
L The low byte of the screen address of the image's first row (its left end)
IX The plain drawer the caller chose
Output
IX The drawer to use: the mirrored one for a width of 6, 5, 4, or anything less
B $F9 when mirrored: the bit-reversal table's page
