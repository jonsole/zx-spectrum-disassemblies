BFE3: Move and draw the three layers of parallax stars
Used by the routine at scroll_landscape.
Called each pass from scroll_landscape after the landscape. Only in the top four rows of the map (the view's map row view_row below 4: open sky) are there stars; lower down this returns at once. It runs in the build's tour at map row 3.
There are three layers of six stars. Each layer keeps an x and a y offset (in star_offsets) that is moved each pass by the scroll steps (scroll_across across, scroll_down down) scaled for the layer by parallax_step: a quarter for the far layer, a half for the middle one, the whole step for the near one. A star's place on the screen is its fixed position in star_layer_pointer plus its layer's offset, wrapping at 256 both ways, so the layers slide past one another at three speeds: parallax for six additions a pass. Last pass's offsets are kept so each star can be erased where it was drawn.
