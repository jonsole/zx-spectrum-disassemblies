C032: Erase and redraw the six stars of one layer
Used by the routine at draw_stars.
One layer of draw_stars. For each star: if it was drawn last pass (bit 0 of its x in the table is the 'drawn' flag) its old place is its position plus the layer's old offsets, and the erase is set up there. Its new place is tested by star_in_sight and, if it is inside the play area, the drawing is set up too. draw_sprite_pair, the sprite compositor, then erases and draws in one go, and the new attribute cell is made white.
A star's image is a graphic number kept in the layer's frame slot: the layer's base number (7, 9 or 11, at +1 of the slot) plus bit 0 of the star's x, since x is held in 2-pixel steps and an odd x takes a copy of the image one pixel further right (see star_offsets).
