C0EA: The parallax stars' positions
Eighteen stars of two bytes, six a layer, the far layer first (draw_stars): x in pixels -- bit 0 is the 'drawn last pass' flag, set and cleared by draw_star_layer, which is why every star's x is even -- and y. A star's place on the screen is this plus its layer's offsets (star_offsets).
