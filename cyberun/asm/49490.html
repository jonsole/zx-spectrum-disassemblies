C152: Scale a scroll step for a parallax layer
Used by the routine at draw_stars.
Divides the step in H by 1, 2 or 4 for the layer in B. A plain arithmetic shift would turn a step of -2 into -1 but +2 into 0 for the far layer, so it would only ever drift one way; instead the far layer takes 0 and -1, or 1 and 0, on alternate passes (bit 0 of the pass counter pass_counter), and moves at a quarter of the speed either way.
Input
B The layer: 1 near, 2 middle, 3 far
H The scroll step (signed pixels)
Output
H The layer's step
