C190: The star images
Graphics 7 to 11, as the compositor draw_sprite_pair draws the stars (three times the size).
Graphics 7 to 11 in the sprite graphic table that graphic_frame_list reads, drawn without a mask (OR on, AND NOT off) by the small-image mover draw_sprite_pair. Each is a width in bytes, a height in rows, then the rows bottom first (everything here is drawn upwards). 7 and 8 are one pixel (at bits 7 and 6), 9 and 10 a two-by-two dot at two positions, 11 a three-row cross. Graphic 12, which an odd x in the near layer would ask for, is not a star; the near layer moves by the whole step, which is always even, so it never is.
