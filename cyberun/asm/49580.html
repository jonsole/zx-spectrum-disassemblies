C1AC: Make an object of a dropped ship part
Used by the routine at carried_part.
Used by carried_part when the carried part is put down on the surface: initialises the object record at IX (free_object) and fills in its graphic number ($0C), its kind (+$13), $FF at +$0D, and its place in the world from four bytes at HL. Read only: none of it ran in the build's sessions.
