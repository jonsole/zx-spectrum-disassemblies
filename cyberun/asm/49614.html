C1CE: Draw the slab-and-spire load on top of the ship, or put it down on the surface
Used by the routines at draw_rocket, draw_ship and play.
The load the ship picks up as an object of kind 9 (collect_pickup sets +$10 of the player's record, player_count_nine, to 9) is the slab and the spire, add-ons 10 and 11 (addon_slab_picture, addon_spire_picture), and rides on top of it in two pieces, 5 bytes wide by $23 rows and 3 wide by $2F rows, drawn by draw_carried_piece at fixed places above the ship: the slab's bottom line $0D above ship_line, which is five lines above the top of the ship's picture, and the spire's $23 higher, standing on the slab. When the ship comes down to the surface (the view's map row, +4 of the ship record, is $0B, the lowest row of the surface above the caverns, and its y in the page is $A7 or more) the load is let go: it is erased and becomes two objects, of kinds 9 and 10, the slab and the spire (place_dropped_part), at the ship's place in the world, the second $10 pixels right and $23 rows up.
Called on even passes from draw_ship, every pass of the death sequence (play) and from draw_rocket. Nothing here ran in the build's sessions (+$10 of the player's record was always zero), so this is read only.
