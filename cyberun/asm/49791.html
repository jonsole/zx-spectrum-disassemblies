C27F: Erase and redraw one piece of the carried load
Used by the routine at carried_part.
The old image is erased only when it has to be: when the load is let go, when a redraw is forced (carried_part_redraw), or when the ship has turned since it was drawn (its facing byte against the one kept at carried_part_facing). Otherwise the new image is drawn over the old one, which is at the same place: the ship does not move on the screen. The place is fixed relative to the ship: x $68 for the 5-wide piece, $78 and $23 rows higher for the 3-wide one, $60 when facing left, and y $0D above the ship's line ship_line, just above the ship's top, so the load rides on top of the ship. Facing left, the mirrored eraser and drawer are used (mirror_ship_sprite, set_mirrored_drawer). Read only: never ran.
