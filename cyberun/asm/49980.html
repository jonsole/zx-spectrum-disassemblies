C33C: Remember where to start the next ship, if this is a safe place
Used by the routine at ship_pass.
Called every fourth pass by ship_pass. A place is safe if the bottom pixel row of the play area (from screen address $57C1, 28 bytes) is nothing but the flat ground's dither ($55: the ship is over flat ground filling the bottom of the screen), or if all four map cells on the screen (the four tile bytes kept by load_map_window) are empty sky (0) or cloud ($A6). Then the view's map cell, its x and y in the cell and the fine scroll are saved in the player's record, where start_landscape finds them when the next ship starts. Bit 0 of the saved x says which kind of place it was (0 over the ground, 1 in the sky): over the ground, start_landscape first paints the dither across the bottom of the play area.
