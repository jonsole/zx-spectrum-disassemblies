C374: Move the ship and the world for one pass of the game
Used by the routine at play.
The first call of each pass of the play loop (play), and of the death sequence. It counts the pass (pass_counter), counts the shield pick-up's timer shield_timer down on odd passes, and every fourth pass offers the place as a restart point (save_restart_point). Then, unless the ship is dying or the finished rocket is lifting off (launch_state), the controls are read and the ship's speeds worked out (move_ship, which leaves this pass's scroll steps in scroll_down and scroll_across), the ship record is given the opposite step (ship_speed_from_scroll) and tested against the landscape (collide_with_landscape) and the other objects (ship_hits_ground_objects), and the record is moved (move_object).
It ends by jumping to scroll_landscape, which scrolls the view, redraws the landscape and the stars, and jumps on to draw_ship to draw the ship: IY, pushed here, is popped there.
