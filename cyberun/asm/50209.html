C421: Erase and redraw the ship's image
Used by the routines at draw_rocket and draw_ship.
Entered from draw_ship on odd passes or when the image must change, and called by draw_rocket. The old image is erased only on a turn, while a part is being fitted (part_being_fitted) or while docking with the finished rocket (death byte 10); the new one is drawn at the same place (ship_screen_x, ship_screen_y), mirrored if the ship now faces left. Then on into draw_trail.
