C4ED: Erase and redraw the ship's trail
Used by the routines at draw_rocket and play.
The trail is a one-byte-wide line behind the ship whose bend follows the speed across (ship_across_speed): the frame is 1 + twice the whole pixels + the half, no more than one past the top speed, mirrored with the direction (record trail_frame-trail_hold, images trail_images). It is erased only when the frame changes, the ship turns (ship_turned) or the collision code asks (trail_collision). Also called at the start of each life and of the death sequence (play; dying, it is only erased) and by draw_rocket. Then on to the pause key, the fire button and the jets.
