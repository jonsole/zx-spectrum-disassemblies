C5FF: Erase and redraw the side jet behind the ship
Used by the routines at check_pause_in_play and draw_trail.
Reached from draw_trail on odd passes, and called by check_pause_in_play during the pause. The add-on fitted when +$0A of the player's record (score) is set (which also raises the top speed across, top_speed, to 4: build_ship_sprite) flickers behind the ship while it is steered left or right: graphics $0E-$10 through the sequence side_jet_frames, with the record side_jet_record and a 2x2 colour patch whose colour cycles through jet_colours. It ran in the build's every-add-on session, but never mirrored: the erase and the draw for steering left are read only.
The end, from the colour patch on, is also used by lift_jet.
