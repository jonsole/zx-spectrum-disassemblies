C6D2: Erase and redraw the lift jet under the ship
Used by the routines at draw_rocket, draw_trail and play.
The add-on fitted when +$0B of the player's record (score) is set (which also raises the top vertical speed top_climb to 4, build_ship_sprite, and lets the ship climb above map row 5, read_controls) is drawn under the ship on even passes: graphic $12, or $11 while thrusting (control bit 3). It is drawn mirrored and not in turn (bit 6 of +3 flips each time), so it seems to spin. While the finished rocket lifts off it is drawn at the rocket's height. With the ship dying (death byte 10 or more) it is only erased, which is why the death sequence (play) calls this once at its start. Its 2x2 attribute patch is white. It ran in the build's every-add-on session, all but the lift-off branch: the launch session had no lift jet.
Stage 0 took this for the death sequence; it is not: the death sequence calls it to take the jet off the screen.
