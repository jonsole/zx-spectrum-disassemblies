C7A3: Get a jet's graphic and its height
Used by the routines at side_jet and lift_jet.
The graphic's address for the sprite record at IX (graphic_frame_list), and its first byte. Only the jets use this (side_jet, graphics $0E-$10; lift_jet, $11 and $12), whose pictures are in the height-first form (puff_graphic), so the byte is the height in lines, not a width.
Input
IX The sprite record (+0 the graphic number)
Output
A The height
DE The graphic's address plus one
sprite_graphic_width C7A3 CALL graphic_frame_list Look it up and read the height.
C7A6 LD A,(DE)
C7A7 INC DE
C7A8 RET
