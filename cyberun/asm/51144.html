C7C8: The trail's six images
Graphics 1 to 6, as the direct drawer draw_direct_pair draws the trail (three times the size).
Graphics 1 to 6 (in the sprite graphic table that graphic_frame_list reads), each one byte wide and 8, 8, 7, 6, 5 and 4 rows high: a width, a height and the rows, bottom first. A line rising from its bottom row and leaning further over at each step, frame 6 a short curve. One byte is 8 pixels: the bend is in the images, not made by shifting. What the trail is in the game has not been seen: drawn behind the ship and bent by its speed across, it looks like a plume.
