C7FC: Accelerate the ship across, and work out this pass's horizontal scroll
Used by the routine at move_ship.
Called by move_ship with the thrust (or the drag) for this pass. The ship's speed across is a word at +$19/+$1A of its record, whole pixels in the high byte (stored high byte first). The thrust is added and the speed clamped to the top speed (clamp_speed_across). With the controls off the drag must not turn the ship round, so a speed that changes sign is made zero instead.
Then speed_to_scroll turns the speed into this pass's horizontal scroll.

IX The ship record
DE The thrust or drag this pass (8.8 fixed point)
