C81F: Turn the speed across into this pass's horizontal step
Used by the routine at hit_block.
The second half of accelerate_across, also used by hit_block. The speed is added to the running position word ship_position_across, and twice the change in its high byte is the step in pixels (the landscape moves in 2-pixel steps). A step of the top speed (top_speed) plus 2 is cut to the top speed. The step goes to scroll_across. One last rule: a step of 4 taken when the fine scroll (origin_x) is at 2 or 6 is halved, so 4-pixel steps start only from 0 or 4.
