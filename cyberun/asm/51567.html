C96F: Copy a bitmap to the screen
Used by the routines at move_method_arrow, draw_panel, draw_frame, draw_fuel_gauge and menu.
Draws a byte-aligned bitmap with no mask, overwriting what is there: a width in bytes, a height in rows, then the rows, bottom row first, drawn upwards from the given pixel position (line_up_hl steps up a row). Used for the panel and the play area's frame.
Input
H The y of the bottom row
L The x (a multiple of 8)
DE The bitmap
Output
DE The byte after it
