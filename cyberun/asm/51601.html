C991: Apply a colour patch
Used by the routines at colour_player_line, contact_damage, draw_beams, colour_sprite, draw_shot, side_jet, draw_panel, flash_fuel_gauge, draw_frame, colour_ship and colour_play_area.
Fills a rectangle of attribute cells with one attribute, from a three-byte record: width in cells, height in cells, attribute. The rectangle is given by its bottom-left pixel and filled upwards. Every sprite's colour comes from such a patch: the patch is all the colour a sprite has, so a sprite takes the colour of the cells it is in (colour_sprite works the patches out for the objects).

L The x
