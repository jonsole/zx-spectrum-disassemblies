CA31: Print the lives of the player in play
Used by the routines at contact_extra_life and draw_panel.
The number shown is the lives less one: the ship in play is not counted. The digit codes start at $1A, so lives plus $19.
print_lives CA31 LD A,(lives) Make the digit and print the record at p1_lives_text (print_string).
CA34 ADD A,$19
CA36 LD (p1_lives_digit),A
CA39 LD DE,p1_lives_text
CA3C JP print_string
