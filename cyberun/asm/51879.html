CAA7: Colour the fuel gauge, flashing red when fuel is low
Used by the routine at update_fuel_gauge.
On odd passes only. With fuel low (the high byte of fuel, $98 or more: about seven tenths used) the gauge's colour patch (2 cells by 16, at x $F0) is red on two passes in four and yellow on the others; otherwise yellow. From the entry point near its end (used by draw_panel): always yellow.
flash_fuel_gauge CAA7 LD A,(pass_counter) Even passes: nothing.
CAAA LD B,A
CAAB AND $01
CAAD RET Z
CAAE LD A,($CC4C) Low and bit 1 of the pass counter set: red.

CAB3 JR C,flash_fuel_gauge_0

CAB7 JR Z,flash_fuel_gauge_0
