CACB: Draw the play area's frame
Used by the routines at draw_panel and menu.
From a 17-byte description at IX (frame_description for the game; the menu, menu, has its own). It draws the two sides, the two edges top and bottom, the four corner pieces with their colour patches, and the ornaments along the edges.
The description: +0 +1 and +2 +3 the screen addresses of the bottom of the left and right sides; +4 their height in rows (each row a byte of $66); +5 +6 and +7 +8 the screen addresses of the two horizontal edges (each two double lines of $FF bytes, with a two-row gap); +9 +A the corner list (four entries of seven bytes: the bitmap's place, the colour patch's place, the patch's width, height and attribute; the bitmaps follow one another from corner_bitmaps, frame_corners); +B the number of ornaments; +C +D their list (pairs: an x for the top and bottom edges, a y for the sides); +E the y of the bottom edge's ornaments (the top's is $BE); +F the x of the right side's (the left's is 0); +$10 the edges' width in bytes.
