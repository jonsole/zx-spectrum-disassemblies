CBF8: The frame's corners
Four entries of seven bytes for draw_frame: the corner bitmap's place (x, y), then a colour patch's place (x, y), width, height and attribute ($4D: bright cyan on blue). The patches are the frame's colour: the top and bottom edges (30 cells by 1) and the two sides (1 by 20). The bitmaps are at corner_bitmaps.
