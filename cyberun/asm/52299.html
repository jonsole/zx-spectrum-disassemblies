CC4B: The fuel
A word, its high byte the gauge's level as a screen row: $42 full, $BE empty, so burning fuel makes it larger. Changed only through add_fuel: the burn at read_controls adds 8 or 16 a pass, contact_refuel takes away $1C00 (a refuel), contact_drain_fuel adds $1000. Filled (high byte $42) at the start of each life by start_landscape; set to empty (high byte $BE) as the death sequence starts (play).
fuel CC4B DEFW $0000
