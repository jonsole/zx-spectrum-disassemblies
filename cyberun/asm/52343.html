CC77: Colour the fuel gauge, and move its fuel column a row towards the fuel level
Used by the routines at start_landscape and play.
Called each pass by the play loop (play) and twice a pass in the death sequence. The fuel inside the gauge is a column three to five pixels wide that runs from the bottom of the gauge up to the level shown. It is never redrawn: each step towards the fuel's level toggles (XOR) two rows: $03,$80 (three pixels in the middle, x $F6-$F8) at one row and $04,$40 (the two pixels either side, $F5 and $F9) at the row below. Two neighbouring steps overlap, so every row inside the column ends up with all five pixels and only the top row has the narrow three: a rounded top, kept up to date by touching two rows a pass.
Going up (filling), the toggled rows are the new level and the one below it; going down (burning), the old level and the one below, which takes the top row's three pixels away and leaves the next row's narrow three as the new top.
