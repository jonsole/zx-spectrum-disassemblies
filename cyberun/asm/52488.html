CD08: Set up a piece's steps from its direction
Used by the routine at draw_landscape.
Called for each piece by draw_landscape with the piece's direction in +3 of the piece state at IX (current_piece). A tile turned upside down (draw_landscape's $99) first remaps the direction through upside_down_table: 0-6 to 7-13 and back, which turns each piece over top to bottom. Then set_piece_shape loads the direction's three steps from piece_shapes: the x step in pixels (piece_dx), the column step in screen bytes (piece_dcolumn) and the rise in rows (piece_rise), each per step of the piece, and sets direction_flag to $FF for a piece that steps right or 1 for one that steps left.
The directions, by their steps (x pixels, bytes, rows): 0 flat (24, 3, 0); 1 a gentle slope (16, 2, 8); 2 a steep one (8, 1, 8); 3 steeper (8, 1, 16); 4-6 the same three going left (-16, -2, 8; -8, -1, 8; -8, -1, 16); 7-13 the same seven upside down (the rise is still taken from 0-6, and piece_ceiling bit 7 marks them); 14 and 15 a wall, going right or left (0, +1 or -1, 16).
From those: piece_rows, the rows to work on per step (piece_rise, or 4 for a flat piece); piece_step_y, the size of the column step; piece_dx_copy, the x step (0 for a wall); piece_step_x, the size of the x step, or 8 for a wall. A wall in an upside-down tile is also moved 8 pixels.
