CD6B: Read a flat piece's end pieces, and mirror a piece for a mirrored tile
Used by the routine at draw_landscape.
Called for each piece by draw_landscape after load_piece_steps, with HL at the byte after the piece's direction-and-steps byte and C its number of steps. Only a flat piece (no rise) has two more bytes: the codes of the pieces at its left and right ends, kept in piece_extra_a and piece_extra_b ($16 means none, and codes from $10 have special handling in flat_step_clip). For a tile mirrored left-right ($FF) they are read the other way round with bit 1 changed (left for right); for one turned upside down ($99) bit 0 is changed as well (top for bottom), and the piece's y is moved (XOR $0C).
The address of the next piece is kept in next_piece. Then, for a mirrored tile, the piece's x is reflected across the page (piece_page_x becomes the complement of x plus its x step plus $0F), its direction is remapped through mirror_table (right for left), set_piece_shape reloads the steps, and a flat run is moved back along by its length less one step (24 pixels a step), so it starts at its other end. A flat piece whose right end is $16 is not mirrored at all.
