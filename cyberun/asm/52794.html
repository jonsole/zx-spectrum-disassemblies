CE3A: An object's box: x; or a list pointer
The object's collision box made by place_collision_box is here and in the next three bytes: x, bottom line (box_bottom), height (box_height) and width (box_width). As a word it is also a list pointer: draw_frame walks the corner bitmaps with it.
box_x CE3A DEFB $00
