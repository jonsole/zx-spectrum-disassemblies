CE5B: A column of a piece is cut off at the top or bottom of the view
Used by the routine at landscape_step_loop.
Jumped to from the column loop (landscape_step_loop) when a column lies across the view's top or bottom edge (edge_flags, the edge flags, bit 0 or 1, or any of bits 0-3 with others). In the upper tiles, a slope ends after this column (C=1). The column is counted in clipped_columns, and the first one so counted calls the code in step_screen_address that keeps the first step's place; then on to the next column.
