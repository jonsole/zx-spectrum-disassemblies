CE79: A lower tile's column reaches the view's bottom edge
Used by the routine at landscape_step_loop.
From landscape_step_loop when, in one of the two lower tiles, the column's top is level with the view's edge (view_y). Bit 7 of the edge flags edge_flags is set; unless the vertical step scroll_down is negative, bit 0 is set instead and copied to piece_clip. On to the horizontal tests.
