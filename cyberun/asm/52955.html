CEDB: A column on the view's left or right edge
Used by the routine at landscape_step_loop.
From landscape_step_loop when the edge flags hold just bit 2 or 3. For a slope going the way that matters for its tile (right in the upper tiles, left in the lower), the piece is cut to at most two more steps (C). The first such column of the piece takes its screen position (step_screen_address) and clears +1 of the piece state; every one is counted in piece_steps_done and clipped_columns by after_step_drawn and is not drawn here.
