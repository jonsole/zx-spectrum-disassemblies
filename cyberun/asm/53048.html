CF38: Colour the attribute cells under a landscape piece
Used by the routine at after_step_drawn.
Called by after_step_drawn after a piece is drawn. The landscape takes its colour from its attribute cells, a colour for the upper tiles (map_row_colour) and another for the lower ones (map_row_colour_next), both set from the level's pair of colours (start_landscape) by set_landscape_colours (the deeper colour from map row $0C down: the caverns). Where the surface meets the caverns (map row $0B in a lower tile, or $0C in an upper one) the deeper colour is used, or the upper one for a piece starting above y $67.
For each step of the piece it colours a column of cells (the rows per step, attribute_rows) one, two or three cells wide (the column step plus one: colour_one_cell, colour_two_cells, colour_three_cells), then moves up by the rise and across by the column step; a flat piece colours three cells wide and as deep as its graphic (about two cells). Only cells inside the play area (in_play_area) and its columns 1-28 are coloured. A piece ending in the special end piece $13 on the right gets one more column when the scroll is not on a byte boundary.
Nothing is coloured while the landscape is being built after a restart (map_rebuild), nor for a piece with no columns drawn.
