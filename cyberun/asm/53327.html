D04F: Colour a column of attribute cells one wide
colour_piece runs on into it for the extra column after an end piece $13, and enters it through IY for the narrowest pieces. Each row upwards: the cell at HL, if it is inside the play area (in_play_area) and its columns 1-28.
Input
B The number of rows
C The attribute
DE $0020
HL The attribute address of the bottom row
colour_one_cell D04F CALL in_play_area One cell a row, upwards.
D052 JR Z,colour_one_cell_0
D054 LD A,L
D055 AND $1F
D057 DEC A
D058 CP $1C
D05A JR NC,colour_one_cell_0
D05C LD (HL),C
colour_one_cell_0 D05D AND A
D05E SBC HL,DE
D060 DJNZ colour_one_cell
D062 RET
