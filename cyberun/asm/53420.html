D0AC: Landscape pieces' collision classes
Sixteen bytes, one per piece direction, put in +4 of each piece's collision box (draw_landscape) for the collision code (collide_with_landscape): 4 flat; 1, 2, 3 the three slopes rising to the right; $41-$43 the same rising to the left (bit 6); 8 flat upside down; $81-$83 and $C1-$C3 the slopes upside down (bit 7); $10 and $20 the two walls.
piece_classes D0AC DEFB $04,$01,$02,$03,$41,$42,$43,$08
D0B4 DEFB $81,$82,$83,$C1,$C2,$C3,$10,$20
