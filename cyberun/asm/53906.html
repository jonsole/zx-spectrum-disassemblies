D292: Test whether a piece in a left-hand map cell ends before the play area's left edge
Used by the routine at draw_landscape.
Used by draw_landscape for each piece of the two left-hand map cells of the 2x2 window (bit 1 of the cell counter ground_cell clear). It finds the piece's rightmost x within the cell from the piece's collision box and compares it with view_x, the in-cell x that sits at the play area's left edge. A piece that ends left of it cannot be seen and is skipped without walking its steps.
The box (IX) is five bytes of a map cell's collision-box list (loader_entry, ground_cell_bottom_left, ground_cell_top_right or ground_cell_bottom_right: the view's page, the one below, right, below-right). It holds the piece's start x at +0 and, for a flat piece, its length in pixels at +2; for a sloped piece +2 holds the x of its far end, and +0 its start x (moved on by a step's width for a piece running left, direction_flag = 1). +4 is a class byte from piece_classes for the collision code (collide_with_landscape).
Input
IX Piece's collision box
B In-cell x at the play area's left edge (view_x)
Output
F Z clear (NZ) if the piece is wholly left of the edge
