D2C0: Test whether a piece in a right-hand map cell starts beyond the play area's right edge
Used by the routine at draw_landscape.
The counterpart of piece_off_left_edge for the two right-hand map cells of the window. In the right-hand cell the play area's right edge falls at the same in-cell x as the left edge does in the left-hand one (view_x), so a piece whose leftmost x is beyond it is out of sight.
Input
IX Piece's collision box
B In-cell x of the play area's right edge (view_x)
Output
F Z clear (NZ) if the piece is wholly right of the edge
piece_off_right_edge D2C0 LD A,(piece_dx_copy) A sloped piece running left: its leftmost x is its far end, +2, less one.
D2C3 AND A
D2C4 JR Z,piece_off_right_edge_0
D2C6 LD A,(direction_flag)
D2C9 CP $FF
D2CB JR Z,piece_off_right_edge_0
D2CD LD A,(IX+$02)
D2D0 DEC A
D2D1 JR piece_off_right_edge_1
piece_off_right_edge_0 D2D3 LD A,(piece_page_x) Otherwise the piece's start x.
piece_off_right_edge_1 D2D6 CP B NZ if beyond the edge. (A piece starting exactly on the edge gives Z and is walked anyway.)
D2D7 RET NC
D2D8 XOR A
D2D9 RET
