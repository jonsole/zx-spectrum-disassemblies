D2E1: Test whether a piece in a lower map cell lies wholly below the play area
Used by the routine at draw_landscape.
The counterpart of piece_off_top_edge for the lower map cells: the piece's topmost y is compared with view_y, the in-cell y of the play area's bottom row. A flat piece's top is its start y less 4 (a flat step is four rows tall); a vertical wall's is +1 less +3 of its collision box; a slope's is +3.
Input
IX Piece's collision box
B In-cell y of the play area's bottom edge (view_y)
Output
F Z clear (NZ) if the piece is wholly below the play area
piece_off_bottom_edge D2E1 LD A,(piece_rise) Flat piece.
D2E4 AND A
D2E5 JR NZ,piece_off_bottom_edge_0
D2E7 LD A,(piece_page_y)
D2EA SUB $04
D2EC JR piece_off_bottom_edge_2
piece_off_bottom_edge_0 D2EE LD A,(piece_dx_copy) Vertical wall.
D2F1 AND A
D2F2 JR NZ,piece_off_bottom_edge_1
D2F4 LD A,(IX+$01)
D2F7 SUB (IX+$03)
D2FA JR piece_off_bottom_edge_2
piece_off_bottom_edge_1 D2FC LD A,(IX+$03) Slope.
piece_off_bottom_edge_2 D2FF CP B NZ if below the bottom edge.
D300 RET NC
D301 XOR A
D302 RET
