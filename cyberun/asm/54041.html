D319: Move to the next step of a piece, cull it, and set up its graphic and blitter
Used by the routine at after_step_drawn.
The per-step loop of the landscape renderer. A piece is a straight run of identical steps; each step moves the drawing position on by its direction's advance (piece_dx pixels across, piece_dcolumn bytes across, piece_rise pixels up, from the table at piece_shapes by set_piece_shape). Directions 0-6 are floors -- flat, three slopes rising to the right, three rising to the left -- and 7-13 the same shapes as ceilings (rock above, sky below; set_piece_shape sets piece_ceiling to $80 for them); 14 and 15 are vertical walls. For each step this routine:
1. Advances the in-cell position (piece_page_x, piece_page_y) and the screen position (piece_x, piece_y). A piece's first step is entered further down, without moving. 2. Culls it against the play area. Vertically: in an upper map cell a step whose base is above the top edge is skipped; in a lower one, a step whose top is below the bottom edge; a step exactly on the edge sets a flag in edge_flags (column_at_view_top, column_at_view_bottom). Horizontally the same against view_x, with flags set by column_at_view_left and column_at_view_right. Skipped steps go to the step counter in after_step_drawn without drawing. 3. With an edge flag set, goes to edge_column or column_clipped (the edge-exact cases). 4. Otherwise reads the step's variant (0-2, from the repeating sequence at column_varieties) and, for the piece's first drawn step, looks its direction up in the fine-scroll table at shift_table (16 entries of four bytes: a blitter address into IY, a graphic address into piece_graphic), works out the screen address (step_screen_address) and finds this direction's row of variant offsets (piece_offsets). 5. Chooses how to draw: a flat step goes to flat_step_clip; a sloped step near the right edge (screen x from $D1) through the patched JR at the end of this routine to slope_step_at_right_edge; others to slope_step_at_left_edge, which clips at the left edge or draws whole. All of them end at draw_step, which draws the step.
Interrupts are off for the whole game, which is what makes it safe for the blitters to borrow SP.
Input
HL' Address of this step's variant byte (in column_varieties)
C' Steps left in the piece
D' Steps drawn so far
