D4CB: Draw a sloped step whole, or decide how it is clipped at the left edge
Used by the routines at landscape_step_loop and slope_step_at_right_edge.
Steps whose screen x is from -8 to 6 hang over the play area's left edge (column 0 is the border, and x from $F0 is "negative": columns 30 and 31 of the same character row). Anything else is drawn whole (with fold_column_step for a vertical wall). A clipped step uses blit_left_clip (dispatch 8) for a piece running right, or blit_mirrored_left_clip (dispatch 11) for one running left; the bytes to skip and the columns to draw are patched into those blitters' JRs, and for blit_left_clip the next step's column into move_across_kept, according to the column the step starts in.
All the clipped cases ran in the build's sessions.
