D672: Draw a landscape step
Used by the routines at landscape_step_loop, set_right_clip_columns, slope_lose_last_column, use_mirrored_right_clip, slope_step_at_left_edge and flat_step_clip.
The draw shared by every direction, reached from landscape_step_loop, set_right_clip_columns, slope_lose_last_column, use_mirrored_right_clip, slope_step_at_left_edge and flat_step_clip (which jumps into it past the first lines with DE already holding a cap's graphic offset). It turns the variant into a graphic address and loads SP with it. If the step hangs below the play area (piece_y from $B8) it pops the rows below the edge away and starts at the bottom row; if it reaches above the top it shortens the row count (and, for a slope, cuts the piece short, since pieces only climb). Then the JR at the end, whose displacement was set to 0, 2, 5, 8 or 11 (0 by landscape_step_loop, the others by the clipping set-ups), lands on one of the five jumps at step_dispatch: the step's own blitter (IY) or one of the four edge-clipping blitters.
Every blitter is entered with A = row pairs to draw, HL = the screen address less the step's byte advance, SP = the first graphic byte, and ends by jumping to after_step_drawn with HL where the next step will start.

E Variant (0-2)
HL' Screen address of the step, less the byte advance (column_address)
