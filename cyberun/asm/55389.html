D85D: Last flat step hangs into the border
$FF when a flat piece's last step hangs off the left edge into the border (set by landscape_step_loop, cleared for each piece by draw_landscape); 0 otherwise. It makes flat_step_clip skip that step, draw_flat_end_lip draw the piece's lip a character row up, and fill_behind_moving_piece treat the piece's fill differently.
