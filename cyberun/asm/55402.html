D86A: Draw the lip at the end of a flat piece whose end cap is $13
Used by the routine at after_step_drawn.
A flat run ending in cap $13 ($93 once seen) gets a lip one byte wide and four rows tall in the byte column after its last step, from floor_lip_bytes (floor) or ceiling_lip_bytes (ceiling) for the current fine horizontal position. Nothing is drawn on a byte boundary or outside the play area's columns, and the lip is cut to two rows at the play area's bottom (piece_y = $B9) or top (piece_y = $19).
