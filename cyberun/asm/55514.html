D8DA: Blank the byte column a flat piece's drop leaves behind when scrolling across
Used by the routine at after_step_drawn.
Called at the end of a flat piece when the fine horizontal position is on a byte boundary and the landscape is scrolling across (scroll_across). A byte column four rows tall beside a drop at one end of the run is cleared to black by fill_under_slope. Which step gives the place depends on the current-piece record's +1: if zero, the piece's first step (first_step_address), provided its cap on the side the scroll moves towards (piece_extra_b when scroll_across is negative, else piece_extra_a) is odd; otherwise the step kept by flat_step_clip (piece_kept set), two columns on.
