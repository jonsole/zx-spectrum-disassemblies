D937: Work out a step's screen position and address
Used by the routines at edge_column and landscape_step_loop.
Screen x = in-cell x (piece_page_x) + horizontal origin (origin_x); screen y = in-cell y (piece_page_y) + vertical origin (origin_y). Both go into the current-piece record (+0, +1) and piece_x/piece_y, and HL is the screen address (pixel_to_screen). For the piece's first step the address is also kept in first_step_address (a character row back when the step hangs off the left edge, x from $F0) and its y in first_step_y; for the piece's first drawn step the screen position is kept in first_step_x/first_step_y_drawn (column_clipped calls the routine's last part for that alone).
