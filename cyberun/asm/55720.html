D9A8: Fill the rows a vertically scrolled piece has uncovered
Used by the routine at after_step_drawn.
When the landscape has moved up or down by vertical_step_size pixels (2 or 4) this pass, each drawn step of the piece leaves that many rows of its old place uncovered: below it when the landscape moved up, above when it moved down. Starting from the first drawn step (first_step_x), this paints them step by step (fill_next_step with IY = fill_two_rows for 2 rows, fill_four_rows for 4) with the checker ($55AA) for ground or black for sky -- the other way round for a ceiling piece (piece_ceiling). The width per step is its byte advance (piece_step_y), adjusted for the shift, a reversing piece and drop caps (landscape_end_pieces, adjust_fill_for_scroll).
