DB5B: Fill one row pair across a step's width
Used by the routines at fill_two_rows and fill_four_rows.
Writes D on the row at HL and E on the row above, for C bytes rightwards, skipping any byte outside columns 1-28, and only when the row (piece_page_y) is inside the play area. Then moves piece_page_y and HL two rows up and a byte advance on.
