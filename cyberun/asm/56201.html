DB89: Draw a sloped step cut off by the right edge
Used by the routine at step_dispatch.
Reached through step_dispatch (dispatch 2) for a sloped step of a piece running right (directions 1-3 and their ceilings) that crosses the play area's right edge: only its left one or two byte columns are written. Like the full blitters it zig-zags up the screen two rows at a time, popping the graphic from SP. After the first column's byte, the JR whose displacement set_right_clip_columns or slope_lose_last_column set decides the rest:
  • 0: the second column, then the upper row's second and first columns, passing the third column's pair -- a three-byte step cut to two.
  • $1E: one column -- a three-byte step cut to one.
  • $1F: one column -- a two-byte step cut to one.
  • $20: one column -- a one-byte step, uncut.
