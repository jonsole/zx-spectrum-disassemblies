DCA1: Set up the landscape for a life and scroll it into view
Used by the routine at play.
Called by play when a player's life starts. Fills the fuel, blacks out the play area's colour, takes the planet's landscape colours from planet_ground_colours, and restores the view from the player's record score: the map column and row (+$13, and +$14 kept one less), the in-cell x and y at the play area's left and bottom edges (+$15, +$16; bit 0 of the x set means the play area does not need filling with the checker first), and the scroll origins (+$17, +$18).
Then, with map_rebuild = $FF (the fly-in), the landscape is scrolled vertically 4 pixels a pass (2 on the first if that brings the origin to a multiple of 4) through scroll_landscape, with fly_in_step deciding when to stop; with map_rebuild = 3 one more pass is drawn; and with map_rebuild = 0 the window is rebuilt with its objects, the ship's record (in bare_ship) gets the map position, and play goes on by way of sound_rising_whine.
