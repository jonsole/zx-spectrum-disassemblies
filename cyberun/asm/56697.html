DD79: Decide whether the landscape's fly-in has finished
Used by the routine at scroll_landscape.
scroll_landscape comes here instead of going on with the play pass while map_rebuild is not zero. In the fly-in ($FF) the landscape keeps scrolling 4 pixels a pass: 40 passes (160 rows, one map cell) when the play area was left unfilled, or, when it was filled with the checker, until the six bytes in two rows at the ship's screen position (kept in bare_ship) are all clear -- the sky has been swept down to the ship's place. Then the colour goes on, one pass is drawn with map_rebuild = 3, and finally the window is rebuilt with objects (map_rebuild = 0), the ship's record (in bare_ship) gets the map column and row (+1, +4) and the in-cell position (+2, +5), and play begins through sound_rising_whine, which returns to the caller of start_landscape.
