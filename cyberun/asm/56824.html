DDF8: Scroll the view, step through the map, and draw the landscape
Used by the routines at ship_pass and start_landscape.
Jumped to at the end of ship_pass (once a play pass) and from start_landscape (the fly-in). scroll_down and scroll_across are this pass's vertical and horizontal scroll in pixels (signed, from the ship's movement). The scroll origins origin_y and origin_x move by them, and the in-cell positions at the play area's bottom and left edges (view_y = $B7 - origin_y, view_x = 8 - origin_x) move the other way. When an edge position leaves its map cell it wraps by a cell -- 160 rows or 224 columns -- and the map row or column (view_row, view_column) moves by one; C counts the wraps into record_ground.
If any happened (or in the fly-in) the window of four map cells is rebuilt (load_map_window) and its colours chosen (set_landscape_colours). The landscape is drawn (draw_landscape) unless the view is in the top three map rows, which hold no landscape (the four collision-box lists are just emptied). The sprites are handled by update_ground_objects before or after the landscape by the scroll's direction, draw_beams and draw_stars run, four rows of the frame below the play area's bottom-left corner are made solid again, and the pass goes on at draw_ship -- or at fly_in_step during the fly-in.
