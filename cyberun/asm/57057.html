DEE1: Choose the landscape attributes for the upper and lower map cells
Used by the routine at scroll_landscape.
When the window has moved (record_ground) or during the fly-in: map_row_colour gets the attribute for the upper map row (view_row) and map_row_colour_next the one for the row below -- ground_attr_above for rows below 12 (above ground), ground_attr_below from row 12 (caverns). The routine's tail is called as a subroutine for the first and fallen into for the second.
set_landscape_colours DEE1 LD A,(record_ground) Nothing changed: done.
DEE4 LD B,A

DEEA LD A,(view_row) The upper row's attribute.
DEED CALL set_landscape_colours_0
DEF0 LD (map_row_colour),A
DEF3 LD A,(view_row) The lower row's.
DEF6 INC A
set_landscape_colours_0 DEF7 CP $0C

DEFD JR C,set_landscape_colours_1

DF02 LD B,A
set_landscape_colours_1 DF03 LD A,B
DF04 LD (map_row_colour_next),A
DF07 RET
