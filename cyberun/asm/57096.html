DF08: Build the 2x2 window of map cells, and bring in the objects they hold
Used by the routines at start_landscape, fly_in_step and scroll_landscape.
HL is the map column (0-95, wrapping at both ends) and row. The four tile bytes -- (column, row), (column, row+1), (column+1, row), (column+1, row+1) -- are read from the map landscape_map (96 bytes a row, read_map_cell) into map_window_tile_bytes, and their tile records' addresses from tile_table into map_window_tiles. At row 28 the lower two are tile $20, at row 29 all four. In the fly-in, tile $3E is drawn as $AA.
In play (map_rebuild zero) it then refreshes the objects: the last two slots of ground_objects, kept for the player's items, are freed if their objects have left the window (drop_far_objects); each of the current player's 19 item records (from +$1D of score; four bytes: map column, map row, x, y; bit 7 of the first set when gone) that lies in the window is put into a free special slot as kind $0C or $1E, unless it is in one already (same_item_in_slot); the four ground-object slots at ground_objects are freed likewise; and spawn_column_objects brings in the map's objects for the window's two columns.
