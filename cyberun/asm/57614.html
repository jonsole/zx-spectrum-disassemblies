E10E: Object kinds: the animated objects' frames
Read by spawn_objects_from_list for a kind whose flag byte in object_kind_flags has bit 4 set: the two bytes at the low four bits' offset go to the slot's +$13, the frame byte (the first frame in bits 0-3, a count and its reload above it), and +$14, the frame range (the first frame in the high nibble, the last in the low), which animate_object steps through. Offsets 0, 2 and 4 are used: kinds 4 and $10 show frames 0 and 1, kinds 5 and $0C frames 2 and 3, and kind $0E, the fuel station, frames 4 and 5 -- the two ship_hits_ground_objects takes for a refuel pad. Read in the build's sessions (spawn_objects_from_list).
