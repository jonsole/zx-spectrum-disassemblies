E205: Object kinds: their colours
One byte per kind (0-16), copied by the kind into the slot's +$0D by spawn_objects_from_list: the object's two colours, one in each nibble (BRIGHT in bit 3 and the ink in bits 0-2, and again in bits 7 and 4-6), which prepare_draw shows on alternate passes, so an object whose nibbles differ flashes between the two.
object_kind_bytes E205 DEFB $EE,$DD,$FF,$BB,$CD,$DF,$DD,$DD
E20D DEFB $DD,$DD,$DD,$FF,$AA,$CC,$EE,$FF
E215 DEFB $DD
