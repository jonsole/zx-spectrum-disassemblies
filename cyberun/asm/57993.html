E289: Draw only a flat piece's left two bytes
One of the clipped ways through the flat-piece blitter blit_flat_step, for a piece that runs off the right-hand side of the play area with two of its three bytes still on it. flat_step_clip sends the loop here by patching the JR in flat_blit_second_byte (offset $26) when the piece's column (the low five bits of column_address) is $18. Each pair of rows is then drawn two bytes wide: the first row's third byte and the second row's right-hand byte are popped and dropped, so SP stays in step with the graphic.
flat_blit_left_two E289 DEC H Up a pixel row: the first row's third byte is not drawn
E28A POP DE Drop it, and the second row's right-hand byte with it
E28B JP flat_blit_upper_row_0 Draw the second row's other two bytes, right to left, and go round the loop
