E2A6: Draw a flat piece's rows that lie right of the play area's left-hand side
Part of flat_blit_left_edge's clipped path. The INC SP here is executed only on the first pass, and only when flat_step_clip left the JR before it at 0 (the piece starts two bytes left of the play area); every later pass of the blitter's loop enters at the POP. The JR at the end is patched by flat_step_clip too: 0 goes on to draw two bytes (flat_blit_left_two_rows), $0B one (flat_blit_left_one_row).
