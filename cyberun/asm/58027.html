E2AB: Draw a pair of rows two bytes wide at the play area's left-hand side
For a flat piece that begins one byte left of the play area: draws the second byte of the first row next to the one flat_blit_left_rows drew, the second row's two right-hand bytes back the other way, and drops the second row's left-hand byte together with the first byte of the next pair (the POPs run one byte out of step with the graphic since flat_blit_left_edge's INC SP).
