E2BD: Landscape pieces: blitters and graphics at a shift of 2 pixels
The first of four tables of 16 entries, one per piece direction (the high nibble of the piece's direction-and-steps byte in its tile record, kept in (IX+3)), chosen by draw_landscape from the fine scroll position and kept in shift_table: this one for a shift of 2 pixels, piece_blitters_shift4 for 4, piece_blitters_shift6 for 6 and piece_blitters_shift0 for 0. Each entry is two words: the blitter that draws a step of the piece, which landscape_step_loop loads into IY and step_dispatch jumps to, and the step's graphic at that shift, which it keeps in piece_graphic and starts SP from.
The directions: 0 is flat, three bytes wide; 1, 2 and 3 are slopes that climb to the right (8 rows over 16 pixels, 8 rows over 8, 16 rows over 8), 4, 5 and 6 the same three climbing to the left; 7 to 13 are 0 to 6 upside down (ceilings: rock above, sky below), and 14 and 15 are a 16-row wall facing either way. Each graphic holds three variants of the step one above the other (9 or 17 rows each; 28 rows for a flat step); landscape_step_loop picks one and the count of row pairs comes in A.
Only nine graphics are stored: a step climbing to the left is its partner's graphic started one row later and drawn by a blitter that mirrors each byte through the bit-reversed table at bit_reverse_table (blit_mirror_two, blit_mirror_one). That works because the graphics zig-zag: each pair of rows is stored left to right and then right to left, so read one row later every row's bytes come in the other order. A mirrored shifted step uses the partner's graphic at the opposite shift: a slope climbing left at a shift of 2 is the right-climbing one at 6, mirrored.
The blitters: blit_flat_step for the flat steps at every shift; at shift 0 blit_two (two bytes wide), blit_one (one byte), and their mirrored twins blit_mirror_two (entered one DEC L early, at the end of blit_mirror_three) and blit_mirror_one; at shifts 2 to 6, one byte wider, blit_three (entered at its second INC L), blit_two (at its second INC L) and the mirrored blit_mirror_three (at its second DEC L) and blit_mirror_two. The walls (directions 14 and 15) enter one instruction later than the slopes, skipping the INC L or DEC L that places a slope in its column.
