E3D8: Look up a terrain piece's shape
Used by the routines at load_piece_steps and piece_ends.
Called by load_piece_steps and piece_ends with the piece in hand. Copies its direction's three bytes from piece_shapes to piece_dx (run in pixels), piece_dcolumn (width in bytes) and piece_rise (rise in rows); directions 7 and up (the ceilings, and the walls) use the records seven lower and set piece_ceiling to $80. Then sets direction_flag and the displacement of the JR at the end of landscape_step_loop to match: for a piece that climbs to the right (width positive) direction_flag is $FF and the JR skips the NEG at slope_step_at_right_edge; for one that climbs to the left it is 1 and the NEG is done.
Input
IX the collision-box list of the map cell being drawn (see draw_landscape); (IX+3) the piece's direction
