E41C: Fill the ground under a terrain piece, column by column
Used by the routines at after_step_drawn and fill_flat_end_column.
Called by after_step_drawn once a piece is drawn, to fill what lies beneath it with the ground's checkerboard (DE = $55AA) or to clear it (DE = 0), and by fill_flat_end_column to clear a column four rows deep. Each column is one byte wide and climbs from HL for C pairs of rows: D is written on one pixel row and E on the row above. After each column L moves on by the piece's width in bytes (piece_dcolumn), and piece_page_y, the screen row the fill has reached, goes up by the column's height. B, the number of columns, is the number of steps of the piece drawn (piece_steps_done), so this is one column a step, each the step's width in bytes across and as tall as the fill needs, which is how the ground under a slope comes out solid.
A column is clipped to the play area: one in screen column 0 or 29 to 31 is not drawn at all (the pointer is only moved on); if the fill starts below row 183 ($B7, the bottom of the play area) the first rows are dropped and it starts at the bottom row of the play area; and near the top it is cut short by what the sum with piece_rows (the piece's height) and 24 says. The arithmetic of the top clip is read but not traced through.
Input
B the number of columns
C' pairs of rows in each column
DE' the two pattern bytes ($55AA for the ground's checkerboard, 0 to clear)
HL' the screen address of the first column's bottom byte
