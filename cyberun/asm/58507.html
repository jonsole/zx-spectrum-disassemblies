E48B: Draw a one-byte-wide terrain step
The blitter for the one-byte-wide slopes at shift 0: directions 2, 3, 9 and 10 here, the wall (direction 14) one instruction on, skipping the INC L. Two rows a POP, one byte a row, climbing the screen with DEC H; about 75 T-states a pair of rows. The right-climbing twin of blit_mirror_one.
Input
A the number of pairs of rows
HL the screen address of the bottom row, less one
SP the graphic (at the variant to draw)
blit_one E48B INC L Step right into the column; B counts the pairs of rows; pop two bytes
E48C LD B,A
blit_one_0 E48D POP DE
E48E LD (HL),E One on this row, up a row, the other on the row above, up a row
E48F DEC H
E490 LD (HL),D
E491 DEC H
E492 LD A,H Into the next character row up if this one is done; the next pair; leave HL as the column for the next step (after_step_drawn)
E493 INC A
E494 AND $07
E496 JR NZ,blit_one_1
E498 LD A,L
E499 SUB $20
E49B LD L,A
E49C JR C,blit_one_1
E49E LD A,H
E49F ADD A,$08
E4A1 LD H,A
blit_one_1 E4A2 DJNZ blit_one_0
E4A4 JP after_step_drawn
