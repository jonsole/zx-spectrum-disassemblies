E4A7: Draw a three-byte-wide terrain step
The blitter for the two-byte-wide slopes shifted by 2, 4 or 6 pixels, which are three bytes wide: directions 1 and 8, entered at the second INC L (the first is not used by the tables). Each pair of rows is three POPs: a row left to right, up a row, a row right to left; about 125 T-states a pair.
Input
A the number of pairs of rows
HL the screen address of the bottom row's first byte, less two
SP the graphic
blit_three E4A7 INC L Step right into the column; B counts the pairs of rows
E4A8 INC L
E4A9 INC L
E4AA LD B,A
blit_three_0 E4AB POP DE Pop two bytes: the row's first two, left to right
E4AC LD (HL),E
E4AD INC L
E4AE LD (HL),D
E4AF INC L
E4B0 POP DE Pop two more: the row's third byte; up a row; the next row's right-hand byte
E4B1 LD (HL),E
E4B2 DEC H
E4B3 LD (HL),D
E4B4 DEC L
E4B5 POP DE Pop two more: that row's other two bytes, right to left; up a row
E4B6 LD (HL),E
E4B7 DEC L
E4B8 LD (HL),D
E4B9 DEC H
E4BA LD A,H Into the next character row up if this one is done; the next pair; leave HL as the column for the next step
E4BB INC A
E4BC AND $07
E4BE JR NZ,blit_three_1
E4C0 LD A,L
E4C1 SUB $20
E4C3 LD L,A
E4C4 JR C,blit_three_1
E4C6 LD A,H
E4C7 ADD A,$08
E4C9 LD H,A
blit_three_1 E4CA DJNZ blit_three_0
E4CC JP after_step_drawn
