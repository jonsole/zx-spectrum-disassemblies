E4F1: Draw a three-byte-wide terrain step, mirrored
Directions 4 and 11 shifted by 2, 4 or 6 pixels, entered at the second DEC L (the first is not used by the tables). As blit_three, but each byte goes through the bit-reversed table at bit_reverse_table, and the graphic is the partner's at the opposite shift, started one row on. The last DEC L is the entry for directions 4 and 11 at shift 0 into blit_mirror_two.
Input
A the number of pairs of rows
HL the screen address, plus two
SP the graphic
