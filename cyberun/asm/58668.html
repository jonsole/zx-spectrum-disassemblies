E52C: Draw a two-byte-wide terrain piece, mirrored
The blitter for the pieces that climb to the left and are two bytes wide: directions 4 and 11 at shift 0 (entered one DEC L earlier, at the end of blit_mirror_three), directions 5, 6, 12 and 13 at shifts 2 to 6 (here), and the wall direction 15 at those shifts (at blit_mirror_two + 1, skipping the DEC L). It draws like the two-byte blitter at blit_two -- POP DE, two bytes left to right, up a row, two bytes right to left, up a row -- but each byte goes through the bit-reversed table at bit_reverse_table (built by build_bit_reverse), so the graphic comes out mirrored. With the graphic started one row later than its right-climbing partner's (see piece_blitters_shift2), each row's bytes arrive in the mirrored order too.
The table lookups make it about 40 T-states a pair of rows slower than the plain blitter: about 150 T-states for two rows of two bytes, against about 105.

A the number of pairs of rows
HL the screen address of the bottom row's right-hand byte, plus one
SP the piece's graphic (at the variant to draw)
