E55B: Draw a one-byte-wide terrain piece, mirrored
The blitter for the one-byte-wide pieces that climb to the left at shift 0: directions 5, 6, 12 and 13 here, and the wall direction 15 at blit_mirror_one + 1 (skipping the DEC L). Each popped byte is mirrored through the bit-reversed table at bit_reverse_table on its way to the screen; one byte a row, two rows a POP. The right-climbing twin is the blitter at blit_one, which the code map left as data.
Input
A the number of pairs of rows
HL the screen address of the bottom row's byte, plus one
SP the piece's graphic (at the variant to draw)
blit_mirror_one E55B DEC L Step left into the piece's column
E55C LD B,$F9 B' holds the table's page; B the count
E55E EXX
E55F LD B,A
blit_mirror_one_0 E560 EXX Pop two bytes: the first, mirrored, then up a row
E561 POP DE
E562 LD C,E
E563 LD A,(BC)
E564 LD (HL),A
E565 DEC H
E566 LD C,D The second, mirrored, then up a row
E567 LD A,(BC)
E568 LD (HL),A
E569 DEC H
E56A LD A,H Into the next character row up if this one is done
E56B INC A
E56C AND $07
E56E JR NZ,blit_mirror_one_1
E570 LD A,L
E571 SUB $20
E573 LD L,A
E574 JR C,blit_mirror_one_1
E576 LD A,H
E577 ADD A,$08
E579 LD H,A
blit_mirror_one_1 E57A EXX The next pair of rows
E57B DJNZ blit_mirror_one_0
E57D EXX Leave HL as the column for the next piece (after_step_drawn)
E57E JP after_step_drawn
