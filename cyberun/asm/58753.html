E581: Landscape graphics: the flat piece (direction 0)
Its three variants and its four end caps, each drawn as one step of direction 0 at shift 0 by the blitter piece_blitters_shift0 gives it (its clipping jumps set to draw every column, as flat_step_clip sets them for a step clear of the edges), in planet 0's surface colour (four times the size).
The terrain pieces' graphics as they came on the tape: the nine stored shapes unshifted (to the end of slope_3_gfx), each after a header, and the room after them for their copies shifted by 2, 4 and 6 pixels, which build_shifted_pieces makes at the start (from slope_2_shifted). The tables at piece_blitters_shift2 point into them. All are drawn bottom row first by the POP blitters, and stored as the blitters read them: each pair of rows left to right, then right to left. Every graphic holds three variants of its piece one above the other.
This one: a two-byte header for shift_flat_piece, then the flat piece, three bytes a row, 28 rows (84 bytes). The ground is the planet's checkerboard ($55 and $AA on alternate rows) with the surface's bumps at the top.
