EC60: Landscape graphics: the wall (direction 14)
Its three variants, each drawn as one step of direction 14 at shift 0 by the blitter piece_blitters_shift0 gives it, in planet 0's surface colour (four times the size).
Header (9, 29, 1), then three variants of 17 rows of one byte; the mirrored direction 15 reads it one byte on. Its last bytes are overwritten by the first shifted copy (wall_shifted) before the shifting reads them, so the partial fourth variant it makes is garbage -- harmless, as it is never drawn.
