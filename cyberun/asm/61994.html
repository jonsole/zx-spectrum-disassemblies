F22A: The menu frame record
The 17-byte record draw_frame draws the menu's frame from, given to it in IX by menu. The play area's frame is drawn from a record of the same shape by draw_panel.
menu_frame F22A DEFB $C0,$57,$DF,$57 The screen addresses of the tops of the left and right edges
F22E DEFB $B0 Their height in rows
F22F DEFB $E1,$56,$01,$46 The screen addresses of the top and bottom edges
F233 DEFW $0000 The corners (menu_frame_corners, set by menu)
F235 DEFB $06 The number of tick-mark pairs
F236 DEFW $0000 The tick marks (menu_frame_ticks, set by menu)
F238 DEFB $06 The y of the top tick marks
F239 DEFB $F8 The x of the right-hand ones
F23A DEFB $1F The length of the top and bottom edges, in bytes
