F23B: Stir the random numbers
Used by the routines at deal_places, menu_loop and play.
Called once a pass of the play loop (play), of the menu's loop (menu_loop) and from deal_places. The two random bytes of random_a are stirred with a byte read from a pointer that walks through memory (kept big-endian after them), and with the player's controls as they were last read (controls_now). Bits 4 and 6 of the pointer's high byte are cleared and it never reaches the top 20K, so the bytes come from the ROM and the game's own code.
