F311: Reset the play for a new life
Used by the routine at play.
Called by play at the start of every life (and of every level). Sets the ship's colour back to white, clears the death byte death_count, the hit-immunity timer shield_timer, the end-of-level byte launch_state, the hit count and two more flags, and empties or resets the game's tables: bit 0 of the first byte of 18 two-byte entries of star_layer_pointer, 96 bytes at shot_records, the first byte of the eight 5-byte entries of rocket_part_table, 16 bytes of beam_grace's table; then copies four records back from the starting copies that follow them (trail_frame, lift_jet_record, side_jet_record, and the 32 bytes of the ship's state in bare_ship), and resets with free_object the nine 24-byte records at object_records and the six 26-byte records at ground_objects.
Which of these tables is which is for the ranges that hold them; here they are named by address only.
