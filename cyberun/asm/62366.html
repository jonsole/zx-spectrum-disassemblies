F39E: Clear a player record for a new game
Used by the routine at new_game.
Zeroes the first 28 bytes of the record (score, flags, lives, add-ons, level), sets the rocket part carried (+26) to $FF (none), and copies the starting restart position from start_position into +19 to +24. The rest of the record (the control method at +28, and the table of the level's objects from +29) is set by new_game.
