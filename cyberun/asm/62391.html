F3B7: The restart position at the start of a game
Three words copied by clear_player_record into a player record's +19 to +24, the place in the landscape a life starts from (see messages_and_players): the values of view_column, view_x and origin_x to restore. Taken from those variables by save_restart_point when the restart position moves on, and put back by start_landscape.
The origins here are $78 and $40 against a view x and y of $90 and $B7: the x pair sums to 8 as origin_x says it always does, but the y pair sums to $F7, not $B7 (origin_y). A vertical move keeps the sum (scroll_landscape adds the step to the origin and takes it from the view's y), and only a wrap into the next cell sets the pair afresh, to $B7 and 0 or $17 and $A0; so from a new game's start the sum is $F7 until the fly-in's first vertical wrap. Why, and what the first passes look like, is not known (read only).
