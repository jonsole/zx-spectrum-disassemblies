F3BD: Set up a new game
Used by the routine at game.
Called by game after the menu. Clears bits 6 and 7 of the 290 bytes from map_object_kinds (mask_world_objects with E = $3F): each player's mark on the level's objects, bit 7 for player 1 and bit 6 for player 2 (see play). Clears both player records (clear_player_record) and gives each five lives; if the score lines were left the other way round by the last game (the byte at p1_lives_text is $A0) they are put back with swap_players, player_in_play being set to $99 meanwhile so that it does not also change the current player. Player 1 is current; with one player, player 2 has no lives. Each player's control method is copied from the menu, the two scores on the panel are set to six noughts ($1A is the font's 0), and each player's table of the level's objects is filled in by deal_places.
