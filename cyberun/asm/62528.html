F440: The play: a life, from its start to its end
Used by the routine at level_done.
Entered at the start of every life after the first: from its own death sequence once lose_life has taken a life, and from level_done at the end of a level. A new game enters further on, past the reset and the panel, from game. Resets the play (reset_for_life), goes back to the menu if the current player has no lives, redraws the panel, and then: puts back the ore (ore) the player had aboard, patches the BIT in spawn_objects_from_list and the SETs in ship_hits_ground_objects and beam_hits_ground_object that test and mark an object gone in map_object_kinds (one byte per object lying in the world) so that they use bit 7 for player 1 and bit 6 for player 2, and starts the level (start_landscape).
The play loop, once a pass (about 20 a second): ship_pass, the fuel gauge (update_fuel_gauge), spawn_tick, update_objects (counts the death byte up, and much else), the random numbers (random). It ends when the death byte death_count reaches 12, or when launch_state is $FF: the rocket, all eight parts built and the ship docked, has risen (level_done).
The death sequence: the landscape is stopped (scroll_down), the fuel is set to empty (fuel), part of draw_ship runs once and lift_jet erases the lift jet; then a shorter loop -- ship_pass, the pause key (check_pause), the fuel gauge, update_objects, carried_part, the gauge again -- runs until update_objects has counted the death byte up to $50. Then the death tune (death_tune), the play area dissolved (dissolve_and_clear), and a life is taken (lose_life) before going round again.
