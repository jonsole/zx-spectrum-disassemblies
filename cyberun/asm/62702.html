F4EE: Say which player is to play
Used by the routines at game, game_over and swap_players.
Puts the player's digit ($1B for 1, $1C for 2, from player_in_play) into the message at messages_and_players + 13 and prints it with print_string.
show_player F4EE LD A,(player_in_play)
F4F1 ADD A,$1B
F4F3 LD (player_digit),A
F4F6 LD DE,player_message
F4F9 JP print_string
