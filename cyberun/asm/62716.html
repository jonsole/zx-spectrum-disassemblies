F4FC: The current player's game is over
Used by the routine at lose_life.
Called by lose_life when the last life has gone. Prints the player's name and GAME OVER (both messages at messages_and_players), puts the score in the high score if it beats it (check_high_score), plays the game-over tune (game_over_tune), waits twelve delays or until 0 is pressed, clears the play area and, with two players, hands over to the other one (swap_players does nothing if that one has no lives either). play then finds the current player with no lives and goes back to the menu.
game_over F4FC CALL show_player PLAYER n, GAME OVER
F4FF LD DE,messages_and_players
F502 CALL print_string
F505 CALL check_high_score A new high score?
F508 LD DE,game_over_tune The game-over tune
F50B CALL play_tune
F50E LD B,$0C Wait twelve delays, or until 0 is pressed
game_over_0 F510 CALL delay
F513 LD A,$EF
F515 OUT ($FD),A
F517 IN A,($FE)
F519 AND $01
F51B JR Z,game_over_1
F51D DJNZ game_over_0
game_over_1 F51F CALL clear_play_area Clear the play area
F522 LD A,(player_count) Two players: the other one's turn, if they have lives
F525 AND A
F526 CALL NZ,swap_players
F529 RET
