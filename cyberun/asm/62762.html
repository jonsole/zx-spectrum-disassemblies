F52A: The end of a level: the rocket has risen
Used by the routine at play.
Jumped to from the play loop (play) when launch_state is $FF, after the rocket has been built and has risen. Clears the play area, shows the end of the level (planet_secured, which also counts the level on in the record, +18), plays its tune (level_tune), waits fourteen delays, clears the play area again and the player's three flag bytes (+3 to +5, see messages_and_players), and starts the next life's play at play.
The rocket's part of this is from the agent who read launch_state's setting; here, planet_secured is read counting the level on.
level_done F52A CALL clear_play_area Clear the play area; the end-of-level display
F52D CALL planet_secured
F530 LD DE,level_tune Its tune
F533 CALL play_tune
F536 LD B,$0E A pause
level_done_0 F538 CALL delay
F53B DJNZ level_done_0
F53D CALL clear_play_area Clear the play area
F540 XOR A Clear the record's three flag bytes, and play on
F541 LD (player_flag_bits),A
F544 LD ($F5EB),A
F547 LD ($F5EC),A
F54A JP play
