F591: Hand over to the other player
Used by the routines at new_game, lose_life and game_over.
Does nothing if the other player has no lives (+6 of the second record). Otherwise swaps the two player records, all 105 bytes, so that the current player is always the one at messages_and_players + 25 and the rest of the game never needs to know which; swaps the two score lines of the panel (p1_score_text and p2_score_text, 16 bytes each); and unless player_in_play is $99 (as new_game sets it while it puts the score lines in order) changes player_in_play to the other player, says PLAYER n (show_player), pauses eight delays and clears the play area.
