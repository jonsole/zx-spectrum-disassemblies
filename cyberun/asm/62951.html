F5E7: The current player's record: the score
The current player's record is 105 ($69) bytes from here, to other_player_record; swap_players swaps it with the other player's at a change of player, so the game only ever reads and writes this one. clear_player_record clears it for a new game and new_game fills in the rest. Each field is an entry of its own; +n below is the offset from here.
+0 to +2: the score, three BCD bytes, least significant first. test_player_shots adds BC to it.
