F5EA: The current player's destroyed beam generators (+3 to +5)
24 bits, one for each map square (map_square_bit picks the byte and the bit): set when the special beam destroys a beam generator there (object kinds 6 and 7, beam_hits_ground_object), and tested by draw_beams, which then draws no beam in that square. Cleared at the end of a level (level_done).
