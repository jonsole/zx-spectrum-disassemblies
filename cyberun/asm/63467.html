F7EB: Build the landscape pieces' shifted copies
Used by the routine at sprite_buffer_two.
Called once, at the start (build_shift_tables), with the shift tables from shift_tables in place. Clears the work bytes from scratch_count, 38 of them, and makes, for each of the nine stored terrain-piece graphics, its copies shifted right by 2, 4 and 6 pixels, one after another at the address given: with shift_slope_piece for the slopes and the wall, with shift_flat_piece for the two flat pieces. The three ceiling slopes are made with piece_step_x set, which pads them with the checkerboard on the left instead of the right.
The tape holds the copies' places as zeros (the generator ORs into them), except that the flat pieces' copies are only partly made here; the rest of them came on the tape (see shift_flat_piece). Each graphic starts with a header (read_piece_header), which is why the source addresses here are two or three bytes before those in the tables at piece_blitters_shift2.
This routine and the ones after it to shift_slope_piece's end run once. Afterwards their bytes are reused: a sprite-compositing buffer of up to 256 bytes starts 21 bytes into this routine, inside the operand of a CALL, so it cannot have a label of its own (copy_box_in copies into it with LDI and dissolve_sprite reads it back; old_beam_frame builds a sprite there and new_beam_frame another from its 128th byte, sprite_buffer_two), over the end of this routine and the ones up to bit_reverse_table; and from bit_reverse_table the bit-reversed table build_bit_reverse makes, over the rest.
