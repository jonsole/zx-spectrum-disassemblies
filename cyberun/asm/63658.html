F8AA: Shift a graphic byte into a row being made left to right
Used by the routines at shift_flat_piece and shift_slope_piece.
ORs the high part of the byte at HL into the byte at HL' and moves on to the next, then ORs the low part into that one: the byte that spills over is left there for the next byte's high part to join.
Input
HL the graphic byte
D the high page of the shift tables
HL' the copy being made
