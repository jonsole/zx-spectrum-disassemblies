F8C3: Fetch the low part of a graphic byte
Used by the routine at shift_flat_piece.
B = C first (the width), when entered here; at its second instruction just A = the low part of the byte at HL, its high part being left for the next call (shift_byte_high) to fetch.
Input
HL the graphic byte
D the high page of the shift tables
shift_byte_low F8C3 LD B,C B is the width
