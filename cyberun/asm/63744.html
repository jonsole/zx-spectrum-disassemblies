F900: Make a flat piece's shifted copies (continued); later, the bit-reversed table
The rest of shift_flat_piece. Once the start-up is over this is the bit-reversed table build_bit_reverse builds: bit_reverse_table + n holds n with its bits reversed, 256 bytes over the end of shift_flat_piece and all of shift_slope_piece, which have done their work by then. The game reads it with LD B,$F9 and the byte in C, to mirror the ship (mirror_ship_sprite), the left-climbing landscape steps (blit_mirror_two, blit_mirror_one) and other sprites (draw_ship, blit_mirrored_left_clip).
