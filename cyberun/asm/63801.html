F939: Make a slope piece's shifted copies
Used by the routine at build_shifted_pieces.
For build_shifted_pieces, the slopes and the wall. A slope is made one byte wider than its graphic, row by row, the rows alternating left to right and right to left like the blitters that draw them; the open side of each row is filled with the checkerboard's bits ($55 on rows made left to right, $AA on the others), on the right normally and on the left for the ceilings (piece_step_x set). The header's first count (row_count) cuts the zig-zag after each variant: a variant of 9 rows is 5 rows left to right and 4 back, so every variant begins with a row made left to right, as the blitter starting there expects.
The pieces' graphics are packed so tightly that each copy reads a few bytes past its graphic's end (into the next graphic's header, or into the first copy made), giving a fourth, partial variant that the game never draws.
