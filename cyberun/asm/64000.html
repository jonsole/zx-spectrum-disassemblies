FA00: The shift tables
Six pages made by build_shift_tables at the start: shift_tables + n is n shifted right 2 bits, the next page's byte n the bits that fall off (at the top of the byte); loader_copy and the page after it the same for 4 bits, loader_leftover_charset and the page after it for 6. Before the start they hold what the loader left: undecoded bytes to the end of the game block, then the copy routine loader_copy, the loader's stack, and a character set.
