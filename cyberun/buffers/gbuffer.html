Game status buffer
Address Label Length Description
5C15 saved_step_x 2
A saved step across
In the system variables' area, which the game has no use for, like all the variables from here to fuel_saver. Many of them are scratch: one routine's loop counter is another's flag, and routines in unrelated parts of the game share a byte. Each is named by its main use and its other users are listed. The snapshot's values are the BASIC system variables put through the loader's nibble shift, and mean nothing.
This word is saved by bounce_shallow_negate from a moving thing's record (+7 and +8, put through three_eighths) and added back further on in the same routine.
5C17 saved_step_y 3
A saved step down
The same as saved_step_x for the record's +9 and +10: saved and added back by bounce_shallow_negate. The byte after it is not used (searched).
5C1A touch_depth 2
How far a touch went in
Written by test_landscape_piece and test_slope_reversed, which test a part of an object against a slope's shape: how far into the shape the part is, made positive. leave_slope reads it, negated, as a new step to push the object back out. after_step_drawn also uses it as a flag between its own instructions. The byte after it is not used (searched).
5C1C scratch_count 1
The busiest scratch byte
Used by more than forty routines as a counter or a holding place. Among them: the slope tests (test_landscape_piece, test_slope_reversed) leave 1 in it for a touch, and load_map_window keeps in it the object kind it gives the pick-up places it puts into object slots ($0C for the first eleven, $1E for the last eight).
5C1D ground_cell 1
Which map cell is being drawn
Which of the four map cells draw_landscape is drawing, 0-3 (see ground_cell_top_left_rest): bit 0 set for the lower row, bit 1 for the right-hand column. Elsewhere a scratch byte (ground_object_fires, start_attacker, next_part, place_collision_box, draw_carried_piece and others).
5C1E row_count 1
A row count
Scratch, mostly a count of rows or lines: the shift-table builders (read_piece_header, next_shift, shift_slope_piece) count rows in it, reloading it from row_count_spare; start_attacker, object_box and test_player_shots keep a line in it; draw_beams reads it.
5C1F row_count_spare 1
The row count's spare
The copy row_count is reloaded from (read_piece_header, next_shift, shift_slope_piece); also scratch for start_attacker and toggle_carried_part.
5C20 pieces_left 2
Pieces left in the tile
The number of pieces left to draw in the map cell's tile: draw_landscape sets it from the tile's record and after_step_drawn counts it down. Also scratch for toggle_carried_part, next_part_boxes, hit_slope, downhill and shift_flat_piece. The byte after it is not used (searched).
5C22 piece_steps 2
Steps in the piece
The number of steps in the piece being drawn: the low nibble of its third byte, 16 when that is 0 (draw_landscape); read again by flat_step_clip. The byte after it is not used (searched).
5C24 piece_step_y 1
The piece's step down
The piece's step in y, from its direction (load_piece_steps, given the sign of the step in x); used by after_step_drawn and fill_behind_moving_piece. Also written by landscape_end_pieces, and by colour_piece as a width less one.
5C25 piece_step_x 1
The piece's step across
The size of the piece's step in x (load_piece_steps), added to its x by piece_ends and draw_landscape. Also a mode byte of the collision routines (collide_with_landscape, next_box, hit_block, hit_slope, leave_slope, ship_on_slope_response, place_collision_box), written by move_bouncing, move_or_explode, update_shot, ship_pass and build_shifted_pieces and read by shift_slope_piece.
5C26 tile_upside_down 1
The tile is upside down
$99 while the tile being drawn is turned upside down (its record starts $99), 0 otherwise (draw_landscape). load_piece_steps then swaps each piece's direction between floor and ceiling through the second half of the table at mirror_table, and piece_ends turns its y over (XOR $0C) and its extra bytes (XOR 1). Floors become ceilings: the caverns.
5C27 piece_kept 1
A step kept for the column fill
Cleared for each piece by draw_landscape; set by flat_step_clip when it keeps a step's address and x for the fill behind a piece; read by fill_flat_end_column, which then uses the kept step.
5C28 sprite_width 1
A width in bytes
Scratch, as a byte and as a word with sprite_width_drawn. The sprite routines keep a width less one in bytes here (draw_sprite_pair copies it and sprite_width_drawn before turning them into pixels); also used by set_old_sprite, set_direct_old, clear_sprite_pair, draw_direct_pair, direct_erase_row, collect_pickup, mirror_ship_sprite, carried_part, draw_carried_piece and draw_ship.
5C29 sprite_width_drawn 1
The width a sprite was drawn with
The width a sprite was drawn with, kept to rub it out (set_new_sprite, set_direct_new, draw_direct_pair, direct_draw_row, colour_sprite); the high byte of sprite_width as a word. Also read by ship_contact, object_box, set_mirrored_drawer and draw_carried_piece.
5C2A stick_out_right 1
How far a box sticks out to the right
Kept by place_collision_box, the collision box's placement, for the part that sticks out past the right of the page into the next (read by part_right and next_part).
5C2B stick_out_up 1
How far a box sticks out above
The same for what sticks out above the page's top (place_collision_box, read by part_above and next_part).
5C2C piece_x 1
The x of the piece's step
Moved on by the step in x at each step of a piece (landscape_step_loop); also used by draw_sprite_pair, dissolve_sprite, buffer_address, part_above, part_right, place_collision_box, slope_step_at_left_edge, flat_step_clip and step_screen_address.
5C2D piece_y 1
The y of the piece's step
Moved by the step in y at each step of a piece (landscape_step_loop); also used by buffer_address, part_above, part_right, next_part, place_collision_box, flat_step_clip, draw_flat_end_lip and step_screen_address.
5C2E move_across_kept 1
A move across
With piece_steps_done, a word: the move across that place_collision_box keeps (beside moved_x) and part_right and next_part read. slope_step_at_left_edge writes this byte as one of its own, a count of bytes to skip.
5C2F piece_steps_done 1
Steps of the piece drawn
How many steps of the piece have been drawn: cleared for each piece by draw_landscape and counted up by after_step_drawn, which draws nothing more for a piece with none; also read by edge_column, draw_flat_end_lip and step_screen_address. Its other use is as the high byte of move_across_kept.
5C30 tile_mirrored_left_right 1
The tile is mirrored left-right
$FF while the tile being drawn is mirrored left-right (its record has an $FF before its piece count), 0 otherwise (draw_landscape). piece_ends then reflects each piece's x, swaps its two extra bytes (XOR 2) and turns its direction round through the first half of the table at mirror_table. Also the low byte of a move down place_collision_box keeps for part_above and next_part.
5C31 edge_flags 1
The view's edge flags
Which of the view's edges a step or an object has crossed: cleared at each step by landscape_step_loop, set and tested by the clipping routines column_at_view_bottom, column_at_view_top, column_at_view_left and column_at_view_right; also written by prepare_draw and read by steer_to_view.
5C32 piece_clip 1
The piece reverses
Set by column_at_view_bottom and column_at_view_top when a piece's steps reverse at an edge, cleared for each piece by draw_landscape, read by fill_behind_moving_piece.
5C33 piece_extra_a 1
A flat piece's first extra byte
The first of the two extra bytes a piece of direction 0 or 7 carries (piece_ends; with the tile mirrored left-right it is the second, XOR 2; upside down, XOR 1). Read by flat_step_clip and fill_flat_end_column, and by landscape_end_pieces and first_piece_shift as a word with piece_extra_b.
5C34 piece_extra_b 1
A flat piece's second extra byte
The second; a $16 is left as it is by both reversals. Read by colour_piece, flat_step_clip, draw_flat_end_lip, fill_flat_end_column, fill_behind_moving_piece and last_piece_width.
5C35 piece_ceiling 1
The piece is a ceiling
$80 when the piece's direction is 7 or more, the ceiling shapes (set_piece_shape); cleared for each piece by draw_landscape; read by after_step_drawn and fill_behind_moving_piece. The collision routines (next_box, hit_block, hit_slope, place_collision_box) also keep in it what has happened this pass, bit 7 a block hit and bit 4 a slope.
5C36 steer_push 1
Which way the ship was pushed
Cleared by move_ship every pass and set there from the controls: $80 pushed left, 3 right, 0 not. draw_ship turns the ship's facing towards it; accelerate_across reads it too.
5C37 controls_now 1
This pass's controls
In the Kempston layout (bit 0 right, 1 left, 2 down, 3 up, 4 fire), as read_controls leaves them; move_ship forces 8 (thrust) while the stage is ending. Read by fire, special_beam_key, end_special_beam, draw_ship, lift_jet, and by random, which stirs it into the random number.
5C38 ship_line 1
The ship's line
The ship's line on the screen, the line its shots fly along: set by build_ship_sprite from the ship's record (+$1E, less the ship's height, plus 9) and by toggle_carried_part; read by ship_contact, draw_beams, update_shot, fire, place_shot, carried_part, draw_carried_piece, draw_ship, lift_jet and start_landscape.
5C39 gun_cooling 1
The gun's cooling count
Set to 6 by fire when it fires a shot and counted down there every pass; it fires again only once the count is 0. draw_ship clears it.
5C3A map_row_colour 1
A colour for the view's page row
Written by set_landscape_colours from one of two bytes kept with the view's four tile numbers (see load_map_window), chosen by whether the map row is above 12 or not; read by colour_piece.
5C3B map_row_colour_next 1
A colour for the page row below
The same as map_row_colour for the row below; read by colour_piece.
5C3C map_rebuild 1
The view is being built
Nonzero while the view is being built afresh rather than played: $FF from start_landscape at the start of a life, 3 from fly_in_step, 0 when play goes on. While it is set draw_beams returns at once and load_map_window puts no objects into slots; with bit 7 set, load_map_window draws map tile $3E as tile $AA (two of its six pieces). draw_landscape copies a nonzero value to record_ground.
5C3D record_ground 1
Record the collision boxes
Set from map_rebuild by draw_landscape and by scroll_landscape: while it is nonzero, draw_landscape writes a collision box for each piece it draws into the map cell's list (ground_cell_top_left_rest), so the ground's boxes are made when the view is built rather than at every pass.
5C3E top_speed 1
The ship's top speed across
2, or 4 when +$0A of the current player's record (score) (an add-on) is set (build_ship_sprite); read by draw_ship, accelerate_across and clamp_speed_across, which hold the speed to it.
5C3F top_climb 1
The ship's top climb
2, or 4 when +$0B of the record (an add-on) is set (build_ship_sprite); set to 4 by move_ship while the stage is ending, and read there and by clamp_climb.
5C40 fuel_saver 1
Halve the fuel burn
Set to $FF by ship_contact, and every pass copied away (to last_pass_contact) and cleared by update_objects; read by move_ship and ship_on_slope_response. While it is set the fuel burn at the end of read_controls is halved (again, after any halving for +$0C of the current player's record).
8790 spawn_countdown 1
Count down to the next attacker
spawn_tick counts it down each pass: at 7 a cloud may start (spawn_cloud), at 0 an attacker (spawn_attacker), and it starts again from 8.
8791 attacker_records 1
How many records attackers may use
Always 4: spawn_attacker looks for a free record among the first four object records (object_records). Nothing writes it.
87F5 meteor_shower 1
The meteor-shower timer
0 none; otherwise the passes left (from 128) of a meteor shower (spawn_tick), during which start_attacker aims rocks and comets near the ship. Cleared by reset_for_life.
896E extra_life_countdown 1
Throws left before the next extra life
Counted down by ground_object_fires at each bullet a ground object throws (kind 1); at 0 that throw is the extra-life icon and the count starts again from the current lives x 4.
896F shield_timer 1
The shield timer
0 off; set to $FF when the ship touches the shield ball (ship_contact), counted down every other pass by ship_pass. While it is not zero the ship takes no damage (contact_damage); the ship flashes in its last $20 (colour_ship). Cleared at a new game.
89D2 attacker_kind 1
The kind of attacker being started
0-9, an entry of the wave table wave_table: set by start_attacker (from the wave counter there) and spawn_cloud (1, a cloud), and 8 or 9 for the meteor shower's rocks and comets.
8C03 speed_limit 1
The speed limit while accelerating
Set by accelerate from the record's top speed, read by limit_speed.
8C04 speed_limit_down 1
The negative speed limit; the spawn's tries
Set by accelerate to minus the top speed, minus one, and read by limit_speed. start_attacker also borrows it to count the screens it tries.
8CB1 last_pass_contact 1
Last pass's cloud-contact flag
update_objects copies fuel_saver here each pass before clearing it; fuel_saver is set when a cloud touches what the ship carries (ship_contact). While it is set contact_slow_ship does nothing.
8D3C rocket_parts_load 1
The rocket parts' share of the load
Half the number of rocket parts delivered, rounded up, while draw_rocket is drawing them, else 0. update_objects starts each pass's load (object_load) from it.
8E2B object_load 1
The load
Worked out afresh each pass by update_objects: the rocket parts' share (rocket_parts_load), one for each record in use, one more for each drawn last pass (count_and_erase) and one more for a big cloud (update_cloud). The spawners read it: update_objects spawns below 4, spawn_tick not at 8 or more, find_free_record not at 7 or more.
8E2C contact_effect 1
The current object's contact effect
What the current object does to the ship when they touch, one bit per effect: set to $50 for each object by update_objects, changed by its update routine, and acted on by ship_contact. Bit 7 slows the ship (contact_slow_ship), 6 damages it (contact_damage), 5 refuels (contact_refuel), 4 pushes (contact_push), 3 collects the object (contact_collect), 2 drains fuel (contact_drain_fuel), 1 gives a life (contact_extra_life), 0 destroys the object (contact_destroy); $FF is a shield, and 0 is harmless.
8F53 death_count 1
The death byte
0 while the ship is alive. A third hit, from an attacker or a beam (contact_damage), or an empty fuel gauge (check_fuel_empty) sets it to 1, and update_objects counts it up by one a pass. 1-3: every object explodes (update_objects). 10: the ship breaks up into pieces (wreck_ship). 12 and over: the play loop runs the death sequence (play), which ends at $50. While it is not zero nothing touches the ship (ship_contact) and nothing new is spawned (find_free_record).
draw_rocket sets it to 10 for the length of two calls when the ship docks with the finished rocket, and puts it back to 0 at once: that is not a death.
8FC3 hit_count 1
Hits taken by this ship
0-3, counted up by each hit (contact_damage); the third kills the ship. Cleared for a new ship (reset_for_life), and set to $FF by a refuel pad's repair (ship_hits_ground_objects), so that the damage code wraps it to 0.
96AB launch_state 1
The launch
0 in play. Set non-zero when the ship docks with the finished rocket (draw_rocket): the rocket rises, nothing touches the ship (ship_contact, beam_hits_ship), the controls are off (ship_pass). $FF when the rocket has left the screen, which ends the play loop (play). Cleared for a new game (reset_for_life).
9932 wave_counter 1
The wave counter
The entry of wave_table that start_attacker spawns next: stepped from 0 to 5, or to 7 when two flags in the player record (score) are both set.
9A64 pass_counter 2
The pass counter
A word, counted up once a pass by ship_pass (and by the menu's loop). Its low bits time things that happen every other pass, every fourth or every eighth (the shield, the engine sounds, type $19's hits), and bit 0 picks the colour of a flashing sprite (prepare_draw).
9BCB random_a 1
A random byte
The first of the two random bytes the game reads (each attacker's choices, the beams, the drops), stepped once a pass by random: the byte at random_pointer's pointer and the controls (controls_now) are added in. The routines that want more randomness also read R.
9BCC random_b 1
A second random byte
Stepped with random_a by random, from the complement of the same byte.
9BCD random_pointer 2
The random number's pointer
A pointer, high byte first, that random steps through memory once a pass (its high byte ANDed with $AF), so that the bytes it reads, code and data, stir the random bytes.
9BD6 beam_state 4
The beams' pattern, colour and timer
For draw_beams: the bit pattern every beam is drawn with this pass (0 while they are off), the colour (bright, ink 1-7), the passes left before the next change, and (the last byte) whether a beam was drawn this pass, for the sound.
9D2F beam_grace 1
The beams' grace count
Counts down the passes after a beam has hurt the ship (beam_hits_ship), set to 16; while it is not zero draw_beams tests no beam against the ship. Cleared for a new ship (reset_for_life).
A1D3 old_sprite_x 1
The old image's screen x
The sprite compositor (draw_sprite_pair) handles a pair of images at a time: the image an object was drawn as last pass (the old, to be rubbed out) and the image it is to be drawn as now (the new). These six bytes, old_sprite_x to new_sprite_rows, hold them. This one is the old image's x on the screen, set by prepare_erase from the record's +$11 (and by draw_star_layer for a star), and read with the next byte as a word.
A1D4 old_sprite_y 1
The old image's screen y
The line of the old image's bottom row, from the record's +$12, clipped to the play area's lines $18-$B7 by clip_sprite_rows through old_sprite_header.
A1D5 new_sprite_x 1
The new image's screen x
Set by place_on_screen when it places an object on the screen, and kept in the record's +$11 for the next pass's erase.
A1D6 new_sprite_y 1
The new image's screen y
The line of the new image's bottom row: set by place_on_screen, clipped by clip_sprite_rows through new_sprite_header. trim_colour_patch moves it up afterwards for the colour patch alone.
A1D7 old_sprite_rows 1
The old image's height
Rows to rub out, after clipping; 0 when there is no old image (cleared by clear_sprite_pair, set by set_old_sprite). Others read it to tell whether an object was on the screen last pass (collect_pickup, attacker_fires, update_objects).
A1D8 new_sprite_rows 1
The new image's height
Rows to draw, after clipping; 0 when there is no new image (cleared by clear_sprite_pair, set by set_new_sprite). trim_colour_patch trims it for the colour patch, which colour_sprite reads.
A289 direct_old_line 1
The direct drawer's old image's bottom line
Set by set_direct_old or draw_ship; draw_direct_pair compares it with direct_new_line to line the two images up, then reuses it for the old image's rows left over.
A28A direct_new_line 1
The direct drawer's new image's bottom line
Set by set_direct_new or draw_ship; reused by draw_direct_pair for the new image's rows left over.
A2F8 old_sprite_width 1
The old image's width in bytes
Kept by draw_sprite_pair before it turns the working copies of both widths (sprite_width, sprite_width_drawn) into pixels; read by erase_old_draw_new. Written as a word with new_sprite_width.
A2F9 new_sprite_width 1
The new image's width in bytes
Kept by draw_sprite_pair; read by erase_old_draw_new and dissolve_sprite.
A2FA sprite_left_clip 1
The compositor's left-clip flag
Not zero while the pair is drawn straddling the left edge of the play area: set by place_on_screen for the new image and by prepare_erase (from +$10 bit 6) for the old, cleared by clear_sprite_pair. draw_sprite_pair then works with both x positions $60 less, and copies only the part of the box right of the edge. object_box and colour_sprite read it too.
A302 dissolve_step 1
The new image's dissolve step
0, or bit 7 and the step 1-7 while the object dissolves: set by prepare_draw from the record's +$17, read by dissolve_sprite.
ABA4 collision_box 2
The collision box's place
A box for boxes_overlap, with collision_box_size: x, then the y of its bottom row. Set from a ground object or pick-up (ship_hits_ground_objects, collect_pickup), an object for the shot tests (object_box), or the ship (draw_beams) before a test.
ABA6 collision_box_size 2
The collision box's size
Width in pixels, then height in rows, of the box at collision_box.
AD46 current_object 2
The object record being drawn
IX of the object whose images are being worked on (prepare_draw), for the routines after the drawing that need it back (ship_hits_ground_objects, object_box, update_ground_objects).
AF01 colour_patch 3
A colour patch: width and height in cells, and the attribute
The record fill_attributes paints from: the width in cells, the height in cells, and the attribute. colour_sprite works out the first two for a sprite; prepare_draw sets the attribute for an object (one of its record's two colours, alternating every pass), draw_shot to $43 for the special beam, colour_carried_parts to $47.
B320 climb_fraction 2
The ship's vertical position, in 256ths
move_ship adds the climb speed to it every pass; the change in its whole part, doubled, is the step up. Zeroed while the special beam holds the ship (move_ship) and by a bounce off a floor or ceiling (hit_block).
B730 shot_alternator 1
Which shot comes next
Bit 0 flips with every shot fired (fire): with add-on 8 every other shot is a missile. special_beam_key sets bit 2 for the one call that launches the special beam.
B832 special_beam_on 1
The special beam is out
$80 while the special beam lasts (fire), 0 when it has gone (draw_shot). While it is set the ship takes only fire and the special key (read_controls) and hangs still (move_ship), and special_beam_key watches for the key being let go.
BA09 collision_object 2
The record being tested against the landscape
collide_with_landscape keeps IX here while IX walks the lists of boxes.
BA8D saved_speed 2
A word kept for a moment
leave_slope keeps an object's speed across here while it pushes the object out of a slope; the sprite code at erase_old_draw_new keeps a screen address here; landscape_end_pieces reads it too.
BC12 ship_on_slope 1
The ship was on a slope last pass
$80 after the ship touches a slope (ship_on_slope_response), cleared when it goes through a whole test without stopping (next_box). A second pass on a slope slides the ship 2 pixels downhill.
BE8D last_speed_across 1
The speed across of the record being tested
Set by place_collision_box: the whole part of the record's speed across (for the ship, last pass's). hit_block tests a wall only if the record is moving into it, and updates this after a bounce.
BE8E last_speed_up 1
The climb speed of the record being tested, negated
Set by place_collision_box: the whole part of the record's speed up and down (+9; for the ship, last pass's climb speed), negated. The collision code works with up positive -- the objects' speeds, kept the other way round, are turned round for it (negate_velocity) -- so this is positive while the record falls. hit_block tests a floor only while it is 0 or more and a ceiling only while it is 0 or less, and updates it after a bounce.
BF7F moved_x 2
A record's place across after its move
The x and the page across of a record's box after this pass's move, or of the part being tested (place_collision_box, part_right, next_part).
BF81 moved_y 2
A record's place down after its move
The line and the page down of a record's box after this pass's move, or of the part being tested (place_collision_box, part_above, next_part).
BF83 sprite_screen_pos 2
Where the ship's picture is drawn
The screen x and line the ship's picture (or a part drawn with it) is drawn at, set by draw_ship and draw_carried_piece; mirror_ship_sprite moves the x for a mirrored one.
C0E6 star_layer_pointer 2
The current star layer's offsets
Used by draw_stars and draw_star_layer: the address of the current layer's two offsets in star_offsets, moved on by two for each layer.
C0E8 star_pointer 2
The current star
Used by draw_star_layer and star_in_sight: the address of the current star in star_positions, moved on by two for each star.
C1A9 ship_height 1
The ship's height
In rows: 14 for the bare ship, more with parts fitted. Set by build_ship_sprite when it builds the ship's image, and changed by 5 by toggle_carried_part when a rocket part is hung under the ship or let go (bit 6 of part_being_fitted); read by draw_ship_image, ship_pass and the collision code.
C1AA ship_graphic 2
The ship's graphic
The address of the ship's image, which build_ship_sprite builds up from the bare ship (bare_ship) and the parts fitted, in a buffer after the player records; toggle_carried_part moves it to take in or leave out a hanging rocket part (bit 6 of part_being_fitted, not the facing). Read by draw_ship_image.
C279 carried_part_facing 1
The facing the carried load was drawn with
Used by carried_part and draw_carried_piece: the ship's facing byte (ship_facing) when the carried load was last drawn, 0 if it has not been; bit 6 set when the load is let go. Cleared when the load is picked up (collect_pickup).
C27A carried_part_redraw 1
Force the carried load to be redrawn
Non-zero to make the next pass erase and redraw the carried load (draw_carried_piece): set by draw_ship_image when a part is fitted, and by draw_rocket and reset_for_life; cleared by carried_part.
C27B carried_big_x 1
The carried load's larger piece: screen x
Where draw_carried_piece last drew the 5-byte-wide piece of the carried load: x here, y in carried_big_y. Read as a word by colour_carried_parts for its colour patch.
C27C carried_big_y 1
The carried load's larger piece: screen y
See carried_big_x.
C27D carried_small_x 1
The carried load's smaller piece: screen x
Where draw_carried_piece last drew the 3-byte-wide piece: x here, y in carried_small_y.
C27E carried_small_y 1
The carried load's smaller piece: screen y
See carried_small_x.
C6D0 lift_jet_skip 2
Skip the lift jet for a pass
Set by hit_block; makes lift_jet erase the lift jet without drawing it again, for one pass. The byte after it is not used.
C7BC trail_frame 1
The trail's frame
The graphic number (1-6, trail_images) of the ship's trail as last drawn by draw_trail. With the next five bytes it is the trail's record, reset each life from trail_start.
C7BD trail_x 1
The trail's screen x
$68, or $78 facing left (draw_trail).
C7BE trail_y 1
The trail's screen y
Its bottom row (draw_trail); lower while a part is being fitted.
C7BF trail_direction 1
The trail's direction
Bit 7 set while the ship's speed across is positive, or under half a pixel; otherwise clear and the trail is drawn mirrored (draw_trail, trail_mirror).
C7C0 ship_turned 1
The ship turned this pass
Bit 7 set by draw_ship_image on a pass the ship turns round; it makes draw_trail erase and redraw the trail.
C7C1 trail_hold 1
Hold the trail straight

CC4B fuel 2
The fuel
A word, its high byte the gauge's level as a screen row: $42 full, $BE empty, so burning fuel makes it larger. The row the gauge's fuel column reaches on the screen. update_fuel_gauge moves it one row a pass towards the fuel's level, so the gauge fills up at the start of a life from $BE (set by start_landscape) and drains one row at a time.

Added to a page position to give the screen y. With view_y it sums to $B7: the scroll moves one up as the other comes down, and the wraps set both afresh.
