No damage
Every hit -- an attacker, a bullet, a rock, a flickering beam -- goes through damage_ship, the second half of contact_damage, at $8F76: it counts the hit in hit_count and kills on the third. A RET as its first instruction and nothing is counted. A refuel pad repairs through the same routine (see the facts), so with the poke a pad leaves the count at $FF instead of nought; nothing else counts it up, so that does no harm. Tested: from the first pass of a new game, build_cyberun's round of keys (thrust, steering both ways, fire) twelve times over, 124 seconds of play, with the tank refilled between slices so that only hits could kill. Without the poke the ship died on its third hit five times, 6.7, 23.5, 37.2, 48.5 and 72 seconds in, and the game was over at 79.7 seconds; with it, no hit was counted and the five lives were still there at the end.

No fuel burned
read_controls burns fuel on every pass the ship steers or thrusts: 8 (16 for both at once, halved with add-on 6) added to fuel through add_fuel, from $B149. A RET there and the burn never happens. The pick-ups and pads still work: a refuel pick-up or pad fills the tank, and a fuel thief still takes its 16 steps. Tested: the same round of keys 24 times, 247 seconds, with the hits undone between slices so that only the fuel could kill. Without the poke the tank ran dry and the ship died 155.4 seconds in; with it, no death, and the gauge moved only for four fuel thieves' drains, to $82 at most (it reads $42 full, $BE empty).

Infinite lives
lose_life takes a life with the DEC A at $F4CF, stores it, and goes to game_over on a zero. The obvious poke, a NOP for the DEC (POKE 62671,0), keeps the lives but not the game: the JR Z then tests the flags the caller left, and the death sequence's last call, clear_play_area, ends on a DEC C that reaches nought -- so every death shows GAME OVER, plays its tune and checks the high score before play goes on (game_over returns, and the lives are not nought). OR A in place of the DEC leaves the lives as they are and sets the flags from them, never nought. Tested: seven deaths in a row by emptying the tank, from a new game. Without a poke the lives went 4, 3, 2, 1, 0, and the fifth death was the game over. With OR A, five lives after each of the seven and game_over never ran, a death taking 7.6 seconds to the next ship. With the NOP, five lives too, but game_over ran at every death and each took 13.6 seconds.

No attackers, no meteor showers
spawn_attacker finds a free record among the first four and calls start_attacker, which takes the next kind from the wave table -- or, during a meteor shower, a rock or a comet -- and places it. A RET as its first instruction and the record is left free. Clouds still come, through spawn_cloud, which goes straight to the placing code, and the ground objects still throw their bullets and pick-ups (read). Tested: the round of keys six times, 62 seconds, refuelled and repaired. Without the poke 38 things started in the attackers' four records: 7 pillars, 5 eye pods, 5 domes, one tough attacker, 11 clouds, and 9 rocks and comets of a meteor shower. With it, none; a meteor shower began and brought nothing, and 47 clouds came in the clouds' own two records.
POKE 35283,201
Harmless beams
A flickering beam that touches the ship's box damages it through beam_hits_ship. A RET as its first instruction and the beams are only lines. (The no-damage poke makes them harmless too, since they hurt through damage_ship; this one leaves every other kind of hit as it was.) Tested with the attackers stopped in both trials (the poke above), the ship hovering for 600 passes where a beam crosses its line -- a new life by the game's own restart at map column 44, row 8, with tile $A6 below -- and the hits undone each pass: without the poke the beam touched the ship 27 times, each a hit; with it, the beams were drawn on 465 of the 600 passes and no hit was counted.
POKE 40206,201
A shield that lasts
The shield ball sets shield_timer to $FF, and ship_pass counts it down on odd passes with the DEC A at $C385; while it is not nought no hit counts, and an attacker that touches the ship is destroyed. A NOP for the DEC and a shield once picked up lasts the rest of the life (each new life clears it). Tested: the timer set to $FF as the pick-up sets it, at the end of a pass, then 1200 passes of the round of keys with the tank refilled. Without the poke it ran out after 509 passes, and three hits and a death followed; with it, it was still $FF after 1200 passes, and no hit counted.

No gun cooling
fire sets gun_cooling to 6 with each shot and counts it down a pass at a time before the next. Make the 6 a nought and the gun fires on every pass that fire is held and one of the four shot records is free. Tested: 420 passes holding fire from the start of a game: 60 shots without the poke, one every seven passes; 120 with it, as many as four records allow.
POKE 46918,0
Rocket parts without the hook
collect_pickup takes a rocket part only with add-on 1, the hook, fitted: the RET Z at $AAE2. A NOP there and the part due next is taken without it. But the ship's picture is not ready for it: toggle_carried_part sets the picture to start at the first row of the buffer whenever a part hangs, which is right only for a ship as tall as the hook makes it. Without the hook the picture drawn is the part and the empty rows above it, and the ship vanishes, leaving the part hanging in the air; after the part is delivered it starts 30 bytes on, still empty, until a new life or an add-on rebuilds it (build_ship_sprite). Tested: rocket part 0 put in the second pick-up record under the middle of the ship as it stands on its pad at the start, as load_map_window fills one. Without the poke it lay there for 100 passes; with it, it was taken on pass 2, as it was with the hook fitted and no poke. Carried without the hook from a new life over the rocket's place and let go there, the part left the ship's picture blank.
Drawn by the game's own code in SkoolKit's simulator, from the screen, the ship on its pad at the start: part 0 taken with the poke and no hook, and without the poke with the hook.
POKE 43746,0
The special beam without add-on 7
special_beam_key launches the special beam when the special key is pressed (the number row on the keyboard), add-on 7 is fitted and the ship is still. A NOP for the RET Z that tests the add-on, and the beam needs only the key and a still ship. Tested: a new life in open sky at map column 30, row 5, by the game's own restart, holding 1 from the first pass. Without the poke, nothing. With it, the beam came out on pass 2, grew a cell every other pass to 8 cells by pass 17 and began to shrink on pass 24 -- pass for pass what add-on 7 gives without the poke.
POKE 47171,0
Deliver the ore tank empty
drop_part lets a carried part go over the rocket's place, but part 6, the ore tank, only with 18 ore aboard: the JR NZ at $9740 skips the ore test for the other parts. Make it a JR and the tank goes like any other part. It falls with the full tank's picture (frame 8 is chosen by the part's number, further on) and the ore aboard is emptied. Taking it off the ship's picture XORs out the empty tank where the full one would be, so with more than five units aboard some rows of ore stay drawn under the hook. Tested: a new life by the game's own restart in the sky over the rocket's place, carrying the tank with six parts built. With the tank empty and no poke it was kept for 150 passes, the ship at x $5C throughout; with the poke it was let go on pass 2, fell with frame 8 and was counted the seventh part on pass 10 -- just as a full tank went without the poke. With 12 units aboard and the poke, seven rows of ore stayed in the picture under the hook; with five, none.
POKE 38720,24
Lives at a new game
new_game gives each player five lives with the LD A,5 at $F3CE. Poke its operand with n from 1 to 9; the panel shows one digit, the lives less the ship in play. Tested: the lives at the first pass of a new game were 5 without the poke, and 9 and 1 with it set to 9 and to 1.
POKE 62415,n
Play on a 128K in 128 mode
Every keyboard read writes the half-rows it is about to read to port $FD first, which pages memory on a 128K (see the bugs). Five of the 15 reads can be given a value with bit 7 clear -- the menu's B, the pause test, the keyboard method's two reads of the bottom right half-row, and read_keyboard, which the title tune calls with nought and a joystick's special key with $7E -- and the IN after each selects the half-rows by itself, so their five OUTs can go: two NOPs each. Tested in SkoolKit's 128K machine, the game put in RAM as a 128 in 128 mode leaves a machine-code program (the 48 BASIC ROM and bank 0 paged in, paging unlocked): without the poke the game crashed on the menu's first pass (see the bugs); with it, the menu, a new game and a round of play, with no write to the paging port. On a 48K the poked game steered, thrust, fired and paused as before.
