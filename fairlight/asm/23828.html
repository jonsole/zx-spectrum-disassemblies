5D14: Frames 1 and 2 of object type 48, striking
Two more 24 by 24 sprites, image then mask like every sprite, in the style of SPRITE5C84 (type 48's own sprite), which they follow directly: the same winged creature in two other poses.
They are its strike. When the knight comes within reach in front of one of these creatures, the object dispatcher (CHE3D, at the author's I4) takes 3 from LIFE and steps the creature's frames from SPRITE5C84 on, 144 bytes each (the animation step, the author's ANIM, inside KNIGHT_CONTROLS). Bits 3-5 of a type 48 object's +$11 give its last frame, and every one has $10 there (measured on those in room 45): frames 0 to 2, SPRITE5C84 and these two. The strike never happened in the build's sessions (that code never ran), nor in half a minute of play in room 45 tried for this description, so no object showed these frames and no generated entry lays them out. CHE3D describes the strike.
