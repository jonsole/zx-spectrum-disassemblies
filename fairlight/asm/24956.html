617C: Leftover source text, and the game's stack
Part of the game's own source code, as text, left in memory when the tape was made: numbered lines (a two-byte line number, the text with tabs between the fields, a carriage return), here lines 6240-6670. They are the source of the end of the pick-up code in PICK_UP_LOOK_ON (the author's WEI3, and calls of routines he called ROMM, DOE, SRP and INFOR: SET_THING_ROOM, the instruction three bytes before it, REDRAW_OBJECT and SHOW_THING_IN_USE) and the start of the joystick and keyboard reading (KNIGHT_CONTROLS, his I88 onwards); the text goes on at MASTER_OBJECTS. The game never reads it.
The top of it is the game's stack: START sets SP two bytes below the block's end, and in the build's sessions the stack reached down 150 bytes below that.
