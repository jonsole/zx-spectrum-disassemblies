C000: Play the loading tune until a key is pressed
Used by the routine at START.
Called once, from START, while the loading screen is still up: two voices on the beeper, a note at a time (TUNE_PLAY_NOTE), with the ROM's KEY-SCAN between notes. The voices' pointers are set from TUNE_VOICES first, so the tune starts from its beginning. The tune lasts 52.6 seconds (418 note bytes a voice, of which the last three rest in both and are passed over, so 415 notes heard, each 0.127 s; measured for the sounds page); after it both voices rest, and the routine goes on scanning the keyboard in silence.
Interrupts are off while it plays (KEY-SCAN does not need them); it turns them on as it returns, and START turns them off again three instructions later, for good.
From the first room on, these bytes are part of the clean copy of the screen (RESTOR copies the screen here), so the tune cannot be played again.
LOADING_TUNE C000 LD HL,TUNE_VOICES Both voices to the start of the tune: the four pointers from TUNE_VOICES.
C003 LD DE,VOICE_ONE_AT
C006 LD BC,$0008
C009 LDIR
C00B DI Interrupts off while it plays.
LOADING_TUNE_0 C00C CALL TUNE_PLAY_NOTE Play a note (once both voices are resting, nothing).
C00F CALL $028E KEY-SCAN leaves E at $FF while no key is pressed.
C012 LD A,$FF
C014 CP E
C015 JP Z,LOADING_TUNE_0
C018 EI A key.
C019 RET
