DFF2: Print the closing lines of the quest
Used by the routine at ROOMST.
The author's MESS2: the source text at SOURCE_AFTER_OBJECTS calls MESS2 and jumps to WAIT at the end of ROOMST, just as the code there calls this and jumps to WAIT.
