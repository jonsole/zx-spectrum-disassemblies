EC75: Clear the panel's box for the thing in use, and number it
Used by the routines at SHOW_THING_IN_USE, CREATURE_UPDATE and MAIN_LOOP.
The author's INFO0 (named by its calls in the leftover source text). The panel shows the thing in use in a box 24 pixels wide and 32 high at x 20, row 40 down, and the number of its carried place (1-5) above it at x 10, y 50. The box is put back as the clean copy of the screen (LOADING_TUNE) has it: the compositor (COMPOSITE_TO_SCREEN) is given its second plane all set, which takes every pixel from the clean copy, so whatever sprite it finds does not matter. Then the digit is printed, from SELECTED. Called on its own when a thing is dropped (CREATURE_UPDATE) or an empty place is chosen (MAIN_LOOP), and by SHOW_THING_IN_USE.
