F0FB: Colour the whole screen in the room's colour
Used by the routines at TITLE_SCREEN, ROOMST and MAIN_LOOP.
The author's ATTRI: all 768 attribute bytes set to ROOM_COLOUR. A room is drawn unseen, its attributes cleared (DRAW_CURRENT_ROOM), and coloured only when it is complete: under the title and GAME OVER (TITLE_SCREEN), at the end of the quest (ROOMST), and after the first pass of a new room, when every object has been drawn (MAIN_LOOP).
ATTRI F0FB LD A,(ROOM_COLOUR)
F0FE LD HL,$5800
F101 LD (HL),A
F102 LD DE,$5801
F105 LD BC,$02FF
F108 LDIR
F10A RET
