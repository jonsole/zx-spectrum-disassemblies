F117: Turn the knight's sprites to face his way
Used by the routine at KNIGHT_CONTROLS.
The author's MIMAN, mirror man: MIRROR (MITRO) for the knight, whose sprites begin at SPRITE9110: 28 planes, the image and mask of all fourteen of his frames. Called from his walking (KNIGHT_CONTROLS).
Input
C His direction bits
E His +$0E
IX His record
MIMAN F117 LD HL,SPRITE9110 The first sprite; 28 planes, counted in the alternate C
F11A EXX
F11B LD C,$1C
F11D JR MIRROR
