F199: Turn facing bits back into a direction, and find the view's sprite
Used by the routines at PICK_UP and KNIGHT_CONTROLS.
The author's FACING, the other way from MIRROR (MITRO): bits 6 and 5 of E give C one of $08 or $40 (view 0) or $04 or $80 (view 1), and for view 0 HL moves on by the word at IY+$62, from the first view's sprite to the other's. Picking up (PICK_UP) and fighting (KNIGHT_CONTROLS) use it to take the pose for the way the knight faces.
Input
E The facing, in bits 5 and 6
HL The pose's sprite in view 1
Output
C The direction bit
HL The pose's sprite in the view he faces
