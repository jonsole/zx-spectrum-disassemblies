FCDC: Test a box against one record, unless it is the mover's own or hidden
Used by the routines at TEST_OTHER and FIND_OBSTACLE.
A record counts unless it is the one being updated (FOUND_RECORD equal to THIS_RECORD), or has bit 5 of +16 set -- carried, or vanished -- when it is not a door. Doors always count.
BOX_OVERLAP, the author's BB2, is the box test itself, used on its own by CHE3D and BUMPED. A box runs from a corner: from +6 for the length +9 along x, from +8 for +11 along z, and down from the top, +7, for the height +10. Two boxes overlap when they overlap along all three axes, each test strict, so boxes that only touch do not; each axis that does not overlap returns at once with the carry clear.
Input
IX The record
B, D, H x, y and z of the box
C, E, L its x length, height and z length
Output
F Carry set if they overlap
TEST_ONE_RECORD FCDC LD A,(FOUND_RECORD) The mover's own record: no
FCDF SUB (IY+$03)
FCE2 RET Z
FCE3 LD A,(IX+$0C) A door: always
FCE6 AND $0F
FCE8 CP $01
FCEA JR Z,BOX_OVERLAP
FCEC AND A Anything else, unless hidden
FCED BIT 5,(IX+$10)
FCF1 RET NZ
