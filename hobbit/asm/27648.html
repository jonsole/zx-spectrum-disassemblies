6C00: The game's entry point
Reached by PRINT USR 27648, and never left: it runs on into MAIN_LOOP at $6D13, which is the game. It first copies the whole of the game's changeable state aside -- the objects to $F400, the rooms straight after them, the variables at $B6EB and the TIMERS block to $5F00 -- and every new game at $6C27 copies it back, which is how dying and starting again restores the world as it was loaded.
