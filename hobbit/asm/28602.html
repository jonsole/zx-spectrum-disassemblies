6FBA: Does the typed word agree with the candidate?
Letter for letter, over the shorter of the two -- which is what lets TOKENISE take a typed word as an abbreviation.
Output
F Z if they agree
LETTERS_AGREE 6FBA LD A,(TYPED_LENGTH) B = the shorter of the two lengths
6FBD LD B,A
6FBE LD A,(ENTRY_LENGTH)
6FC1 CP B
6FC2 JR NC,LETTERS_AGREE_0
6FC4 LD B,A
LETTERS_AGREE_0 6FC5 LD HL,TYPED_LETTERS Compare that many letters
6FC8 LD DE,ENTRY_LETTERS
LETTERS_AGREE_1 6FCB LD A,(DE)
6FCC CP (HL)
6FCD RET NZ
6FCE INC DE
6FCF INC HL
6FD0 DJNZ LETTERS_AGREE_1
6FD2 RET
