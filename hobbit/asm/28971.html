712B: Tell the player what was done, as a sentence
Built from the action's pattern: who did it, "cannot" if it was refused, the verb -- or GO and the direction, for a move, or GO SOMEWHERE in the dark, when the player cannot see which way -- then the first object after its particle, and the second after its preposition, and a full stop. So what the other characters are seen to do is told by the same code, from the same patterns, as the player's own actions.
A pattern with bit 4 of $B71D set is not narrated at all: that is LOOK and INVENTORY, the only two, which change nothing anyone could see.
