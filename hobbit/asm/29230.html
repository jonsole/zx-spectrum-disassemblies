722E: Does a typed word fit a word of a name?
A word that was not typed -- zero -- fits anything. Otherwise only the twelve-bit dictionary offset is compared, not the flag nibble above it. Both pointers move on two bytes either way.
WORD_MATCHES 722E PUSH HL
722F LD A,(HL) Not typed at all? Then it fits anything
7230 INC HL
7231 OR (HL)
7232 JR Z,WORD_MATCHES_0
7234 LD A,(IY+$01) Same top half of the twelve-bit offset, ignoring the flag nibble...
7237 XOR (HL)
7238 AND $0F
723A JR NZ,WORD_MATCHES_0
723C DEC HL ...and the same low byte
723D LD A,(HL)
723E CP (IY+$00)
WORD_MATCHES_0 7241 POP HL On to the next word of both names either way
7242 INC HL
7243 INC HL
7244 INC IY
7246 INC IY
7248 RET
