7478: Print a noun, with its article in the one case PRINT_NAME leaves it out
PRINT_NAME prints the article itself, except when NOUN_ONLY asks for the noun alone; then it is done here. The flag bits are taken off the reference first.
Input
DE The noun's word reference
PRINT_NOUN 7478 PUSH DE
7479 LD A,(NOUN_ONLY)
747C AND A
747D CALL NZ,ARTICLE
7480 POP DE
7481 LD A,D
7482 AND $0F
7484 LD D,A
7485 JP PRINT_WORD_DE
