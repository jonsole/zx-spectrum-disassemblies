7585: Parse one command from the tokens
Called by the main loop with TOKEN_POINTER pointing into TOKENS; returns NZ to go back for another line. A line of several commands -- joined by THEN, or by a full stop -- is taken one command at a time, the main loop coming back here while MORE_COMMANDS says there is more.
