7F78: Draw the current location's picture
Looks the location up in the picture table at PICTURE_TABLE, takes the stream pointer out of the record and runs it. Does nothing at all if the byte at PICTURES_ON is zero, which is believed to be the graphics on/off flag -- v1.2 was also sold as a text-only edition, and this is the only test standing between a location change and the whole of the drawing code.
