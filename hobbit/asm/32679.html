7FA7: Interpret a picture stream
IY walks the stream; D and E are the current point, in a 256x128 space with y measured upwards from scanline 127. The opcodes are: $00 end, $08 move, $2x paint attributes, $4x flood fill, and anything with bit 7 set a line. Bits 0-2 of a fill or paint opcode are its colour.
A line is two bytes, and both are split: the opcode byte carries the direction in bits 0-2 and part of the minor-axis step in bits 2-5, while the second byte carries the length in bits 0-5 and the last two step bits in bits 6-7. That is a Bresenham line of up to 64 pixels in 16 bits.
