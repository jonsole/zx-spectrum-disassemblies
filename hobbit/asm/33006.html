80EE: Is the pixel at (D,E) set?
Returns NZ if it is. Two instructions around PIXEL_ADDRESS, and the fill's inner loop.
PIXEL_SET 80EE PUSH HL
80EF CALL PIXEL_ADDRESS Address and mask for (D,E), and test that bit on the screen
80F2 AND (HL)
80F3 POP HL
