8271: The special words, and what PARSE_SPECIAL does with each
Thirteen word references, then a handler for each, reached through JP (HL) -- so the handlers are code seeds. Among them are the game's own commands: SAVE and LOAD, which the playthrough never types, QUIT, PAUSE, HELP, SCORE, and PRINT and NOPRINT. Slot 0 holds no word -- the reference zero -- and that is what a quote mark comes through the tokeniser as: its handler is SPECIAL_QUOTE's. ONE's handler just goes on to the next word.
