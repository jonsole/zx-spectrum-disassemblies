82A5: PRINT: copy the game's text to a ZX Printer, if there is one
Bit 6 of port $FB is low when a ZX Printer is attached; only then is $B6F2 set. NOPRINT, at $82AF, clears it. Both go back to the parser for the next word at $82B3.
82A5 IN A,($FB) No printer: nothing changes
82A7 BIT 6,A
82A9 JR NZ,$82B3
82AB LD A,$01 Copy to the printer from now on
82AD JR $82B0
