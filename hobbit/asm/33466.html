82BA: EXCEPT: only after ALL
ALL ... EXCEPT ... is kept as ALL_EXCEPT = 2, and the ALL bit set on the verb; EXCEPT on its own is an error, through NOT_ALLOWED_HERE.
WORD_EXCEPT 82BA LD A,(ALL_EXCEPT)
82BD CP $01
82BF JP NZ,NOT_ALLOWED_HERE
82C2 LD A,$02
82C4 LD (ALL_EXCEPT),A
82C7 LD A,(IY+$01)
82CA OR $80
82CC LD (IY+$01),A
82CF JP NEW_NOUN_PHRASE
