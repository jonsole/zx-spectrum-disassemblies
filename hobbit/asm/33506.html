82E2: IT: the last noun phrase again
The noun phrase kept at IT_NAME from the last sentence is copied into PHRASE, as if it had been typed, and handed to the first or the second phrase's handler.
WORD_IT 82E2 PUSH DE
82E3 LD HL,IT_NAME
82E6 LD DE,PHRASE_NOUN
82E9 LD BC,$0006
82EC LDIR
82EE POP DE
82EF BIT 7,E
82F1 CALL IT_PHRASE
82F4 JP NEW_NOUN_PHRASE
