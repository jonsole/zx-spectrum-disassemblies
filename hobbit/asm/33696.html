83A0: HELP: a hint for where the player is
Eleven places have a hint of their own, in HELP_HINTS; anywhere else it is "YOU'RE DOING FINE.". Only the player gets help: a character told HELP just goes on to the next word.
DO_HELP 83A0 LD A,($B6EA) Not the player: ignore it

83A7 PUSH HL
83A8 PUSH IX

83B0 LD IX,$83CD

83B9 LD L,(IX+$01)
83BC LD H,(IX+$02)
DO_HELP_0 83BF LD A,$01 Print it
