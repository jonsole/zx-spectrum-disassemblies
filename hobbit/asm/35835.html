8BFB: What each key gives
Forty characters, one per key, indexed by half-row times five plus the key's bit: capital letters, SPACE, ENTER and a backspace on 0; and on 5, 6, 7 and 8, the cursor keys, the codes ONE_KEY_MOVES turns into moves. The rest of the number row gives nothing.
