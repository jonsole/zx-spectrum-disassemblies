8CF1: Can the actor pick the first object up?
Used by the routines at DO_TAKE and DO_THROW_AT.
Its weight and all it holds must fit what the actor can carry -- byte 3 of the actor's record less its own weight and load -- or "the ... is too heavy to lift." and "you are carrying too much."; and a liquid cannot be picked up at all. A refusal leaves the handler that called it.
