8F3B: GO THROUGH, carried by doors and the like as their own handler
The exit that goes through the object; refused if there is none, if it leads nowhere yet, or if the object will not let anyone through ($8E85). Otherwise it is a move in that exit's direction, into MOVE past its darkness and captivity checks. $8F3E is the way in for callers that have found the exit already.
