8F69: FILL WITH, carried by the barrel
Filling from a river's water fetches a fresh water of the same kind (objects $15 and $16, kept nowhere for this), which is what goes in; then it is PUT IN with the objects swapped. A container already full says so. As read, the liquid would then be refused by the last part of CAN_LIFT, which DO_PUT_IN begins with, so that FILL can never succeed; that has not been tried in play.
DO_FILL 8F69 LD IX,(TARGET_RECORD) From a river's water? A fresh one
8F6D LD A,(INSTRUMENT)
8F70 CP $17
8F72 JR Z,DO_FILL_1
8F74 CP $18
8F76 JR Z,DO_FILL_2
DO_FILL_0 8F78 LD IY,(INSTRUMENT_RECORD) Only with a liquid
8F7C BIT 1,(IY+$07)
8F80 JP Z,REFUSE
8F83 BIT 2,(IX+$07) Full already: "the ... is full."
8F87 LD A,$82
8F89 JP NZ,SAY_STATE
8F8C LD HL,DO_PUT_IN Put it in, the objects swapped
8F8F JP SWAPPED_OBJECTS
DO_FILL_1 8F92 LD A,$15 The fresh water, held by nothing
8F94 LD IY,WATER
8F98 JR DO_FILL_3
DO_FILL_2 8F9A LD A,$16
8F9C LD IY,BLACK_WATER
DO_FILL_3 8FA0 LD (INSTRUMENT),A
8FA3 LD (INSTRUMENT_RECORD),IY
8FA7 LD (IY+$01),$FF
8FAB JR DO_FILL_0
