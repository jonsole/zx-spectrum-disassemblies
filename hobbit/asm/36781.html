8FAD: RUN: off in any direction that has a way out
A random direction from 1 to 10, then the first from there on, going round, that the location has an exit in; then MOVE.
DO_RUN 8FAD LD A,$0A A random direction, 1 to 10
8FAF CALL RANDOM_POSITIVE
8FB2 CP $00
8FB4 JR Z,DO_RUN
DO_RUN_0 8FB6 LD B,A Is there a way out that way? If not, the next, round from 10 to 1
8FB7 CALL FIND_EXIT
8FBA CP B
8FBB JR Z,DO_RUN_1
8FBD LD A,B
8FBE INC A
8FBF CP $0A
8FC1 JR C,DO_RUN_0
8FC3 LD A,$01
8FC5 JR DO_RUN_0
DO_RUN_1 8FC7 LD (ACTION),A Go
8FCA JP MOVE
