8FCD: ENTER and GO INTO
Looks for an exit whose destination is the number in $B6E8 and goes through it as GO_THROUGH does. For these two actions that number is a place, not an object: the parser, at $7DFE, tries a noun against the names of the rooms this one's exits lead to (ROOM_BY_NAME) before it tries the objects, so ENTER THE CAVE names the cave.
8FCD LD A,($B6E8)
8FD0 CALL $9F2D
8FD3 JP $8F3E
