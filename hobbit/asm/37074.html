90D2: The player is dead: say so and start again
Used by the routines at MOVE, INTO_THE_RIVER, KILL, DRAGON_HUNTS, SINKING_IN_BOG, GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER, TROLLS_EAT, WEB_SMOTHERS, EYES_STING and AT_FOREST_RIVER.
Prints "you are dead." as a sentence about the player, calls SHOW_SCORE, waits for any key and goes back into the start-up at NEW_GAME. Reached, for one, from MOVE when the player falls in the dark once too often.
