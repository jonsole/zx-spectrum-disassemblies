90D2: The player is dead: say so and start again
Prints "you are dead." as a sentence about the player, calls $83F5, waits for any key and goes back into the start-up at $6C27. Reached, for one, from MOVE when the player falls in the dark once too often.
PLAYER_DIES 90D2 SUB A The sentence is about the player
90D3 LD ($B6EA),A
90D6 LD HL,$AFF1 "you are dead."
90D9 CALL RUN_MESSAGE_0
90DC CALL SHOW_SCORE Not yet worked out
This entry point is used by the routine at CHECK_WON.
PLAYER_DIES_0 90DF XOR A Wait for any key
90E0 IN A,($FE)
90E2 AND $1F
90E4 CP $1F
90E6 JR Z,PLAYER_DIES_0
90E8 JP START_0 And start again
