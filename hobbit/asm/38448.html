9630: Describe a location in full: "you are in ...", the picture, and what is there
Used by the routines at DO_LOOK, MOVE and JUMP_ONTO_BARREL.
What MOVE does on the first visit to a place. The opening phrase is a message with a word left to fill in: bits 1-3 of byte 0 of the room's record pick it from ROOM_PREPOSITIONS, and it is written into the message at MSG_IN before DESCRIBE_LOCATION prints it -- "you are in", "you are on", "you are outside" and so on.

A The location
