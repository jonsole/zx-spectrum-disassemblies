99C6: A character tries the action in ACTION to INSTRUMENT
Checked by WOULD_WORK first, as the player's sentences are; then carried out by DO_ACTION, and the player is told of anyone who has just come into view -- "... enters." for the actor, "... appears." for the second object, through ANNOUNCE_ARRIVAL. ACTOR_DOES is the way in for an order the character was given, skipping the check.
