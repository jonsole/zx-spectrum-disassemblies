9B02: Note where the player is, and whether it is too dark to see
Used by the routines at CHARACTERS_ACT and MOVE_CONTENTS.
CHARACTERS_ACT starts with this. PLAYER_AT is the player's location and PLAYER_IN_DARK is 1 in the dark, 0 in the light: in the dark the other characters are heard, not seen.
NOTE_LIGHT 9B02 LD A,$00 PLAYER_AT = where the player is
9B04 CALL LOCATION_OF
9B07 LD (PLAYER_AT),A
9B0A CALL TOO_DARK PLAYER_IN_DARK = 1 if too dark to see
9B0D LD A,$00
9B0F JR NC,NOTE_LIGHT_0
9B11 INC A
NOTE_LIGHT_0 9B12 LD (PLAYER_IN_DARK),A
9B15 RET
