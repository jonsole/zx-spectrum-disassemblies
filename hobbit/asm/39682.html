9B02: Note where the player is, and whether it is too dark to see
CHARACTERS_ACT starts with this. $B6F5 is the player's location and $980C is 1 in the dark, 0 in the light: in the dark the other characters are heard, not seen.
9B02 LD A,$00 $B6F5 = where the player is
9B04 CALL $9F82
9B07 LD ($B6F5),A
9B0A CALL $95ED $980C = 1 if too dark to see
9B0D LD A,$00
9B0F JR NC,$9B12
9B11 INC A
9B12 LD ($980C),A
9B15 RET
