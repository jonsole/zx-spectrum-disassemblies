9B16: A character held by something tries to get out
A character held by another character, or by something with flag bit 3, goes on with its script as usual. Held by something closed -- flag bit 5 clear, not to be seen into -- it can do nothing. Otherwise it tries action $37, CLIMB OUT OF, on what holds it.
