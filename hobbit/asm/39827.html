9B93: Step to the next three-byte entry
Used by the routines at NEXT_OBJECT_KEEP_A, ROOM_LEFT, FIND_NAMED_OBJECT, ROOM_BY_NAME, FIND_EXIT and EXIT_TO.
Adds 3 to IX and returns Z at the $FF that ends a list. Shared with other lists of three-byte entries, which is why it also loads IY from bytes 1 and 2 -- for an exit those are the object it goes through and the destination, not an address.
