9BB1: Find a location's record
Used by the routines at ROOM_NAME_AT, CAN_PASS, LOOK_THROUGH, DESCRIBE_ROOM, DESCRIBE_LOCATION, DESCRIBE_BRIEFLY, ROOM_LEFT, ACTOR_ROOM, ROOM_BY_NAME, EXITS_OF, DRAGON_HUNTS and ELROND_READS_MAP.
Anything from $50 up is not a location and gets zero back; otherwise the record's address is read straight out of ROOM_POINTERS, two bytes per location. Unlike objects there is no search: locations are numbered densely, so a table indexed by number is cheaper than FIND_RECORD.

A The location
Output
IX Its record
