9D53: Empty an object into whatever holds it
Used by the routines at KILL, BARREL_REACHES_LAKE and TROLLS_TURN_TO_STONE.
Everything held by the object in A passes to the object's own holder, except liquids (flag bit 1): those are poured away -- nowhere, held by nothing, not visible -- with a message.
