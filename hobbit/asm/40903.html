9FC7: List what object A holds, or with A = $FF what lies loose in location B
Each thing is named and followed by a full stop, and then -- unless $A050 says otherwise -- whatever it holds is listed after it, two places further in: the indent is kept at $869F, and this calls itself. Left out: the actor itself, anything out of the actor's reach, and loose things that are in more than one place at once, which are the doors and other fixtures -- EXITS_THROUGH has already told of those.
Input
A The holder, or $FF
B The location
D The indent
Output
C How many were listed, added on
