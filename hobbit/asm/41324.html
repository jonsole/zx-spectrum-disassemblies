A16C: "the ... is ...": an object and its state
The state word is picked by A from STATE_WORDS: bits 0-6 the pair, bit 7 which of the two. A kill says "the ... is dead.", CLIMB OUT OF something shut "the ... is closed.". $A172 is the way in with the name already in HL.

IX The object's record
