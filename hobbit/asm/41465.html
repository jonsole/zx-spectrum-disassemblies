A1F9: Is the first object locked, or open?
NZ with A a SAY_STATE state for "locked" or "open"; OPEN_STATE asks only whether it is open.
Output
F NZ if it is locked or open
A LOCKED or OPEN, for SAY_STATE
LOCK_STATE A1F9 LD IX,(TARGET_RECORD)
A1FD BIT 0,(IX+$07)
A201 LD A,$80
A203 RET NZ
This entry point is used by the routine at DO_UNLOCK.
