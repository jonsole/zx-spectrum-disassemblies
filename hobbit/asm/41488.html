A210: The ten directions, as words
In the order of the direction codes, 1 to 10. DIRECTION_WORD indexes from DIRECTION_WORDS-2, two bytes earlier, because there is no direction 0.
DIRECTION_WORDS A210 DEFW $0480 NORTH
A212 DEFW $0607 SOUTH
A214 DEFW $01FE EAST
A216 DEFW $0779 WEST
A218 DEFW $0485 NORTHEAST
A21A DEFW $048E NORTHWEST
A21C DEFW $060C SOUTHEAST
A21E DEFW $0615 SOUTHWEST
A220 DEFW $0725 UP
A222 DEFW $01C4 DOWN
