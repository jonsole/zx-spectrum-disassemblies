A248: TIE TO
Only with the rope. TIE ROPE TO X is made TIE X TO ROPE by swapping the objects (SWAPPED_OBJECTS). Not a liquid, not anything with something visible in it ("the ... is already tied."), and not a living character -- a dead one can be. The thing is then held by the rope; and the rope goes to the one who tied it if they have the thing, or could take it, and is left lying otherwise.
