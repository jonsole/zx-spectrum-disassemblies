A3BC: TAKE OFF, carried by the ring
Not if it is not being worn ("you are not wearing the ..."). Visible again, four times as strong, and the timer stopped.

A3C0 LD IY,$C31A
A3C4 BIT 7,(IY+$07) Not worn: refused
A3C8 LD HL,$B074
A3CB JP NZ,RUN_MESSAGE_0
A3CE CALL FOR_REAL The test ends here
A3D1 SET 7,(IY+$07) Seen again, and as strong as before; the timer stopped
A3D5 SET 7,(IX+$07)
A3D9 SLA (IX+$05)
A3DD SLA (IX+$05)
A3E1 SUB A
A3E2 LD ($CAAF),A
A3E5 RET
