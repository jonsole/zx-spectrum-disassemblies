A448: After a goblin is attacked: a dead goblin comes back
Killing a goblin does not get rid of it. "the ... falls down a hole and vanishes.", it is alive again, back in its slot in CHARACTERS and in its own place, named as it was, from GOBLIN_HOMES; and if that is where the player is, "another goblin enters.".
