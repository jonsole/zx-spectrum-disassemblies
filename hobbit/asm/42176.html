A4C0: The goblins' door's own OPEN
Only from location 16, the big goblins' cavern, where it is opened as any door is (through DO_OPEN); then timer 3 shuts it again two turns later. From its other side, the goblins' dungeon, it will not open.
GOBLINS_DOOR_OPENED A4C0 LD IX,(ACTOR) Not in the cavern: refused
A4C4 LD A,(IX+$10)
A4C7 CP $10
A4C9 JP NZ,REFUSE
A4CC CALL DO_OPEN Open it
A4CF CALL ONLY_IF_DONE
A4D2 LD A,(TIMER3) Start timer 3
A4D5 LD (TIMER3_COUNT),A
A4D8 RET
