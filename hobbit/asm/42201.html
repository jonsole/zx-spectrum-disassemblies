A4D9: Timer 3: the goblins' door shuts
Clears bit 5 of the goblins' door's flags -- the bit that lets it be seen through, which a door has while it is open.
GOBLINS_DOOR_SHUTS A4D9 LD HL,$C34B
A4DC RES 5,(HL)
A4DE RET
