A5FB: Timer 0: the barrel is thrown up on the long lake
Two turns after it is started, the barrel goes to location 34, the long lake, and anything in it goes with it -- the player too, who is told so and arrives there. Then it is emptied, with printing off, so that what spills is not reported; and the wine is put back into a barrel in location 32, the elvenking's cellar.
BARREL_REACHES_LAKE A5FB SUB A Only one timer fires in a turn
A5FC LD (TIMER_FIRED),A
A5FF LD A,(PLAYER_HOLDER) In the barrel ($13)? "you are thrown onto the bank of the long lake."
A602 CP $13
A604 LD HL,MSG_YOU_ARE_THROWN_ONTO
A607 CALL Z,RUN_MESSAGE_HL
A60A LD A,$22 The barrel is at location 34 now
A60C LD (BARREL_WHERE),A
A60F LD B,A ... and so is everything in it
A610 LD A,$13
A612 CALL MOVE_CONTENTS
A615 LD IX,BARREL The barrel: at the lake; flag bit 5 (seen into) off, bit 2 on
A619 LD (IX+$10),$20
A61D RES 5,(IX+$07)
A621 SET 2,(IX+$07)
A625 SUB A Empty it with printing off
A626 LD (PRINTING_ON),A
A629 LD A,$13
A62B CALL EMPTY_OUT
A62E LD A,$01
A630 LD (PRINTING_ON),A
A633 LD IY,WINE The wine: back in location 32, held by the barrel
A637 LD (IY+$10),$20
A63B LD (IY+$01),$13
A63F RET
