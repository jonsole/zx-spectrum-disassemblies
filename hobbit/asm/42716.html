A6DC: Once the treasure is gone from its hall, the dragon hunts the player
While the treasure is still in the lower halls, nothing. Once it is not, wherever the player is in the open -- a lit place -- four times in five "in the distance you see the shape of a monstrous dragon flying after you.", and otherwise "the dragon descends and in a terrific spout of flames burns you to a crisp." and PLAYER_DIES.
DRAGON_HUNTS A6DC LD A,(VALUABLE_TREASURE_WHERE)
A6DF CP $29
A6E1 RET Z
A6E2 LD A,(PLAYER_WHERE)
A6E5 CALL GET_ROOM
A6E8 BIT 7,(IX+$00)
A6EC RET Z
A6ED CALL FOR_REAL
A6F0 LD A,$01
A6F2 LD (PRINTING_ON),A
A6F5 LD HL,MSG_IN_THE_DISTANCE_YOU
A6F8 LD A,$64
A6FA CALL RANDOM_POSITIVE
A6FD CP $50
A6FF JR C,DRAGON_SAYS
A701 LD HL,MSG_THE_DRAGON_DESCENDS_AND
A704 CALL RUN_MESSAGE_HL
A707 JP PLAYER_DIES
