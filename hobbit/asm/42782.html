A71E: The magic door's own EXAMINE
Anyone who can be seen -- flag bit 7 -- sees "nothing special here." Anyone who cannot, sets timer 5 going: "the magic door warns of elves approaching.", and three turns later it opens for an elf to sweep past (MAGIC_DOOR_OPENS).
MAGIC_DOOR_EXAMINED A71E CALL FOR_REAL
A721 LD IX,(ACTOR) Visible? "you see nothing special here."
A725 BIT 7,(IX+$07)
A729 LD HL,MSG_SEE_NOTHING_SPECIAL_HERE
A72C JP NZ,RUN_MESSAGE_HL
A72F LD A,(TIMER5) Start timer 5
A732 LD (TIMER5_COUNT),A
A735 LD HL,MSG_THE_MAGIC_DOOR_WARN "the magic door warns of elves approaching."
A738 JP RUN_MESSAGE_HL
