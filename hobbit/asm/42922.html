A7AA: Timer 4: sinking into the deep bog
Timer 4's routine for both its warning and its end. Standing in location 29, the deep bog, stops the timer; either way "you are slowly sinking into the bog", and if the timer has stopped, whether by running out or by the player being in the bog, PLAYER_DIES.
SINKING_IN_BOG A7AA LD A,($C12B) In the bog? Stop the timer
A7AD CP $1D
A7AF JR NZ,SINKING_IN_BOG_0
A7B1 SUB A
A7B2 LD ($CAA1),A
SINKING_IN_BOG_0 A7B5 LD HL,$B153 "you are slowly sinking into the bog."
A7B8 CALL RUN_MESSAGE_0
A7BB LD A,($CAA1) Stopped: dead
A7BE CP $00
A7C0 RET NZ
A7C1 JP PLAYER_DIES
