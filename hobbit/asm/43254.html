A8F6: Gollum hears the answer to his riddle
The answer has to be given to Gollum as an order -- said to him, in the ORDERS slot he takes his turn from. It is right if the answer's word reference appears anywhere in it; with no answer, or a wrong one, "someone strangles you from behind." and PLAYER_DIES.
GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER A8F6 CALL FOR_REAL No longer expecting an answer
A8F9 SUB A
A8FA LD (RIDDLE_ASKED),A
A8FD CALL TAKE_ORDER Nothing said to him: strangled
A900 JR Z,GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER_1
A902 LD BC,$0018 Look through what was said for the answer word
A905 LD DE,(RIDDLE)
A909 LD A,(DE)
GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER_0 A90A CPIR
A90C JR NZ,GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER_1
A90E INC DE
A90F LD A,(DE)
A910 DEC DE
A911 CP (HL)
A912 JR NZ,GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER_0
A914 RET
GOLLUM_HEARS_ANSWER_1 A915 CALL FOR_REAL "someone strangles you from behind.", and dead
A918 LD A,$01
A91A LD (PRINTING_ON),A
A91D LD HL,MSG_SOMEONE_STRANGLE_YOU
A920 CALL RUN_MESSAGE_HL
A923 JP PLAYER_DIES
