A9BD: The trolls, once the player comes to their clearing: their lines
Fails, and so is tried again every turn, until the player is in location 5; then the hideous troll and the vicious troll each say their line and the script moves on.

A9C3 CALL FOR_REAL
A9C6 LD A,(ACTING)
A9C9 LD HL,MSG_BLIMEY_LOOK_AT_THIS
A9CC CP $47
A9CE JR Z,TROLLS_TALK_0
A9D0 LD HL,MSG_YER_CAN_TRY_BUT
