AA5C: Timer 1: the broken web is mended
Two turns after WEB_BROKEN: clears the web's flag bits 3 (broken) and 5 (can be seen through), doubles its byte 5, and gives it back SPIDER as the adjective in its name. Not yet seen in play.
AA5C LD IX,$C205 Flags and byte 5
AA60 RES 3,(IX+$07)
AA64 RES 5,(IX+$07)
AA68 SLA (IX+$05)
AA6C LD DE,$0623 Its name
AA6F LD ($C20F),DE
AA73 RET
