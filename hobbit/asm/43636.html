AA74: Timer 9: the hole vanishes again
Unless the door has been opened (flag bit 5), the timer is started again and the door hidden, so the hole comes and goes every five turns until somebody opens it.
SIDE_DOOR_VANISHES AA74 LD HL,$C2BC Opened? Then it stays
AA77 BIT 5,(HL)
AA79 RET NZ
AA7A LD A,($CAC3) Start the timer again
AA7D LD ($CAC4),A
AA80 LD HL,$C2BC Hidden
AA83 RES 7,(HL)
AA85 LD A,($C12B) At location 42? "the hole vanishes."
AA88 CP $2A
AA8A RET NZ
This entry point is used by the routine at SIDE_DOOR_CLOSED.
SIDE_DOOR_VANISHES_0 AA8B LD HL,$B2A4
AA8E JP RUN_MESSAGE_0 }
