AAB3: Timer 5's warning: the magic door opens and an elf sweeps past
Sets bit 5 of the magic door's flags, reports it to a player in either of its rooms, and goes on to timer 6's routine.
MAGIC_DOOR_OPENS AAB3 LD HL,$C388 Open
AAB6 SET 5,(HL)
AAB8 LD HL,$B037 "the magic door opens."
AABB CALL AT_MAGIC_DOOR
AABE LD HL,$B113 "an elf sweeps past."
AAC1 CALL AT_MAGIC_DOOR
AAC4 JP RING_CHECK
