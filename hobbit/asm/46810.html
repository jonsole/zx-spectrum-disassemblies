B6DA: The game's variables
The working state, from here to ENDINGS. The part from SAVED_STATE to PICTURES_ON is the game's own -- where things stand, the score, the riddle, the map -- and is what START keeps a copy of for a new game and SAVE writes to tape; the rest is scratch for the parser, the printer and the action in hand. DRUNK, at DRUNK, and ACTOR, at ACTOR, have blocks of their own.
VARIABLES B6DA DEFB $00,$00 Where the last word began in the input, echoed back when a word is not known
TOKEN_POINTER B6DC DEFB $00,$00 The parser's place in TOKENS
LAST_CLASS B6DE DEFB $00 The class of the last word, which the parser and the special words go back to
NAME_FAILED B6DF DEFB $00 Set by NAME_MATCHES; MATCH_PATTERN ends in UNKNOWN_VERB when it is
IT_NAME B6E0 DEFB $00,$00,$00,$00,$00,$00 The last target's name, for IT
PARSED_ACTION B6E6 DEFB $00 The action code, a copy kept by PARSE_ACTION
ACTION B6E7 DEFB $00 The action code being carried out
TARGET B6E8 DEFB $00 Its first object, the target, or $FF
INSTRUMENT B6E9 DEFB $00 Its second object, the instrument, or $FF
ACTING B6EA DEFB $00 Who is acting, and who the sentence is about: 0 for the player
