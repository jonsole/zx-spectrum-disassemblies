B71F: The endings a word can be given
Eight slots of four characters, chosen by bits 5-7 of a word's third dictionary byte when PRINT_WORD inflects it: "s" for fifty verbs, "es" for six (GO, CROSS, PUSH, SLASH, SMASH, TORCH), "ies", "d", "ing", and one worth a second look -- a backspace then "ies", which is how CARRY prints as CARRIES: the backspace takes the Y back off. The last two slots are empty. EMPTY is given plain "ies", which would print EMPTYIES if it were ever inflected; that has not been checked.
