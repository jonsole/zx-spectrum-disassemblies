BA80: How the player is placed in each kind of room
Word references, picked by bits 1 to 3 of byte 0 of a room's record. Only five are needed: most rooms use IN, twenty ON and nine AT, and INSIDE and OUTSIDE are the two sides of the goblins' gate, locations 19 and 20. The room records follow straight on.
ROOM_PREPOSITIONS BA80 DEFW $84C2 OUTSIDE
BA82 DEFW $837B INSIDE
BA84 DEFW $8378 IN
BA86 DEFW $84AA ON
BA88 DEFW $8070 AT
