C11B: Record for the player: you
A 16-byte head, where it is when the game starts -- location 1 -- and 2 handler(s) of its own, ending at $FF.
C11B DEFB $01 How many places it is in
C11C DEFB $FF What holds it or has it inside; $FF for nothing
C11D DEFB $10,$40 Its size, then its weight; for a character the weight byte is the most it can carry
C11F DEFB $10,$40,$40 Not yet worked out
C122 DEFB $E0 Flags: see OBJECT_INDEX
C123 DEFB $A8,$87,$00,$00,$00,$00 Its name: noun, then adjectives
C129 DEFB $00,$00 No description of its own
C12B DEFB $01 Its location
C12C DEFB $1B,$B5,$92 Action 27: 92B5
C12F DEFB $00,$D2,$90 Action 0: 90D2
C132 DEFB $FF End of this object's handlers
