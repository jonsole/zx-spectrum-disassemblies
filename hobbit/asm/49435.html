C11B: You (the player)
You: the player. It starts in tunnel like hall. It is present, a character, open, or can be seen into. It has its own handling for EAT.
A record is a 16-byte head -- where it is, what holds it, its size, weight, strength and defence, its flags, its name and its description -- then the places it is in, then its own handlers, ending at $FF. OBJECT_INDEX describes each field.
