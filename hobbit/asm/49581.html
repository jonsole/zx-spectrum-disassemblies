C1AD: Record for object $02: small curious key
A 16-byte head, where it is when the game starts -- held by goblins cache, location 13 -- and 0 handler(s) of its own, ending at $FF.
C1AD DEFB $01 How many places it is in
C1AE DEFB $1F What holds it or has it inside; $FF for nothing
C1AF DEFB $00,$00 Its size, then its weight; for a character the weight byte is the most it can carry
C1B1 DEFB $00,$00,$00 Not yet worked out
C1B4 DEFB $80 Flags: see OBJECT_INDEX
C1B5 DEFB $A5,$03,$E1,$05,$63,$01 Its name: noun, then adjectives
C1BB DEFB $00,$00 No description of its own
C1BD DEFB $0D Its location
C1BE DEFB $FF End of this object's handlers
