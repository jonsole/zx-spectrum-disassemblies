C1BF: Record for object $04: large key
A 16-byte head, where it is when the game starts -- held by hideous troll, location 5 -- and 0 handler(s) of its own, ending at $FF.
C1BF DEFB $01 How many places it is in
C1C0 DEFB $47 What holds it or has it inside; $FF for nothing
C1C1 DEFB $01,$01 Its size, then its weight; for a character the weight byte is the most it can carry
C1C3 DEFB $00,$00,$00 Not yet worked out
C1C6 DEFB $80 Flags: see OBJECT_INDEX
C1C7 DEFB $A5,$03,$BF,$03,$00,$00 Its name: noun, then adjectives
C1CD DEFB $00,$00 No description of its own
C1CF DEFB $05 Its location
C1D0 DEFB $FF End of this object's handlers
