C2DD: Record for object $0C: large trap door

C2DE DEFB $FF What holds it or has it inside; $FF for nothing

C2E1 DEFB $00,$00,$30 Not yet worked out
C2E4 DEFB $80 Flags: see OBJECT_INDEX
C2E5 DEFB $C0,$01,$BF,$03,$E0,$06 Its name: noun, then adjectives
C2EB DEFB $00,$00 No description of its own
C2ED DEFB $20,$21 The locations it is in at once
