C381: Record for object $0D: magic door
A 16-byte head, where it is when the game starts -- locations 30, 28 -- and 4 handler(s) of its own, ending at $FF.
C381 DEFB $02 How many places it is in
C382 DEFB $FF What holds it or has it inside; $FF for nothing
C383 DEFB $FF,$FF Its size, then its weight; for a character the weight byte is the most it can carry
C385 DEFB $00,$FF,$FF Not yet worked out
C388 DEFB $80 Flags: see OBJECT_INDEX
C389 DEFB $C0,$01,$24,$04,$00,$00 Its name: noun, then adjectives
C38F DEFB $00,$00 No description of its own
C391 DEFB $1E,$1C The locations it is in at once
C393 DEFB $1E,$3B,$8F Action 30: 8F3B
C396 DEFB $18,$EC,$8E Action 24: 8EEC
C399 DEFB $2C,$A4,$94 Action 44: 94A4
C39C DEFB $1C,$1E,$A7 Action 28: A71E
C39F DEFB $FF End of this object's handlers
