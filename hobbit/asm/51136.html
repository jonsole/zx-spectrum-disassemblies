C7C0: Arriving in the elvenking's cellar
Starts timer 9, the hole in the mountain's side, at three turns rather than its usual five; and brings the dragon and Bard into the story, each unless it has flag bit 3, by giving them the empty CHARACTERS slots at $CB03 and $CAFC.
C7C0 LD A,$03
C7C2 LD ($CAC4),A
C7C5 LD HL,$C13A
C7C8 BIT 3,(HL)
C7CA JR NZ,$C7D1
C7CC LD A,$3C
C7CE LD ($CB03),A
C7D1 LD HL,$C4CA
C7D4 BIT 3,(HL)
C7D6 RET NZ
C7D7 LD A,$46
C7D9 LD ($CAFC),A
C7DC RET
