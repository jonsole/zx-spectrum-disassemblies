The Hobbit The map

Every location, placed by the compass direction of the exits between them -- north up, east right, and up and down on the diagonals -- and moved aside where the game's geography will not fit a grid. A line is a way between two places, with a head at each end it can be taken towards; dashed, it goes through something, a door or a river. Click a place for its entry.
