82EA: Sparkle, size 4 of 6
One of six sizes of sparkle, smallest first, shared by every kind of appearing and vanishing. Types $70 to $77 run from big to small as something vanishes (init_death_sparkles starts a death this way); $78 to $7F run from small to big as the player appears, at the start of a life or in a new room; the rest are the sparkles over the cauldron, of the end-of-game animation, of a collapsing block and of a charm that is lost. Types $74, $7B, $85, $A2, $A6.
