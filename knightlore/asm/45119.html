B03F: Redraw the whole screen on entering a room
Used by the routine at end_of_frame.
Only on the first frame in a room, which build_screen_objects flags at $5BB7. Every other frame draws only what changed; this one paints the room's colour over the whole screen, redraws the panel, the carried objects, the sun or moon and the counters, and then copies the whole buffer to the display.
