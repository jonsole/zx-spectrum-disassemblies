B42E: Sound: thud
Used by the routines at bounce_ball_on_landing, fire_turn_if_blocked and ud_ball_rise_or_fall.
Four low blips of three waves each, their pitches taken from the first four bytes of the ROM with the top two bits forced on so they are all long. Played when a bouncing ball lands or a moving flame turns.
