B4E6: Sound: C waves at one pitch
Used by the routines at sound_movable_block, sparkle_blip, thud_blip, sound_pitch_from_a, crash_blip, toggle_audio_hw_x16 and toggle_audio_hw_x24.
The common tail of most of the effects. toggle_audio_hw leaves B as it found it, so every wave has the same pitch.
Input
B the half-wave count
C the number of waves
