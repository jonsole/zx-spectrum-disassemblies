B538: Does this object take part in collisions?
Used by the routines at overlap_test_obj, dX_obj_loop, dY_obj_loop and dZ_obj_loop.
Bit 1 of byte 7 takes an object out of collision tests. Objects set it on themselves while they are being moved, and some keep it: Sabreman's upper half, the legs of the guards and the wizard, the cauldron bubbles and the finale sparks.
Input
IY the object
F on exit: Z if the slot is empty or bit 1 of byte 7 is set, NZ for an object things can collide with
