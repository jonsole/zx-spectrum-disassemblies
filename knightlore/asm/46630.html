B626: Bouncing ball: set the chase direction and the bounce height
Used by the routine at upd_182_183.
Writes the opcode chosen in upd_182_183 into the two conditional jumps at bounce_ball_y_sense and bounce_ball_x_sense. With JR C a ball flees the player; with JR NC it chases him.
Then works out how hard it will bounce: a dZ of 4 in an even-numbered room, and 4 plus a random 0 to 3 in an odd-numbered one.
