B6EF: Shuttling block: move
Used by the routine at shuttle_block_step.
The first instruction's displacement is patched by shuttle_block to set dX or dY. dZ is set to 1 so that the DEC in the move leaves it at 0: the block floats rather than falling.

A the step, +1 or -1
