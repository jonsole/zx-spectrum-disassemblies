BD23: The menu loop
Used by the routine at check_for_start_game.
One pass: redraws the menu (only its attributes change on screen after the first time), plays the tune if it has not played yet, and reads keys 1 to 5 into E. The control byte is kept in A through the key tests that follow and saved in $5BA6 first, so that check_for_start_game can click when it changes.
