BDA2: Menu: the colour of each line
Eight attribute bytes for the lines of menu_text: the title, the four control methods, directional control, start, and the copyright line. Bit 7 (FLASH) marks a selected option; flash_menu sets and clears it, and the initial value flashes the first method, keyboard.
