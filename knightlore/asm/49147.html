BFFB: Is the pick-up/drop control pressed?
Used by the routines at handle_pickup_drop, try_pickup_loop and wait_pickup_release.
The input bits in $5BB5 have pick-up/drop in bit 4 for every control method. With a joystick and directional control, though, the stick's down direction is a direction rather than pick-up, so bit 5 -- set by keys on the keyboard -- is used instead.
Input
F on exit, Z clear if it is pressed
chk_pickup_drop BFFB LD HL,$5BA4 joystick (method not 0)...
BFFE LD A,(HL)
BFFF AND $06
C001 LD A,($5BB5)
C004 JR Z,test_pickup_bit
C006 BIT 3,(HL) ...and directional control: use bit 5
C008 JR Z,test_pickup_bit
C00A RRCA
