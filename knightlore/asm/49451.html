C12B: Shift the carried slots along
Used by the routines at room_to_drop and pickup_object.
Moves slots 0 to 2 ($5BD8 to $5BE3) up one to slots 1 to 3 and empties slot 0. Whatever was in slot 3 has already been put down, so the carried objects behave as a queue: first picked up, first dropped.
