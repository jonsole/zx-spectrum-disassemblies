C3C3: Draw the sun and moon window
Used by the routine at inc_days.
Builds the window in the screen buffer and copies it to the screen: a patch 48 pixels wide and 31 high in the bottom right corner, cleared, the sun or moon drawn at its current position, and the two halves of the window frame (types 90 and 186) drawn over it using the scratch record at sprite_scratchpad. Because the frame is drawn last, the sun or moon rises from behind its left side and sets behind its right.
