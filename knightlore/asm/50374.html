C4C6: Move under gravity, and redraw if moved
Used by the routine at extra_life_fall.
Applies gravity and moves the object; if it moved, its horizontal velocity is cleared (so a push moves it one step, not a slide), a sound is made and it is redrawn. Shared by the table (upd_84) and the extra-life object (upd_103).
