C65E: A portcullis at rest (type 8)
A portcullis moves between the floor and 32 above it. Only one in a room moves at a time: $5BAF counts the one moving, and while it is non-zero nothing here happens. A portcullis anywhere below the top starts to rise at a random moment, on average once every 32 frames. One at the top drops at once for its first four falls after the room is entered (counted in $5BB0); after that it too waits for a 1-in-32 chance on each frame. Setting bit 0 of the type makes it type 9, handled by upd_9 while it moves.
Input
IX the portcullis's record
upd_8 C65E CALL adj_m6_m12 draw 12 left and 6 down
C661 LD HL,$5BAF another portcullis is moving
C664 LD A,(HL)
C665 AND A
C666 RET NZ
C667 LD A,($5BAE) on the floor ($5BAE is the floor's height): rise
C66A CP (IX+$03)
C66D JR Z,init_portcullis_up
C66F ADD A,$1F anywhere below the floor + 31: rise
C671 CP (IX+$03)
C674 JR NC,init_portcullis_up
C676 LD A,($5BB0) fewer than four falls so far: fall now
C679 CP $04
C67B JR C,init_portcullis_down
C67D LD A,($5BA5) otherwise a 1-in-32 chance
C680 AND $1F
C682 RET NZ
C683 OR $80 $80 + 1 is stored as the count; any value from 4 up would do the same
