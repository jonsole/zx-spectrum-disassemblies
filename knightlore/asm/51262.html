C83E: Read the controls and act on them
Used by the routine at upd_player_bottom.
The player's turn, in order: change between man and werewolf if the sun or moon says so, read the controls into C (bit 0 left, 1 right, 2 forward or up, 3 jump, 4 pick up or drop, or down on a joystick), pick up or drop, turn, jump, and step the legs. Then, if the player is standing in a doorway -- past the line of the room's walls -- plyr_OOB forbids any upward movement before the move is made.
