C87A: Is the player inside the room's walls?
Used by the routines at handle_pickup_drop and player_controls.
Compares the player's distance from the room's centre, $80 on each axis, with the room's half-size less the player's own half-width. Carry means the whole of the player's footprint is inside on both X and Y; no carry means it touches or crosses the line of a wall, which only an archway allows.
Also used by handle_pickup_drop.
Input
IX the player's bottom record
F carry set on exit if inside the walls
chk_plyr_OOB C87A LD HL,($5BAB) L = X half-size less our half-width, H = the same for Y
C87D LD A,L
C87E SUB (IX+$04)
C881 LD L,A
C882 LD A,H
C883 SUB (IX+$05)
C886 LD H,A
C887 LD A,(IX+$01) A = |X - $80|
C88A SUB $80
C88C JP P,oob_test_x
C88F NEG
