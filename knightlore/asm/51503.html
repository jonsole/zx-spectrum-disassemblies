C92F: Turn: mirror the sprite, and turn the top half to match
Used by the routines at left_right_calc_sprite and turn_right.
Toggles the mirror bit, then sets the top half's type to the legs' type plus 16 so the body turns in the same frame; upd_player_top would otherwise only catch up on its own update.
