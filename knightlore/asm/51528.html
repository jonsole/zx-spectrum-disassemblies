C948: Start a jump
Used by the routine at player_controls.
Jump starts only when the jump control is held, the room-entry walk is over, no jump is already in progress (bit 3 of +$0C, cleared on landing by move_player_apply), and the player is not already falling. The launch speed is 8 units up; apply_gravity then takes it away again frame by frame.
The test is on the speed, not on standing: a dZ of -1 or more is enough, and the collision code leaves dZ at 0 for a player on the ground.
