C969: Step the legs
Used by the routine at player_controls.
While the player is walking forward, jumping, or on the room-entry walk, the legs cycle and a footstep sounds. When none of those applies the legs do not simply stop: legs_to_rest carries the stride on to one of the two standing frames.
Input
C the controls
handle_forward C969 LD A,(IX+$0C) room-entry walk or jump: step
C96C AND $F0
C96E JR NZ,walk_step_sound
C970 BIT 3,(IX+$0C)
C974 JR NZ,walk_step_sound
C976 BIT 2,C forward not held: finish the stride
C978 JR Z,legs_to_rest
