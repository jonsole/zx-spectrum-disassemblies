CB29: Leave by the south wall?
Out if the north edge after the move is south of the south wall at $80 less the Y half-size. Y becomes the marker 0 (arrive at the north wall) and the room number goes down a row.
Input
HL the room's half-sizes (pushed by handle_exit_screen)

CB2C SUB H
CB2D LD H,A

CB31 ADD A,(IX+$0A)
CB34 ADD A,(IX+$05)
