CBAF: Shorten dX: does the move hit it?
Used by the routine at dX_obj_shorten.
If the boxes would overlap in X too, the move is stopped (bit 0 of +$0C) and harm passes. Bit 6 of +$0D means "has been harmed": the obstacle gets it if the mover has bit 7 (harms what it runs into), and the mover gets it if the obstacle has bit 5 (harms what runs into it). This is how the player dies of a guard or of spikes: upd_player_bottom watches bit 6.
