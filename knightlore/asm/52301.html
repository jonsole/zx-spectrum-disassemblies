CC4D: Shorten dZ: does the move hit it?
Used by the routine at dZ_obj_shorten.
As dX_obj_hit_test, and two things more. The obstacle gets bit 3 of its +$0D: something has come down on it (or up against it). A block that gives way when stood on, upd_91, waits for that bit. And a mover that can be carried -- bit 2 of +$07, which the player has -- takes on the obstacle's dX and dY wherever its own are zero, so standing on something that moves carries it along.
