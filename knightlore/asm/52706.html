CDE2: Update the player's head
Used by the routine at upd_32_to_47.
The head has no movement of its own: it copies the legs. First, unless the game has been won ($5BC3), a head whose kill flag (bit 6 of +$0D) has been set -- by the legs' handler at upd_player_bottom when the legs are killed -- turns into the death sparkle at init_death_sparkles.
Input
IX the head object (record 1)
upd_player_top CDE2 LD A,($5BC3) once the game is won the player cannot die
CDE5 AND A
CDE6 JR NZ,head_follows_legs
CDE8 BIT 6,(IX+$0D) killed: become the sparkle
CDEC JP NZ,init_death_sparkles
