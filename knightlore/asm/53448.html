D0C8: Keyboard
Used by the routine at check_user_input.
The whole keyboard is in use, a pair of half-rows to each action. The bottom row alternates: Z, C, B and M turn left, X, V, N and Symbol Shift turn right. A to G and H to Enter walk forward, Q to T and Y to P jump, and the number keys pick up and drop. Caps Shift and Space are left alone -- they are the pause keys (handle_pause).
