D111: Finish reading the controls
Used by the routines at check_user_input, if2_done, kempston_done, cursor_down and keyboard_pickup.
Common to every method. Bit 5 is set if any key is down other than the number keys, Caps Shift and Space. It is the pick-up key for a joystick in directional mode, where bit 4 is a direction. The byte is stored at $5BB5.
finished_input D111 LD A,$7E bottom two half-rows together, less Caps Shift and Space
D113 CALL read_port
D116 AND $1E
D118 PUSH BC or'd with the A..G, Q..T, Y..P and H..Enter half-rows
D119 LD B,A
D11A LD A,$99
D11C CALL read_port
D11F OR B
D120 POP BC
D121 JR Z,store_input any of them: bit 5
D123 SET 5,C
