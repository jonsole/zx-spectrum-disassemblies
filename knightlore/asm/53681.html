D1B1: Set up the player for a new game
Used by the routine at main.
Fills the saved records at plyr_spr_1_scratchpad and plyr_spr_2_scratchpad that lose_life starts each life from: the initial values from plyr_spr_init_data, knight legs and head as the types to become after materialising, and one of four starting rooms, chosen by the low two bits of the random seed at $5BA0.
init_start_location D1B1 LD HL,plyr_spr_init_data
D1B4 LD DE,plyr_spr_1_scratchpad
D1B7 LD BC,$0008
D1BA LDIR
D1BC LD DE,plyr_spr_2_scratchpad
D1BF LD BC,$0008
D1C2 LDIR
D1C4 LD A,$12 become knight legs (18) and knight head (34)
D1C6 LD (plyr_spr_1_tail),A
D1C9 LD A,$22
D1CB LD (plyr_spr_2_tail),A
D1CE LD A,($5BA0) one of four rooms
D1D1 AND $03
D1D3 LD L,A
D1D4 LD H,$00
D1D6 LD BC,start_locations
D1D9 ADD HL,BC
D1DA LD A,(HL) into both records
D1DB LD (start_loc_1),A
D1DE LD (start_loc_2),A
D1E1 RET
