D237: Copy a sprite's four bytes into the drawing record
Used by the routines at transfer_sprite_and_print, display_panel and print_border.
Loads a sprite type, flags (for the flip bits), pixel x and pixel y from a four-byte entry into the object record at IX -- the spare record at sprite_scratchpad -- ready for print_sprite.
Input
HL the four-byte entry
IX the record to draw with
HL on exit, the next entry
