D255: Draw the scroll-work at the foot of the screen
Used by the routine at no_delay.
Draws the panel artwork under the room from panel_data: a slanting run of five of one piece on each side, and two more pieces on each side, the right side the left mirrored. The picture of the sun and moon and the lives and days are drawn over it by other routines.
display_panel D255 LD IX,sprite_scratchpad the first entry
D259 LD HL,panel_data
D25C CALL transfer_sprite
D25F LD DE,$F810 left run: five copies, each 16 pixels right and 8 down
D262 LD B,$05
D264 CALL multiple_print_sprite
D267 CALL transfer_sprite_and_print two more pieces, drawn once each
D26A CALL transfer_sprite_and_print
D26D CALL transfer_sprite the right run's piece
D270 LD DE,$0810 five copies, each 16 pixels right and 8 up
D273 LD B,$05
D275 CALL multiple_print_sprite
D278 CALL transfer_sprite_and_print two more pieces, drawn once each
D27B JP transfer_sprite_and_print
