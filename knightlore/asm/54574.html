D52E: Wait for SPACE to be let go, sound, and carry on
The release is waited for so that the unpausing press does not pause the game again on the next frame.
debounce_space_release D52E LD A,$7E
D530 CALL read_port
D533 BIT 0,A
D535 JR NZ,debounce_space_release
D537 JP toggle_audio_hw_x24 the sound again, and back to the game
