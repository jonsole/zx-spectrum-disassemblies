D56F: Copy the whole buffer to the display, clearing it as it goes
Used by the routines at no_delay, print_charms_and_show and display_text_list.
Used when a new room is shown: the whole room is composed in screen_buffer first and then appears at once. The buffer runs the other way up from the display -- its first row is the bottom line of the screen -- because the game's pixel y counts up from the bottom; so the copy starts at the display's bottom-left byte, $57E0, and works up a line at a time.
Clearing as it copies leaves the buffer blank. That is safe because from here on only the areas render_dynamic_objects wipes and redraws are ever copied out of it, so what the rest of the buffer holds does not matter.
update_screen D56F LD HL,screen_buffer from the start of the buffer to the bottom-left of the display; B = 32 bytes a row, C = 192 rows
