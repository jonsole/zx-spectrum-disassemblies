D5B2: Wipe the next object that moved
Used by the routines at wipe_height and wipe_rect.
Bytes $18 and $19 of a record are the width in bytes and height in rows of its sprite as last drawn, and $1A and $1B its pixel x and y; save_2d_info copies all four to bytes $1C to $1F before the object's handler runs, so they hold last frame's picture while $18 to $1B are about to get this frame's. The rectangle wiped is the smallest one that covers both.
