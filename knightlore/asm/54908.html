D67C: Copy a rectangle of the buffer to the display
Used by the routines at show_carried_slot, display_frame, blit_2x8, copy_next_rect and blit_next_row.
Works up the screen a row at a time, the same way as update_screen, but leaves the buffer as it is. Used for the wiped areas at the end of a frame and by the status area, which draws into the buffer and copies its own parts out.
