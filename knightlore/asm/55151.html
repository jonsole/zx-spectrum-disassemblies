D76F: The byte-aligned case, and the start of its unrolled run
Used by the routine at print_sprite.
For a sprite on a byte boundary each byte of a row is one unit of eight bytes of code: the background byte, cleared under the mask with CPL / OR E / CPL, has the image ORed in and is stored. Five units run on from the POP below, and the JR at sprite_row_jump is set to enter them as many units from the end as the sprite is wide.
