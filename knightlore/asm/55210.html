D7AA: Start a row
Used by the routines at draw_sprite_rows and sprite_next_row.
The row count goes to A', and A takes the first background byte: the shifted units expect the byte they are finishing to be in A already.
sprite_row D7AA EX AF,AF'
D7AB LD A,(BC)
