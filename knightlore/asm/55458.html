D8A2: Mirror the sprite if its object wants it the other way round
Used by the routine at vflip_sprite_data.
Mirroring a row is two things at once: the order of its bytes is reversed, and so are the bits of each byte. The bytes of a row are pushed as mask and image pairs, each byte reversed through the table at reverse_bits_tbl on the way; popped off again they come back last first, and are written over the same row.
