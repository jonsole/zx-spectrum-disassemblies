Knight Lore room editor

Rearrange the castle of Ultimate’s Knight Lore (1984) and play it: open a snapshot of your own copy of the game, edit its rooms, and download the game again with your rooms in it. Nothing leaves this page — there is no server.

Open a snapshot of Knight Lore Drop a 48K .sna or .z80 here, or click to choose one. Take it at the menu, before a game has started.

What you can change

  • Rooms: each room’s colour and floor shape, its scenery and every object in it; add a room by clicking an empty square on the map, or delete one.
  • Templates: what each piece of scenery and each kind of object is made of — an arch, a wall, a block — which every room using it follows.
  • Where each of the 32 collectables starts.

What the original will hold

  • No spare bytes. The game’s room tables are 3,498 bytes and full, so adding something costs bytes that must come from somewhere else — take an object out of another room. Download says how many are free.
  • 36 pieces a room, counting every piece of every template it places. The room busiest in the original already has 36; the editor marks a room over it.
  • Eight objects a group, 32 kinds of object, and a room has to have something in it.

Keeping your work

  • Save keeps it in this browser, and it is offered back when you return. Export writes it to a file you can Import into any copy.
