Nightshade Cell types: the town map's 36 kinds of cell

The 36 cell types, the numbers the town map holds (TOWN). A type is all there is to a cell: it picks the building that stands on it, two definitions for the two ways round (BUILDING_TABLE); the boxes the knight and everything else bump into (BOX_TABLE, tested by HIT_BOXES_U); the ink of its walls (COLOUR_WALL: bright green, cyan, yellow or white by its low two bits); and, from the same two bits, the kind of antibody found in it (SPAWN_FIND), from a stock the type's cells share.

For each type: its walls in the usual view and turned round, as the game draws them behind the knight (DRAW_WALLS); the line on the ground it draws instead when the cell is in front of him or is his own (DRAW_OUTLINE), both ways round; and its boxes, drawn on the cell's ground in the same projection -- the page's drawing of the list, since the game never draws a box. A box is its centre and its half-sizes in half units: a cell runs from 64 to 191 each way, U down to the right and V up to the right. All the pictures but the boxes are the game's own code run in the simulator.
