BBAA: Game variables and the object records
Cleared from here at the first start (START), and from CELL_LOOKED_UP at every new game (NEW_GAME), so only the random number, the font base, the saved stack pointer and the two control bytes survive from one game to the next; the turn counter is outside the cleared part too, but is set again from FRAMES at every new game. The snapshot's bytes here are what the tape's block held: none of them is ever read before it is written.
The 23 object records from KNIGHT on are 16 bytes each, updated in turn every turn by the main loop through UPDATES: +0 the graphic, which picks the update routine (0 an empty record); +1 and +2 U, a word whose high byte is the town's column and low byte the place across the cell; +3 and +4 V the same, the high byte the row; +5 the speed (for a find, the type of cell it came from, SPAWN_FIND); +6 the facing in bits 6 and 7 (bit 6 along U rather than V, bit 7 backwards), and in the low bits a count -- for the knight bits 0-2, his turning; for a monster or a find bits 0-5, the turns before it next changes direction (WANDER_STEP, STEER); +7 the flags -- bit 0 stopped by a wall this turn (MOVE_CLIPPED), bit 1 drawn this turn (DRAW_SPRITE, cleared by the sound routines that play only for things on the screen), bit 5 a monster that makes for the knight when it turns (STEER), bit 6 its sprite mirrored and bit 7 upside down (TURN_SPRITE); +8 and +9 the half-size in U and V, centre to edge; +A and +B this turn's step in U and V, signed; +C and +D the drawing offset, added to where it projects on the screen; +E and +F where it was last drawn.
RANDOM BBAA DEFB $00,$00 The random number, stirred with R and the turn counter after every record's update and at every random choice (NEXT_TURN)
FONT_BASE BBAC DEFB $00,$00 The address of character code 0 for the printer (PRINT_CHAR): each routine that prints sets it first -- but see PRINT_PERCENTAGE
SAVED_SP BBAE DEFB $00,$00 The stack pointer, kept while the drawing and the clearing of the buffers borrow SP (SHOW_PLAY_AREA)
CONTROL BBB0 DEFB $00 Control method: bits 1-2 keyboard, Kempston, cursor, Interface II; bit 3 directional control (MENU)
LAST_CONTROL BBB1 DEFB $1E The control method before the last key on the menu
TURNS BBB2 DEFB $00,$00 The turn counter: FRAMES at every new game (NEW_GAME), one more each turn (NEXT_TURN); its low byte paces the finds (SPAWN_FIND), the monsters (SPAWN_MONSTER) and the creature (SPAWN_CREATURE)
CELL_LOOKED_UP BBB4 DEFB $00,$0F The column and row last looked up on the map (LOOK_UP_CELL)
DRAW_X BBB6 DEFB $00,$3F Where on the screen a thing or a building is drawn: x
DRAW_Y BBB8 DEFB $1E,$80 ...and y
VIEW BBBA DEFB $00 Bit 0: the town is seen turned round, from the other side (Z or SYMBOL SHIFT, TURN_TOWN); the panel's heading says south instead of north (PRINT_HEADING)
COLUMNS_DRAWN BBBB DEFB $1F,$0F A bit for each column of the screen a building has been drawn in this turn (DRAW_WALL_COLUMN)
KEY_LATCH BBBD DEFB $FF Set while a key that acts once a press is held
TOP_SPEED BBBE DEFB $03 The knight's top speed: 10, and 18 while a bonus's faster walk lasts (SPEED_BONUS)
MENU_SHOWN BBBF DEFB $C0 Set once the menu's frame has been drawn (DISPLAY_MENU)
CELL_TYPE BBC0 DEFB $80 The type of the cell being drawn (DRAW_WALLS)
TUNE_PLAYED BBC1 DEFB $3F Set once the menu's tune has played (PLAY_TUNE_ONCE); cleared with the rest at every new game, so it plays again after each
SPRITE_WIDTH BBC2 DEFB $1C The width of the sprite being drawn
SPRITE_ROWS BBC3 DEFB $FF The rows of it that are drawn
DRAW_LIST_AT BBC4 DEFB $FC,$E0 Pointers into the list of things to draw (SORT_AND_DRAW_THINGS)
DRAW_LIST_NEXT BBC6 DEFB $40,$1F
FIRE_DELAY BBC8 DEFB $0B Turns before the knight can throw again (KNIGHT_THROWS)
SCORE BBC9 DEFB $FF The score, BCD, highest first: the top two digits, of which only the lower is printed (ADD_SCORE)
BBCA DEFB $FF The next two, where a villain's 250000 goes
SCORE_LOW BBCB DEFB $C0 The next two, the hundreds and thousands, where everything else scores
SCORE_ZEROS BBCC DEFB $80 Printed as the score's last two digits, and never written: so every score ends 00
LIVES BBCD DEFB $0F Lives in hand besides the one being played: six at a new game, from the code at LIVES_BYTE (NEW_GAME), and one taken as each life starts, the first included; none left to take is game over (NEW_LIFE)
STOCKS BBCE DEFB $07,$FF,$FF,$E0,$C0,$1F How many more finds the cells of each type have, by type, shared by every cell of it: types 2 on get 16-31 at every new game (STOCK_BUILDINGS), types 0 and 1 none; one is taken as a find appears (SPAWN_FIND) and put back if it is left behind (FIND_WANDER)

HITS BBF3 DEFB $FF Hits the knight can still take in this life: 3 at each life and at the cure (HITS_BONUS), one fewer for each monster or creature that touches him (WANDERING_MONSTER); his colour shows them (KNIGHT_COLOURS)

EFFECT_TIME BBF9 DEFB $FF Notes left of the sound effect under way, one a turn (EFFECT_NOTE); in the ending, a count the ending's note rises from (ENDING_BEEP)
EFFECT BBFA DEFB $05 Which sound effect (EFFECT_TABLE): 0 a new cell with a building, 1 the faster walk, 2 the cure, 3 an object taken up
FLASH BBFB DEFB $FE The paper colour of the play area, flashed when a villain dies (VILLAIN_DYING), and back to black at the end of every turn (NEW_GAME)
LAST_FLASH BBFC DEFB $D0 The paper this turn's play area was cleared to: FLASH as the last turn ended, which the knight's and the buildings' colours keep (COLOUR_KNIGHT)
PERCENT BBFD DEFB $7F The percentage of the game done, BCD: the hundreds (PERCENTAGE)
PERCENT_LOW BBFE DEFB $1F ...and the tens and units
ENDING BBFF DEFB $FF Set while the ending plays (GAME_OVER): the main loop then only updates the records
ENDING_COLOUR BC00 DEFB $C1 The colour the ending's pictures are drawn in
CONTROLS BC01 DEFB $FE What the controls say this turn: bits 0-1 turn, 2 walk, 3 throw, 4 down, 5 turn the town round (READ_CONTROLS)
ARRIVING BC02 DEFB $D4 Counts up by two a turn from 40 while the knight appears at a new life, and is $70 once he has (UPDATE_KNIGHT): until then nothing touches him and no find or monster comes (TOUCHING_KNIGHT)
CARRIED BC03 DEFB $FF,$7F,$FF,$F9,$FF The things carried, in the order taken up: 1-4 the four objects, 5-8 the four kinds of antibody, 0 an empty place (PICK_UP_THING); each has its place on the panel, from the bottom up (COLOUR_CARRIED)
