C19C: Which villain is nearest the thing at IX?
Used by the routine at APPEARING_UPDATE.
Nearest by the columns and the rows apart added together (VILLAIN_DISTANCE); the first found wins a tie. Returns 3 for the villain in the first record down to 0 for the last: the kind of the monsters that villain brings (APPEARING_UPDATE). With no villain left, 3.
Input
IX the record
Output
A 3 - the nearest villain's record number
