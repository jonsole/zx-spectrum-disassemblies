C400: A footstep
Used by the routine at WALK_ON.
WALK_ON calls this for every step of the knight's walk; FOOTSTEPS counts them. Walking (speed under 12) every fourth plays four waves, at a half-wave count of 64 and 96 by turns; at the faster speed a bonus gives, every second, so the steps come twice as often. Pentagram's footstep plays two waves, and has no faster half.

IX the knight's legs
FOOTSTEP C400 LD HL,FOOTSTEPS one more step; how fast is he going?
