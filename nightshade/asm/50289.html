C471: One wave
Used by the routines at BUMP_SOUND, FIRE_SOUND and ARRIVE_SOUND.
Speaker bit on for B turns of a 13 T-state loop, then off for as long, with the border black throughout. The same routine as Knight Lore's, Alien 8's and Pentagram's.
Input
B the half-wave count, kept

C479 XOR A speaker off; wait; B back
