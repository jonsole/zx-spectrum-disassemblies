C4DC: Colour the carried things on the panel, flashing an object near its villain
Used by the routine at NEW_GAME.
Every turn. Each carried thing's icon is given its colour from THING_COLOURS, two by two, from the bottom of the panel up to the first empty place: an object in the colour its villain is drawn in once destroyed (DRAW_VILLAINS), the four kinds of antibody magenta, green, cyan and yellow. An object whose villain is less than five cells from the knight in column and row flashes through the antibody colours instead, one a turn: the game's sign that the villain it kills is near.
