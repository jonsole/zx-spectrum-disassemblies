C661: Play one note
Used by the routines at PLAY_TUNE_ONCE and PLAY_TUNE.
A note byte's low six bits index NOTES, with 0 a rest; its top two bits are its length less one, one to four units. The note's three bytes are two loop counts that time each half of a wave and how many waves make one unit, and the wave count rises with the pitch, which keeps every unit close to the same length whatever the note. The speaker is driven directly, off for one half-wave and on for the other, with the border black.
The routine and NOTES are byte for byte Knight Lore's (play_note there), Alien 8's and Pentagram's, at other addresses.

A the note byte
DE the address of the note byte; on exit, the next one
