CB3E: Print four characters as a piece two characters square
Used by the routines at PRINT_COMPASS, DRAW_PLAY_FRAME, PRINT_BORDER, PRINT_BLOCK_ROW, PRINT_BLOCK_COLUMN and PRINT_LIFE_ICON.
Each character is coloured as it is printed (PRINT_CHAR_COLOURED). HL comes back as it was. The frames are made of these (FRAME_PIECES), and so are the compass (PRINT_COMPASS) and the lives (PRINT_LIFE_ICON).
Input
HL the screen address of the top-left
DE the four codes: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right
A' the colour
Output
