CDAF: The text after a game
Each an attribute and then the text, printed in turn by GAME_OVER.
The four lines' places are in GAME_OVER: GAME OVER at the top, the percentage line, then COMPLETED with the percentage after it, and SCORE with the score after it.
END_TEXT CDAF DEFM $47 Attribute
CDB0 DEFM "GAME OVE",$D2 "GAME OVER"
CDBA DEFB $45 Attribute
CDBB DEFM "PERCENTAGE OF GAM",$C5 "PERCENTAGE OF GAME"
CDCD DEFB $45 Attribute
CDCE DEFM "COMPLETED ",$BD "COMPLETED ="
CDD9 DEFB $46 Attribute
CDDA DEFM "SCOR",$C5 "SCORE"
