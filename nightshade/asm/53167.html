CFAF: List the things in a cell
Used by the routine at DRAW_CELLS.
The first half of drawing the things in one cell of the town (the drawing order at DRAW_CELLS calls it for every cell whose things are drawn, and SORT_AND_DRAW_THINGS follows it). Every object record from KNIGHT to the end of the records whose graphic is not 0 and whose column and row -- the high bytes of U and V, +2 and +4 -- are this cell's has its address put on DRAW_LIST, which a zero word then ends.
The cell comes in the drawing order's terms, which are turned round with the town (TURN_CELL), and the records keep the town's own; TURN_CELL is its own inverse, so it turns the cell back. Nothing counts the entries: the list has room for fifteen, and the most measured in the simulator, at 1748 stops here over thirty random cells of play, was four.
Input
HL the cell as the drawing order sees it: L the column, H the row
LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL CFAF LD IY,DRAW_LIST IY at the start of the list, IX at the first record
CFB3 LD IX,KNIGHT
CFB7 CALL TURN_CELL The cell in the town's own terms, into DE: E the column, D the row
CFBA EX DE,HL
LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_0 CFBB LD A,(IX+$00) An empty record is not drawn
CFBE AND A
CFBF JR Z,LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_1
CFC1 LD A,E Is the record in this cell: its U's high byte this column, its V's this row?
CFC2 CP (IX+$02)
CFC5 JR NZ,LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_1
CFC7 LD A,D
CFC8 CP (IX+$04)
CFCB JR NZ,LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_1
CFCD PUSH IX It is: its address onto the list
CFCF POP HL
CFD0 LD (IY+$00),L
CFD3 LD (IY+$01),H
CFD6 INC IY
CFD8 INC IY
LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_1 CFDA LD BC,$0010 The next record, until the end of the records at START
CFDD ADD IX,BC
CFDF PUSH IX
CFE1 POP HL
CFE2 LD BC,START
CFE5 AND A
CFE6 SBC HL,BC
CFE8 JR C,LIST_THINGS_IN_CELL_0
CFEA XOR A A zero word ends the list
CFEB LD (IY+$00),A
CFEE LD (IY+$01),A
CFF1 RET
