D6D6: Send four sparkles out from a villain destroyed
Used by the routine at OBJECT_FLIGHT.
Called by the thrown object's update (OBJECT_FLIGHT) when it strikes its villain. The four records at FINDS become sparkles, graphic 140 (SPARKLE_FLY moves them), at the villain's U and V, each facing a different way so that they fly apart: a speed of 12, half-sizes of 16 and the drawing offset things of that size have. Graphics 140-143 share their sprites with the first frames of the four kinds of find (48, 52, 56 and 60), so the sparkles show the finds' pictures. Whatever the four records held -- things found in buildings and not yet picked up -- is lost. The flags (+7), the step (+A, +B) and where each was drawn (+E, +F) are left as they were. The ending waits for the sparkles to finish (CHECK_QUEST_DONE).
Input
IY the villain's record
VILLAIN_SPARKLES D6D6 LD HL,FINDS The four records at FINDS
D6D9 LD B,$04
VILLAIN_SPARKLES_0 D6DB LD (HL),$8C Graphic 140
D6DD INC HL U and V (+1 to +4), the villain's
D6DE PUSH IY
D6E0 POP DE

D6E2 LD C,$04
VILLAIN_SPARKLES_1 D6E4 LD A,(DE)
D6E5 INC DE
D6E6 LD (HL),A

D6E8 DEC C
D6E9 JR NZ,VILLAIN_SPARKLES_1
D6EB LD (HL),$0C Speed (+5) 12
D6ED INC HL Facing (+6, bits 6-7): 3, 2, 1 and 0 for the four records, so they fly apart
D6EE LD A,B
D6EF DEC A
D6F0 RRCA
D6F1 RRCA

D6F5 INC HL Half-sizes (+8, +9) 16 and 16
D6F6 INC HL
D6F7 LD (HL),$10

D6FA LD (HL),$10
