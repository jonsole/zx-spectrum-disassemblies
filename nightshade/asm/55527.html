D8E7: Put the four villains in random cells at a new game
Used by the routine at NEW_GAME.
The same as PLACE_OBJECTS, with VILLAIN_RECORD and the villains' graphics: 108, 104, 100 and 96 for records 0 to 3 (four pictures each, FACING_PICTURE). Called just before it, and each call advances the turn count and stirs the random number, so the two start from different places in the ROM. A villain can be placed in the knight's first cell; he then dies there as he appears, as often as he comes back.
