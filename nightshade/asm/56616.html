DD28: Decide whether a walker turns: at a wall, when its count runs out, or towards the knight
Used by the routines at MONSTER112_UPDATE and VILLAIN_WANDER.
Used by the villains (VILLAIN_WANDER) and the monsters of graphics 112-127 (MONSTER112_UPDATE). Stopped by a wall, it turns a quarter left or right at random and starts a new count of up to 63 turns. Otherwise the count (the low bits of the facing byte) goes down each turn, and when its low five bits reach 0: with bit 5 of the flags clear it turns at random the same way; with it set it turns towards the knight (KNIGHT_DIRECTION, TURN_TO_KNIGHT_TABLE) and starts a count of 1-16.
Bit 5 is set only for a monster spawned in the first half of a 256-turn cycle (SPAWN_MONSTER); the villains never have it, so they wander and never chase. The counts are set from the random number's high byte and tested on only five bits, so a count of 32-63 runs out after the count less 32.
Input
IX The record
STEER DD28 BIT 0,(IX+$07) Stopped by a wall: turn
DD2C JR NZ,STEER_0
DD2E DEC (IX+$06) Count down; done unless the low five bits are 0
DD31 LD A,(IX+$06)
DD34 AND $1F
DD36 RET NZ
DD37 BIT 5,(IX+$07) A chaser turns towards the knight
DD3B JR NZ,STEER_3
STEER_0 DD3D LD A,(RANDOM) Turn left or right at random
DD40 CP $80
DD42 LD A,(IX+$06)
DD45 JR NC,STEER_2
DD47 ADD A,$40 Right
STEER_1 DD49 LD C,A A new count, the random number's high byte's low six bits ORed with what was left of the old one
DD4A LD A,($BBAB)
DD4D AND $3F
DD4F OR C
DD50 LD (IX+$06),A
DD53 RET
STEER_2 DD54 SUB $40 Left
DD56 JR STEER_1
STEER_3 DD58 CALL KNIGHT_DIRECTION A: the knight's direction, 0-3
DD5B LD C,A
DD5C LD A,(IX+$06) Where it is compared with the facing: 0 ahead, 1 to the left, 2 behind, 3 to the right
DD5F RLCA
DD60 RLCA
DD61 SUB C
DD62 AND $03
DD64 LD BC,TURN_TO_KNIGHT_TABLE Turn by it (TURN_TO_KNIGHT_TABLE)
DD67 JP DISPATCH
