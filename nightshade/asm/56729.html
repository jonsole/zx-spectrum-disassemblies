DD99: Which way is the knight from a record?
Used by the routine at STEER.
The facing that points most nearly at the knight: along V if he is at least as far away in V as in U, along U if further in U, the sign from the difference.
Input
IX The record
Output
A 0 +V, 1 +U, 2 -V, 3 -U: the facing towards him, divided by $40
KNIGHT_DIRECTION DD99 LD HL,(KNIGHT_U) His U less its U; DE its size

DDA8 CALL NZ,NEGATE_HL
DDAB EX DE,HL His V less its V; HL its size
DDAC LD HL,(KNIGHT_V)
DDAF LD C,(IX+$03)
DDB2 LD B,(IX+$04)
DDB5 AND A

DDB8 PUSH HL
DDB9 BIT 7,H

DDBF SBC HL,DE
