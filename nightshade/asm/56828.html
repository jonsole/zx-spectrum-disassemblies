DDFC: A random step from the bits of a byte
Used by the routine at WANDER_STEP.
Two for each 0 among the low B bits of C, negative if the next bit is 0: a step of 0 to twice B either way, those near B likeliest, as the count of zeros among B random bits is.
Input
B The number of bits
C Random bits
Output
