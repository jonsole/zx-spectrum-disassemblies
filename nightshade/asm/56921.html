DE59: Trim a record's step at the town's walls
Used by the routines at MONSTER112_UPDATE, SPARKLE_FLY, ANTIBODY_FLIGHT, OBJECT_FLIGHT, VILLAIN_WANDER, WALK_ON and MOVE_SPLIT.
Clears the wall flag (bit 0 of +7) and jumps to MOVE_TABLE's routine for the facing, which trims the step so that the record stops against a wall instead of in it, and sets the flag if it had to. From offset 4 (MOVE_CLIPPED_AGAIN) the flag is left as it is, for a second move in the same turn. The step is only trimmed here: APPLY_STEP makes the move.
Input
IX The record
MOVE_CLIPPED DE59 RES 0,(IX+$07) No wall met yet
