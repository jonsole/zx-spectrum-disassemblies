5E07: Room sizes
Three entries: the half-size in U, the half-size in V (about the room's centre at 128), and the floor's height, 128 in all three. Bits 3-7 of a room record's third byte pick one, and the builder (BUILD_ROOM) copies it to ROOM_EXTENT.
ROOM_SIZES 5E07 DEFB $40,$40,$80 Size 0
5E0A DEFB $20,$40,$80 Size 1
5E0D DEFB $40,$20,$80 Size 2
