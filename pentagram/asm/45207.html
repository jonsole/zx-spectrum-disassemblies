B097: Put the quest things in this room into the object records
Used by the routine at START.
The quest things -- the four quest items, the five collectables, the eight pieces of the pentagram and the bucket -- are not in the room directory. They live in 18 records of 16 bytes at QUEST_RECORDS, each with its room at +8, and are copied into the object records when he enters a room, after the room is built (START). QUEST_OUT_OF_ROOM copies them back when he leaves.
Each thing in this room goes into the lowest free record of the room's 48, with its flag to be drawn set, the address of its quest record in +10 and +11, and the rest of the record zeroed. Two exceptions: the pieces of the pentagram stay out until all four quest items are done (PENTAGRAM_ON, CONTROL), and anything but a piece is lifted 12 at a time until it overlaps nothing (DO_ANY_OBJS_INTERSECT), so that it stands on whatever it was left on top of -- in the sessions the lift never ran; poked in, a quest item put where a hazard stands came in 12 higher (measured). With no free record left it gives up on the rest.
QUEST_INTO_ROOM B097 LD IY,QUEST_START_BUCKET IY 16 bytes before the live quest records, since the loop adds 16 before it looks; 18 records
B09B LD B,$12
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_0 B09D LD DE,$0010 C = the room he is in
B0A0 LD A,(PLAYER_ROOM)
B0A3 LD C,A
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_1 B0A4 ADD IY,DE The next record; graphic 0 has nothing to place: a thing being carried, or the bucket before the well gives it
B0A6 LD A,(IY+$00)
B0A9 AND A
B0AA JR Z,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_2
B0AC LD A,(IY+$08) In this room?
B0AF CP C
B0B0 JR Z,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_3
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_2 B0B2 DJNZ QUEST_INTO_ROOM_1 No: the next
B0B4 RET
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_3 B0B5 LD HL,ROOM_OBJECTS Find the lowest empty record of the room's 48
B0B8 LD DE,$0020
B0BB PUSH BC
B0BC LD B,$30
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_4 B0BE LD A,(HL)
B0BF AND A
B0C0 JR Z,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_5
B0C2 ADD HL,DE
B0C3 DJNZ QUEST_INTO_ROOM_4
B0C5 POP BC None free: give up
B0C6 RET
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_5 B0C7 EX DE,HL DE = the free record
B0C8 LD A,(IY+$00) A piece of the pentagram (graphics 128 to 135) stays out until all four quest items are done
B0CB AND $F8
B0CD CP $80
B0CF JR NZ,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_6
B0D1 LD A,(PENTAGRAM_ON)
B0D4 AND A
B0D5 JR Z,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_9
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_6 B0D7 SET 4,(IY+$07) To be drawn: set in the quest record itself, so the copy carries it
B0DB PUSH IY The quest record's 16 bytes into the object record
B0DD POP HL
B0DE LD BC,$0010
B0E1 LDIR
B0E3 PUSH IY Its address into +10 and +11, so the thing can find its record again
B0E5 POP HL
B0E6 EX DE,HL
B0E7 LD (HL),E
B0E8 INC HL
B0E9 LD (HL),D
B0EA INC HL
B0EB LD B,$0E +12 to +1F zeroed
B0ED XOR A
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_7 B0EE LD (HL),A
B0EF INC HL
B0F0 DJNZ QUEST_INTO_ROOM_7
B0F2 LD DE,$FFE0 IX = the record: back 32 from its end
B0F5 ADD HL,DE
B0F6 PUSH HL
B0F7 POP IX
B0F9 LD A,(IX+$00) A piece of the pentagram lies where it is
B0FC AND $F8
B0FE CP $80
B100 JR Z,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_9
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_8 B102 CALL DO_ANY_OBJS_INTERSECT Anything else: does it overlap something?
B105 JR NC,QUEST_INTO_ROOM_9
B107 LD A,(IX+$03) Yes: lift it 12 and try again
B10A ADD A,$0C
B10C LD (IX+$03),A
B10F JR QUEST_INTO_ROOM_8
QUEST_INTO_ROOM_9 B111 POP BC The next quest record
B112 DJNZ QUEST_INTO_ROOM_0
B114 RET
