B178: Copy the screen buffer to the screen
Used by the routines at OBJECT_DONE, DISPLAY_MENU and WON.
All 192 rows of 32 bytes from BUFFER. The buffer runs the other way up from the display -- its first row is the bottom line of the screen -- because the game's pixel y counts up from the bottom; so the copy starts at the display's bottom-left byte and works up a line at a time. Used on the first turn in a room (OBJECT_DONE) and for the text screens (DISPLAY_MENU, WON). Knight Lore's copy clears the buffer as it goes; this one leaves it as it is.
