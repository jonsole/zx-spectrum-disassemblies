B531: List the objects to be drawn
Used by the routine at OBJECT_DONE.
Called by the main loop (OBJECT_DONE) once every object has been updated. Writes into DRAW_LIST the number (0-53) of every record in use whose draw flag, bit 4 of +$07, is set, in record order, and an $FF after the last. RENDER_DYNAMIC_OBJECTS wipes the objects' old places from this list and SORT_AND_DRAW draws from it.
