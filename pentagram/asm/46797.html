B6CD: The chain of candidates since the last draw
Record numbers, ended by $FF, of every object IY_GOES_FIRST has made the candidate since DRAW_CANDIDATE last drew something; DRAW_CANDIDATE empties it by putting an $FF in the first byte. Sixteen bytes, where Knight Lore has eight.
