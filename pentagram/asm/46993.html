B791: Shorten dV against the other objects
Used by the routine at ADJ_FOR_OUT_OF_BOUNDS.
ADJ_DU_FOR_OBJ_INTERSECT for V: an object has to overlap in U, after the accepted dU, and in Z to be in the way. Stopping sets bit 1 of +$0C; harm passes the same way, and a pushable obstacle takes on the mover's dV.
Input
IX the moving object
L dV
C dU as accepted
H dZ as accepted
Output
L dV, shortened
