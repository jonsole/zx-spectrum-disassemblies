B963: Shorten dZ at the floor
Used by the routine at ADJ_FOR_OUT_OF_BOUNDS.
The floor is the only limit on Z: there is no ceiling. The floor's height is the third byte of ROOM_EXTENT, 128 in all three room sizes. A move that would take the object's base below it is shortened a unit at a time until it would not, and bit 2 of +$0C records that the move was stopped.
Input
IX the object
H dZ
Output
H dZ, shortened
