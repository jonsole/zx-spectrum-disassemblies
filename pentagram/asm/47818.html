BACA: A spare object record, for drawing on the panel
Never one of the room's objects: SHOW_CARRIED draws the carried things through it, DRAW_LIVES the lives icon and DISPLAY_PANEL another picture on the panel. They set its graphic (+0), its flags (+$07) and its pixel position (+$1A, +$1B), and DRAW_SPRITE (DRAW_OBJECT) writes +$18 and +$19. Zero on the tape.
