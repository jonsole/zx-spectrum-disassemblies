BB64: Flash one of B attributes, and steady the rest
Used by the routine at FLASH_MENU.
Sets bit 7, FLASH, on the A'th of B attribute bytes from HL, counting from 0, and clears it on the others. FLASH_MENU marks the chosen control method on the menu with it. This entry deals with the first byte; the loop is FLASH_NEXT_ONE. HL comes back just past the last byte.
Input
HL the first attribute
B how many
A which one flashes
TOGGLE_SELECTED BB64 AND A not the first
BB65 JR NZ,FLASH_NEXT_ONE_0
This entry point is used by the routine at FLASH_NEXT_ONE.
TOGGLE_SELECTED_0 BB67 SET 7,(HL) flash this one
BB69 JR FLASH_NEXT_ONE_1
