BBF9: The menu: where the lines go (the title's y; keys 1 and 2)
The positions go on from MENU_XY, MENU_COLOURS's last byte, an (x, y) pair per line. The option lines start at x 48, character column 6, and step down 16 pixels, two character rows, from y 143.
