BD31: The pieces of the panel's scroll-work
Ten entries of four bytes, as TRANSFER_SPRITE reads them: the graphic, the flags (bit 6 mirrored, bit 7 upside down), then x and y in pixels, y counting up from the bottom. DISPLAY_PANEL draws some once and repeats others along a line. Each line here is a left and right pair.
