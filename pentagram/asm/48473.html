BD59: Draw the border round the screen
Used by the routines at DISPLAY_MENU and WON.
From BORDER_DATA, into the buffer through the spare record at PANEL_RECORD: the corner sprite drawn four times, turned each way, the top and bottom edges as eight pieces 24 pixels apart and a shorter one to close each, and the sides as six pieces 24 pixels apart. It frames the menu and the end screens (DISPLAY_MENU, WON); a room has none.
PRINT_BORDER BD59 LD IX,PANEL_RECORD the four corners
BD5D LD HL,BORDER_DATA
BD60 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT
BD63 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT
BD66 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT
BD69 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT
BD6C CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE the top edge: eight pieces, 24 pixels apart
BD6F LD DE,$0018
BD72 LD B,$08
BD74 CALL MULTIPLE_PRINT_SPRITE
BD77 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE the bottom edge
BD7A LD B,$08
BD7C CALL MULTIPLE_PRINT_SPRITE
BD7F CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT the short pieces that close the top and bottom
BD82 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE_AND_PRINT
BD85 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE the left edge: six pieces, 24 pixels apart going up
BD88 LD DE,$1800
BD8B LD B,$06
BD8D CALL MULTIPLE_PRINT_SPRITE
BD90 CALL TRANSFER_SPRITE the right edge
BD93 LD B,$06
BD95 JP MULTIPLE_PRINT_SPRITE
