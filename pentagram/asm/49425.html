C111: The update routine for a puff (graphics 64 to 70)
One frame a turn, with a burst of noise (PUFF_SOUND); graphic 71 is the last (END_PUFF).

C114 CALL PUFF_SOUND the noise, and the next frame
C117 INC (IX+$00)
This entry point is used by the routines at START_PUFF and END_PUFF.
