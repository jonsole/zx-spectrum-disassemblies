C291: Make an object deadly both ways
Used by the routines at STILL_DEADLY, SPIKES, DEADLY_BOBBER, DEADLY_PACER_U, DEADLY_PACER_V, SPIDER, ROAMER and SKY_WALKER.
Sets bits 7 and 5 of +$0D: it kills what it moves into and what touches it. As Knight Lore's set_both_deadly_flags.
Input
IX the object
MAKE_DEADLY C291 LD A,(IX+$0D)
C294 OR $A0
C296 LD (IX+$0D),A
C299 RET
