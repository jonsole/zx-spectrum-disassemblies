C302: The quest is complete
Used by the routine at COLLECTABLE.
Jumped to by COLLECTABLE when the fifth collectable reaches its place. Clears the buffer, fills the attributes with bright cyan, prints the six lines of WON_CONGRATULATIONS -- congratulations, the quest done (with a misspelling), and the next adventure named as Mire Mare -- clearing the screen-shown flag (FRAME_DRAWN) first, so that the list also draws the border and shows the screen -- then plays TUNE_WON to its end and goes on to the game-over screen.
Mire Mare was to be the next Sabreman game; it was never released.
