C3FF: The player, as he came into this room
His two records, legs and body, 64 bytes, which RESTART_PLAYER copies over OBJECTS at the start of every life. Each time he goes through a doorway, EXIT_LOW_U and its neighbours copy his records here as he comes into the new room, so a life starts at the doorway he came in by; a new game puts the start of the legs' record back from PLAYER_START, and CHOOSE_START sets the room. What is kept is the exit marker, not a position: after an exit it held the new room with V $FF, so a restart runs the arrival code again (measured).
