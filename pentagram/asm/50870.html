C6B6: Build the room he is in
Used by the routine at START.
Clears the screen buffer, builds the room's object records (BUILD_ROOM), puts the player in the doorway he came in by if he came through one (ADJUST_PLYR_UVZ_FOR_ROOM_SIZE), marks the room seen (MARK_ROOM_SEEN) and sets NEW_ROOM for the main loop (OBJECT_DONE), which redraws the panel and clears it.
ENTER_ROOM C6B6 LD IX,OBJECTS IX = the player's legs, whose room is the one to build
C6BA CALL CLEAR_BUFFER
C6BD CALL BUILD_ROOM
C6C0 CALL ADJUST_PLYR_UVZ_FOR_ROOM_SIZE
C6C3 CALL MARK_ROOM_SEEN
C6C6 LD A,$01 The room is new: NEW_ROOM
C6C8 LD (NEW_ROOM),A
C6CB RET
