C6CC: Mark the player's room as seen
Used by the routine at ENTER_ROOM.
Sets the room's bit in ROOMS_SEEN: byte room/8, bit 7 minus room mod 8, so room 0 is bit 7 of the first byte. The 31 bytes have room for 248 rooms. PERCENTAGE counts them.
