C843: Out through the wall at low U?
Out if the player's far edge -- U plus his half-size -- is below the wall at 128 less the room's half-size. U is then set to 0: not a position but a marker, which ADJUST_PLYR_UVZ_FOR_ROOM_SIZE sees when the next room is built and puts him in the doorway of its wall at high U. The other exits use $FF and 0 the same way.
Input
IY The player's legs
IX The pillar
EXIT_LOW_U C843 POP HL L = the wall
C844 LD A,$80
C846 SUB L
C847 LD L,A
C848 LD A,(IY+$01) Still at or past it: stay
C84B ADD A,(IY+$04)
C84E CP L
C84F RET NC
C850 LD (IY+$01),$00 Arrive at the wall at high U
This entry point is used by the routines at EXIT_HIGH_U, EXIT_HIGH_V and EXIT_LOW_V.
