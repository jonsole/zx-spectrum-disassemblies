C89A: Out through the wall at low V?
Out if the far edge in V is below the wall at 128 less the room's half-size in V. V becomes the marker 0: arrive at the wall at high V.
Input
IY The player's legs
IX The pillar
EXIT_LOW_V C89A POP HL H = the wall
C89B LD A,$80
C89D SUB H
C89E LD H,A
C89F LD A,(IY+$02) Still at or past it: stay
C8A2 ADD A,(IY+$05)
C8A5 CP H
C8A6 RET NC
C8A7 LD (IY+$02),$00 Arrive at the wall at high V
C8AB JR EXIT_SCREEN
