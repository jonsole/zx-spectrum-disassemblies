D071: Set up a walk through the room's 48 object records
Used by the routines at BAN_DROPS, WELL and BUCKET.
For a loop of the form test (IY+n), ADD IY,DE, DJNZ.
Output
IY the first of the room's records, ROOM_OBJECTS
DE 32, the size of a record
B 48, the number of them
ROOM_OBJECT_LOOP D071 LD DE,$0020 DE = 32, IY = ROOM_OBJECTS, B = 48
D074 LD IY,ROOM_OBJECTS
D078 LD B,$30
D07A RET
