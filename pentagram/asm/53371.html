D07B: Set up a walk through the quest records
Used by the routines at CHANGE_GRAPHIC, BUCKET and ALL_FOUR_DONE.
As ROOM_OBJECT_LOOP, for the eighteen quest records at QUEST_RECORDS.
Output
IY the first quest record
DE 16, the size of a record
B 18, the number of them
QUEST_RECORD_LOOP D07B LD DE,$0010 DE = 16, IY = QUEST_RECORDS, B = 18
D07E LD IY,QUEST_RECORDS
D082 LD B,$12
D084 RET
